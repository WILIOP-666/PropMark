## Our Story
Founded as a neighbourhood analysis tool, Microburbs has grown to become Australia's leading property data platform, serving buyers, investors, and agents nationwide with objective data for competitive advantage. Luke Metcalfe stands at the intersection of data science and property innovation, applying advanced AI and machine learning to decode Australian neighbourhoods. Industry leader Eugene Dubossarsky describes him as "one of Australia's greatest data scientists." His entrepreneurial track record includes building two data ventures that were successfully acquired. Today, Microburbs processes over 1 billion data points to provide street-level insights into every suburb in Australia. Our machine learning models analyze historical trends, demographic shifts, and economic indicators to forecast growth and identify opportunities.

## Our Philosophy
The principles that guide everything we build
### Data Ownership
We collect and process our own data. No middlemen, no aggregators. This gives us complete control over quality and accuracy.
### Machine Learning First
Our algorithms analyze patterns across millions of properties to identify growth opportunities before they become obvious to the market.
### Street-Level Precision
We analyze at the microburb level - small clusters of streets that share similar characteristics, not just suburb averages.
### Real-Time Updates
Our data refreshes daily with new listings, sales, and market changes so you always have the latest insights.
### Transparency
We show our methodology and explain how our scores are calculated. No black boxes or hidden formulas.
### Customer Success
Our success is measured by your success. We provide tools and support to help you make better investment decisions.
