# 10 Most Important Numbers to Examine when Buying a House in Australia
![Luke Metcalfe](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/8afa250d100e-66a04ac3bc627c5d1992a6be_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke MetcalfeJanuary 4, 2023
Looking to buy a house in Australia? You're in luck! Microburbs has all the data you need to make an informed decision about the suburb you choose to call home. Here are the top 10 numbers to consider when house hunting:
  1. "The Price: What Can You Afford?" - Don't break the bank! Check out our [interactive map](https://www.microburbs.com.au/Real-Estate/Bottom-suburbs-for-Median-sale-price:-All-houses/Sydney) to see the average house prices in different suburbs.
  2. " [Location, Location, Location](https://www.microburbs.com.au/blog/location-location-location) : How Close is the House to Amenities?" - No one wants to live in the boonies. Use our neighborhood scores tool to find a suburb close to schools, shopping, and public transportation.
  3. "Size Matters: How Much Space Do You Need?" - Don't squeeze your family into a shoebox. Use our property size tool to find a suburb with a good mix of house sizes.
  4. "The Condition of the Property: Is It Move-In Ready or Will It Require Renovations?" - Don't get stuck with a fixer-upper. Use our property condition tool to see if the houses in the suburb are generally in good shape.
  5. "The Age of the Property: Is It a New Build or an Older Home?" - Newer isn't always better. Check out our property age tool to see the mix of old and new houses in the suburb.
  6. "The Layout: Is It Practical and Functionally Designed?" - Don't get stuck with a suburb that's all style and no substance. Our property layout tool lets you see the mix of house styles and sizes in the area.
  7. "The Neighborhood: Is It Safe and Family-Friendly?" - Safety first! Use our neighborhood [safety tool](https://www.microburbs.com.au/Crime-Disadvantage/Bottom-suburbs-for-Safety-score/Sydney) to check crime rates in the suburb.
  8. "The Value of the Property: Is It a Good Investment?" - Make sure you're getting a good deal. Our property value tool shows you the average prices for houses in different suburbs.
  9. "The Future: Will You Want to Sell or Rent the Property in the Future?" - Think about your long-term plans. Will you want to sell or rent the property in the future? Make sure the suburb is a good fit for your future needs.


Buying a house is a big decision, and it's important to do your research and consider all the factors before making a commitment. By examining these 10 important numbers, you can make an informed decision and find a suburb that is perfect for you and your family. Be sure to check out the wealth of data and tools available on Microburbs to help you make the best decision.
