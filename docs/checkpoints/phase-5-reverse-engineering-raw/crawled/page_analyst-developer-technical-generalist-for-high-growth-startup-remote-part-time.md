## Analyst Developer / Technical Generalist for High Growth Startup (Remote, Part Time)
**Microburbs** — Transforming Australian Property Data  
$30–$35/hr | Flexible | 20+ hrs/week
### Do you want to build end-to-end solutions, not just write code?
Microburbs is looking for product-minded coders who love turning ideas into working software - fast. We’re not looking for narrow specialists. We want people who enjoy moving fluidly between backend, frontend, data, and growth - people who get a kick out of making things _work_ and _matter_.
### About Microburbs
Microburbs.com.au is a high-growth Australian proptech company that helps investors and homebuyers make smarter property decisions with data.  
  
We’ve been growing **10% month-on-month for 15 months** , and we’re still just getting started. Tens of thousands of investors use our insights every month.
Our founder **Luke Metcalfe** is a veteran data scientist who has previously built and sold two data companies - both focused on democratising access to complex information.
You’ll work closely with Luke and a small, high-performance team across engineering, AI, data science, marketing and product.
### What You’ll Do
This isn’t a traditional dev role. You’ll wear many hats — sometimes in the same day.  
Depending on your strengths, you might:
  * Build and ship new product features end-to-end using Python, Flask, Webflow and modern frontend tools
  * Automate data flows and build internal tools to make the team faster
  * Work with AI APIs (OpenAI, Claude, etc.) to prototype smart user-facing features
  * Analyse real estate and economic data
  * Design, UX and interfaces
  * Contribute to growth - e.g., by improving UX, conversion funnels, or analytics instrumentation


You’ll have _autonomy to identify problems_ and the trust to solve them your way.
### Who You Are
  * You’ve built things that work - whether side projects, startups, or internal tools
  * You’re excited to learn whatever tech gets the job done
  * You think in systems and can connect technical decisions to user impact
  * You can prioritise, move fast, and clean up as you go
  * You’d rather ship something 90% perfect today than 100% next month
  * Python coding experience


### Nice-to-Haves
  * Flask
  * Pandas, Jupyter, or data analysis
  * JavaScript/HTML (basic frontend or Webflow embedding)
  * SQL / Postgres
  * Linux familiarity (systemctl, cron, logs)
  * AI integration experience (OpenAI API, etc.)


### Why Microburbs
  * **Autonomy:** You own features end to end
  * **Real impact:** Your code directly helps people make multi-hundred-thousand-dollar decisions
  * **Learn fast:** Work directly with an experienced founder and a high-output team
  * **Flexible:** Choose your hours; we care about outcomes, not clock time
  * **Growth:** Generous equity options given to all part time staff - we all win together


### Apply
Send your resume to luke.met@microburbs.com.au
