# Australian Property AI Chatbot: Ask Microburbs

A suburb looks affordable. The rent looks promising. But what is being built nearby, and does the particular address carry risks the suburb average hides? An **Australian property AI chatbot** is useful when it helps you work through those questions with evidence you can inspect.
**Microburbs AI Chat** puts our property research into a conversation. Ask about a suburb, compare places or investigate an address, then ask the follow-up that the answer raises. It can query Microburbs data through connected tools and explain the results in plain English.
It is free to use with a Microburbs account, subject to usage limits. You need to sign in, and free accounts need a verified email. Start with the examples on the [Microburbs AI Chat page](https://www.microburbs.com.au/chat-intro).
## What can you ask AI about a suburb?
Start with a decision you are trying to make. “Tell me about this suburb” gives the assistant a broad job. “Compare these suburbs on rental yield, vacancy and new supply” gives it something specific to investigate.
Microburbs holds 90M+ property records, with daily data updates and coverage across every Australian address and locality. The data surface includes valuations, sale and rent history, comparable sales, demographics, schools, transport, risk overlays, zoning and development. National coverage does not mean every field is available for every property.
The point is to move between those subjects without losing the question you started with. A rental yield might lead you to ask about vacancy. A price estimate might lead you to look at nearby sales. A promising suburb might lead you to inspect a particular street.
## Compare suburbs on the things that matter to you
**Try: “Compare Zetland and Alexandria on yield, vacancy and new supply.”** This example from our AI Chat page brings rental returns, empty rental homes and development activity into the same comparison. It is a research prompt, not a claim that either suburb is the better purchase.
Ask for the figures behind the comparison, their dates and any gaps. Then narrow it: houses or units, the period you care about, and the trade-off you want to understand. The useful answer is one you can trace back to the underlying numbers.
For a broader view of either place, open our [suburb reports](https://www.microburbs.com.au/suburb-reports). Chat helps you pursue a question. A report gives you a structured place to explore the suburb in more detail.
## Check an address beyond the suburb median
**Try: “What is 12 Smith Street worth, and what sold nearby recently?”** This is the valuation example on our AI Chat page. Replace the placeholder with a full address, including suburb and state, so the assistant can identify the property you mean.
The available data includes automated valuations with confidence ranges and comparable sales. Ask to see the estimate alongside the sales evidence, then look at how closely those homes resemble the property you are researching.
A suburb median cannot describe a home's condition, renovation quality or the result of your inspection. An automated valuation is an estimate, not a promised sale price. Use the conversation to organise the evidence and identify what still needs checking.
## Ask about risk and zoning before you shortlist
**Try: “Is this address affected by flood or bushfire? What is the zoning?”** This example focuses the investigation on a specific property. Give the full address and ask which mapped overlays and planning information are available.
Keep missing data separate from a reassuring answer. If a lookup does not return an overlay, ask whether that means the record is unavailable or the returned data explicitly reports no mapped exposure. Those are different findings.
Chat can help you read the information returned. It does not inspect the building, issue a planning approval or establish that a property is risk-free. Use it to identify questions for the relevant authority or professional before committing to a purchase.
## Turn a budget into a suburb search
**Try: “Find Brisbane suburbs under $900k with yields above 4% and falling vacancy.”** These are the example search criteria on our AI Chat page, not reported market results. The request combines affordability, rental yield and a change in rental availability.
Make the criteria explicit. Say whether your price threshold refers to houses or units, and ask which period is being used for falling vacancy. If the search returns no matches or lacks a required measure, that is useful information too.
A suburb shortlist is a starting point for investigating individual homes. When you are ready to move to properties, explore the [Property Finder](https://www.microburbs.com.au/property-finder-intro). Keep the suburb-level evidence alongside the details of each address you consider.
## What this property research assistant does not do
**It does not guarantee a complete or correct answer.** The assistant uses a language model to interpret your request and explain tool results. It also has access to sources beyond the property database. Ask which source supports a figure rather than assuming every sentence came from a Microburbs record.
Data tools can be unavailable, and account allowances can limit their use. Chat may still continue without a fresh property lookup. If the answer does not contain the evidence you need, ask for it and treat the question as unresolved until you can check it.
Daily updates describe the data pipeline, not a promise that every underlying observation is from today. Ask for the date attached to the measure you are using. Forward-looking growth forecasts require a paid subscription, and a forecast is not a guaranteed outcome.
## Use chat here, or connect the data to your own tools
You can use the built-in chat without setting up a developer integration. Sign in to Microburbs and ask your question.
If you are building an app or dashboard, explore [Microburbs API access](https://www.microburbs.com.au/api-access) and the [API documentation](https://www.microburbs.com.au/developers/api-docs). If you want to connect the data to a compatible external assistant, use the [MCP connection guide](https://www.microburbs.com.au/developers/api-docs/mcp). MCP is the connection standard that lets an assistant call external data tools. Those integrations have their own access and billing arrangements.
## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is Microburbs AI Chat free?
Yes, with a Microburbs account and within usage limits. Sign-in is required, and free accounts need email verification. Data access has an allowance, and forward-looking growth forecasts require a paid subscription.
### Can I ask about a particular property?
Yes. Give the full address, suburb and state, then ask about the information you need, such as a valuation, comparable sales or mapped risks. Availability varies by property and data field.
### Where should I start?
Open [AI Chat](https://www.microburbs.com.au/chat-intro), sign in and pick a suburb or address you already know. Ask a specific question, then ask to see the numbers and dates behind the answer.
