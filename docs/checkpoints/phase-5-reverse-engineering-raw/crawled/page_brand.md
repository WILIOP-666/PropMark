## Using it well
  * **Give it room.** Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the house on every side.
  * **Keep it legible.** Do not show the full logo below 120 pixels wide in print or on screen; use the icon instead.
  * **Do not redraw it.** No stretching, rotating, recolouring, outlines, shadows, or the name retyped in another font.
  * **Keep the contrast.** Full colour on light, white on dark, black where one colour is all you get.
