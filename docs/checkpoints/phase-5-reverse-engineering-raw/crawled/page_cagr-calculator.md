# CAGR Calculator
Calculate your property's compound annual growth rate

Formula
CAGR = (End Value / Start Value)^(1/Years) - 1
### Enter your property details
Fill in the purchase price, current value, and years held to calculate your CAGR.
## How to use this calculator
### When to use this
You want to know the annual growth rate of a property you own or are researching. It's great for comparing how different properties or suburbs have performed over time.
### Steps
  1. Enter what the property was worth when purchased (or its value X years ago).
  2. Enter what it's worth now (or its later value).
  3. Enter the number of years between those two values.


### Understanding your results
  * **CAGR** stands for Compound Annual Growth Rate. It smooths out year-to-year ups and downs into a single annual percentage — think of it as the average yearly return.
  * **Comparison bar** shows how your property stacks up against the national average (~7% p.a.).
  * **Projection table** shows where the property could be in 5–25 years if growth continues at this rate.


### Tips
  * Past growth doesn't guarantee future growth — use projections as a guide, not a guarantee.
  * Compare CAGR across different suburbs to find consistent performers over time.
