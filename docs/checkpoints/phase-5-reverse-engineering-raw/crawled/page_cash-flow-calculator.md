## How to use this calculator
### When to use this
You own or are evaluating an investment property and want to see the actual cash in and out each year. This gives you the full picture of holding costs before tax.
### Steps
  1. Enter the property value and expected weekly rent.
  2. Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and choose between Interest Only or Principal & Interest.
  3. Adjust the ongoing expenses — management fees, council rates, insurance, maintenance, water rates, and strata.
  4. Review the results to see your weekly, monthly, and annual cash flow.


### Understanding your results
  * **Positive** (green) means your rental income exceeds all expenses — the property pays for itself.
  * **Neutral** (amber) means you're within $1,000/year of breaking even.
  * **Negative** (red) means you're out of pocket — but you may still benefit from tax deductions and capital growth.


### Tips
  * Aim for at least neutral cash flow. Negative cash flow properties rely on capital growth and tax benefits to make up the shortfall.
  * Factor in vacancy — typically 2–4 weeks per year with no rent coming in.
  * Management fees are tax deductible, as are most other expenses shown here.
