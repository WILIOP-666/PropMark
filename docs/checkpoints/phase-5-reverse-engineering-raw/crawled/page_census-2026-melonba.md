# Melonba: what changed since the 2021 Census
440 homes at the last Census. 4,007 addresses today. +632% against the national rate, and the Census map redrawn around it.
![Luke Metcalfe](https://www.microburbs.com.au/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fluke-headshot-circle.png&w=128&q=75)By Luke Metcalfe, Founder & Chief Data Scientist, 15+ years in property data analytics · September 2026

Melonba did not exist as a suburb most people had heard of in 2021. The Bureau counted 440 homes. Today it holds about 4,000 addresses, and the Census map has been redrawn from 7 areas to 83 to keep up.
![Melonba from space in 2021](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/insights/census-2026-melonba-before.png)![Melonba from space in 2026](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/insights/census-2026-melonba-after.png)
Left, Melonba as the 2021 Census saw it, photographed 2021-01-24. Right, the same ground on 2026-08-31. Both from the European Space Agency's Sentinel-2 satellite.

Melonba is the fastest-moving suburb in the country against the national rate: a place that was a name on a plan at the last Census and is a suburb now.
**What this means if you are buying here.** Any 2021 Census figure you are shown for Melonba is describing a place that has since been carved into 83 areas and has 3,567 more addresses than it did. The suburb average is the wrong tool. ## For developers: the same answer as a request
Everything on this page comes from data built for the [Microburbs API](https://www.microburbs.com.au/report_generator/api/docs). The API already resolves any address and returns its mesh block today. Three Census requests are specified and being built on top of it; they are not live yet, and this page will say so until they are. Access and plans are on the [API access page](https://www.microburbs.com.au/api-access).  
| Request, coming  | What it will answer  |  
| --- | --- |  
| `GET /v1/census/resolve?gnaf_pid=GAVIC720355173`  | Which Census area this address is in, for 2021 and for 2026, and whether it changed.  |  
| `GET /v1/census/lineage?mesh_block_2021=21327400000`  | What an old area became, with the ABS's own population-weighted shares. This one returns 43 rows.  |  
| `GET /v1/census/releases`  | Which Census data exists today and which is still to come, with dates.  |  
[Read the API documentation](https://www.microburbs.com.au/report_generator/api/docs) for what is live today, or [see worked examples of the three Census requests](https://www.microburbs.com.au/insights/census-2026-api) as they will answer.
Addresses from the national address file. 2021 counts from the Bureau of Statistics' 2021 mesh blocks. 2026 areas from the Bureau's ASGS Edition 4 boundaries and correspondence. Listings from our own records since 2022.
