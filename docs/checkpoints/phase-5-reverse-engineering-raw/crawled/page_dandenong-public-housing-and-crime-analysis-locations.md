# 33.9% Public Housing on Stud Road: Crime and Public Housing in Dandenong Analysis

#### Key Insights:
  * **Public Housing and Crime**
    * **Stud Road** and surrounding streets such as Gladstone Road (13.7%) and Cleeland Street show higher concentrations of public housing, while areas closer to Metro Village have lower public housing rates.
    * Dandenong has a mixed reputation in terms of safety. Some locals feel safe during the day but advise caution at night, particularly around the **Dandenong Plaza** and **train station** , where incidents related to youth gangs and drug-related activities are occasionally reported.
    * While crime in Dandenong is above the state average, it is important to note that public housing areas vary greatly. For example, some pockets house elderly residents or families, while others may have a more transient, younger population.
  * **Safety and Surrounding Areas**
    * The northern parts of Dandenong, such as Dandenong North and **Rosewood Downs** , are often viewed as safer and more family-friendly, with a sense of community and fewer reports of crime.
    * Areas closer to **Heatherton Road** or around the **Dandenong Market** can experience petty crime, especially around public transport hubs and parks, though there is increased police presence in these areas.
    * Public housing concentrations, such as those along Stud Road and Heatherton Road, are typically associated with more affordable housing but may have slower growth compared to areas with less public housing.
  * **Crime Statistics & Community Insights**
    * Greater Dandenong, including the pockets with high public housing, recorded approximately **7,000 criminal incidents** in the past year, with property crimes like theft and damage being the most common. This crime rate is above the state average.
    * Community feedback suggests that while Dandenong has its challenges, there is a strong sense of community in certain pockets. Residents in family-oriented areas like Rosewood Downs report feeling safe and connected.
  * **Impact on Property Values and Capital Growth**
    * Pockets with higher concentrations of public housing, such as those along **Stud Road** , present opportunities for affordable entry points into the property market. However, these areas may experience slower property value growth compared to others undergoing gentrification, particularly near the Dandenong CBD.
    * Areas like Metro Village and Rosewood Downs, which have lower concentrations of public housing, are experiencing faster property price increases due to ongoing developments and their proximity to key transport links such as the **Monash Freeway** and **EastLink** .
    * Dandenong is also undergoing significant redevelopment, with new apartments, shops, and public infrastructure planned around the CBD. This is expected to drive future capital growth, although public housing concentrations may moderate this growth in certain pockets.


#### Public Housing Location and Its Impacts:
  * **Stud Road and Heatherton Road** feature high concentrations of public housing. Some locals feel uneasy in these areas after dark due to groups loitering, while others find these pockets peaceful and community-focused during the day.
  * **Dandenong North** , with lower public housing and larger family homes, is often seen as a more desirable residential area with better long-term growth potential.
  * The affordability of public housing pockets may attract first-home buyers and investors looking for lower-cost properties, although the surrounding amenities and crime rates should be carefully considered.


#### Is Dandenong Safe to Live In?
Dandenong is a suburb of contrasts. While areas with high concentrations of public housing face safety challenges, many parts of the suburb are safe, vibrant, and family-friendly. Ongoing developments and the government's investment in infrastructure are slowly transforming the suburb, offering growth potential for those willing to invest in its future.
**Tips for Buyers:**
  * Consider focusing on areas away from high-concentration public housing for better safety and growth potential, such as **Dandenong North** and **Metro Village** .
  * Areas like **Stud Road** and **Heatherton Road** may offer lower entry points for buyers, but these pockets could experience slower capital growth due to their public housing concentration.
  * As the suburb continues to gentrify and infrastructure improves, Dandenong's future looks promising, particularly in areas with ongoing developments near the CBD.


Overall, Dandenong is evolving, and for those willing to explore beyond its reputation, there are significant opportunities for capital growth, particularly in areas with a lower concentration of public housing and closer to new developments.
