## How to use this estimator
### When to use this
You own an investment property and want to estimate your tax depreciation deductions — one of the biggest (and most overlooked) tax benefits of property investing.
### Steps
  1. Enter the property purchase price.
  2. Enter the estimated construction cost (usually 40–60% of the purchase price for houses — the default is pre-filled for you).
  3. Enter the year the property was built.
  4. Select your marginal tax rate (the tax bracket you're in — check your last tax return if unsure).


### Understanding your results
  * **Division 43** covers building structure depreciation — the walls, roof, and foundation. It's claimed at 2.5% per year over 40 years.
  * **Division 40** covers fixtures and fittings like appliances, carpets, and blinds. These depreciate faster, giving you bigger deductions in the early years.
  * **Total deduction** is what you can claim on your tax return to reduce your taxable income.
  * **Tax saving** is the actual cash benefit — your total deduction multiplied by your tax rate.


### Tips
  * Always get a professional quantity surveyor's report — it typically pays for itself in the first year through higher deductions.
  * Properties built after 1985 qualify for Division 43 deductions. Post-2017 rules mean you can't claim Division 40 on second-hand fixtures in established properties.
