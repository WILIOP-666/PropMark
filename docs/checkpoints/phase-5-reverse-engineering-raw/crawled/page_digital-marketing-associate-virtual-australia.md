# Digital Marketing Associate - Virtual (Australia)
![Luke Metcalfe](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/8afa250d100e-66a04ac3bc627c5d1992a6be_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke MetcalfeMarch 28, 2023
Microburbs (<https://www.microburbs.com.au/>) is Australia’s most comprehensive resource for real estate and liveability. Our goal is to understand what makes every single suburb in Australia unique and what patterns drive them together. The purpose is to give people real information instead of stereotypes to help them find their dream suburbs.
We are a small data startup focused on providing insights on suburbs to help people make informed real estate investments.We want to grow our brand and reputation. This is a role where you will have high autonomy to pursue your own ideas.
Ultimately, we believe in hiring people, not the role. If you are someone who is curious, dedicated and looking for responsibility, apply. We are all passionate high achievers with a result-oriented and cooperative culture.
About you:
  * Ability to craft compelling content
  * Curious, analytical mindset
  * Excellent written English
  * Diligent and rigorous
  * Comfortable with working in a fast-paced and dynamic environment, with the ability to adapt to changing priorities and deadlines.
  * Prepared to work extensively with GPT-4 to augment your capabilities.


Role may include things like:
  * Evolving a marketing strategy and associated tactics in a fast-moving environment
  * Creating content for social media
  * Press releases and interaction with online news sites
  * Some content editing
  * Reviewing analytics and optimising campaigns
  * Creating and executing email marketing campaigns
  * Syndicating and promoting our podcast.
  * Managing partnerships and collaborations with other businesses or organizations


Not essential but familiarity with any of these will help:
  * Geographic based studies (For example: real estate, land economics etc…)
  * Australian public data (data.gov.au)
  * The Australian urban landscape
  * Search engine optimisation (SEO)
  * Social media marketing
  * Real estate
  * Content management
  * Blogging
  * Internet Marketing


This is our current website:
Hours are highly flexible but minimum 20 hours per week. This is a remote position where you can work anywhere.
To apply, please [contact us](https://www.microburbs.com.au/contact-us).
