## How to use this calculator
### When to use this
You already own a property and want to know if you have enough equity to buy another one.
### Steps
  1. Enter your property's current market value (check recent sales nearby for a rough idea).
  2. Enter your remaining loan balance (check your bank app or last mortgage statement).


### Understanding your results
  * **Usable equity** is the amount you can actually access for another purchase. Banks typically let you borrow up to 80% of your property's value — usable equity is that 80% minus what you still owe.
  * **What could you buy?** estimates the maximum purchase price of your next property, using your usable equity as a 20% deposit.


### Tips
  * Get a formal valuation if you think your property has grown significantly — online estimates can be off by 10–15%.
  * Usable equity can be accessed via a loan increase or line of credit — talk to your broker about the best option.
