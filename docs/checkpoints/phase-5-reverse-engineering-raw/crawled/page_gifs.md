# Market Cohesion B-Roll
Animated GIFs for the video. White background versions of each B-roll visualisation.
![Map vs Street](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/market-cohesion-gifs/map-vs-street.gif)
### Map vs Street
Australia with scattered houses showing wildly different returns, vs a single street where returns cluster tight.
National vs Street comparison[View live version](https://www.microburbs.com.au/market-cohesion/b-roll/map-vs-street)
![Map Zoom](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/market-cohesion-gifs/map-zoom.gif)
### Map Zoom
Concentric rings narrowing from national to street level. Properties cluster tighter as the ring shrinks.
Scene 2: The Question[View live version](https://www.microburbs.com.au/market-cohesion/b-roll/map-zoom)
![Cohort Circles](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/market-cohesion-gifs/cohort-circles.gif)
### Cohort Circles
Dashed circles group properties in the same cohort. Arrows show whether each beat or missed the median.
Scenes 4-8: Each market level[View live version](https://www.microburbs.com.au/market-cohesion/b-roll/cohort-circles)
![Deviation Funnel](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/market-cohesion-gifs/deviation-funnel.gif)
### Deviation Funnel
Mean absolute deviation shrinks from 10pp/yr (national) to under 1pp/yr (street). Dots narrow into a tight band.
Scene 9: The Big Picture[View live version](https://www.microburbs.com.au/market-cohesion/b-roll/deviation-funnel)
![80% Myth Buster](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/market-cohesion-gifs/myth-buster.gif)
### 80% Myth Buster
The classic "80% heavy lifting" claim gets crossed out. The real number: suburbs do 61%. Streets do 89%.
Scene 10: Key Takeaways[View live version](https://www.microburbs.com.au/market-cohesion/b-roll/myth-buster)
![Dual Axis Chart](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/market-cohesion-gifs/dual-axis.gif)
### Dual Axis Chart
Same-direction percentage rises on the left axis while deviation falls on the right. Two lines, one story.
Scene 9: The Big Picture[View live version](https://www.microburbs.com.au/market-cohesion/b-roll/dual-axis)
![Summary Waterfall](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/market-cohesion-gifs/waterfall.gif)
### Summary Waterfall
Uncertainty removed builds up level by level: 24% (national) to 89% (street), with verdict badges.
Scene 10: Summary[View live version](https://www.microburbs.com.au/market-cohesion/b-roll/waterfall)
