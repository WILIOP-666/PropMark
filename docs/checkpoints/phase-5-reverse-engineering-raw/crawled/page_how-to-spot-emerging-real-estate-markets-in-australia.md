# How to Spot Emerging Real Estate Markets in Australia
![Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/8afa250d100e-66a04ac3bc627c5d1992a6be_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.January 2, 2023
![How to Spot Emerging Real Estate Markets in Australia](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/950c51aed78f-5eaecfb21_free-photo-of-house-renovation-project-with-tools-and-scaffolding.jpeg)
As a real estate investor, it’s always a good idea to stay on the lookout for emerging markets that have the potential for strong returns. But how do you identify these markets, and what factors should you consider when evaluating them? Here are a few tips to help you spot emerging real estate markets in Australia:
  1. Look for areas with strong economic growth. One of the key indicators of a strong real estate market is economic growth. Look for areas with robust job markets, expanding businesses, and rising incomes, as these can be signs that a particular market is on the rise. At Microburbs, we provide data on employment, businesses, and income levels for suburbs across Australia, so you can easily assess the economic health of a particular area.
  2. Consider the demographics of the area. Demographics can also play a role in the strength of a real estate market. For example, areas with a growing population of young professionals or families with children may be more attractive to potential buyers and renters, which can drive up demand for housing. At Microburbs, we provide detailed demographic data, including information on age, family composition, and education levels, for suburbs across Australia.
  3. Look for areas with a shortage of housing. When supply is low and demand is high, it can be a good sign for real estate investors. Look for areas where there is a shortage of housing, as this can be an indication that there is strong demand for properties in the area. Microburbs provides data on the supply and demand dynamics of the real estate market in suburbs across Australia, so you can easily assess the balance between supply and demand.
  4. Keep an eye on infrastructure projects. Infrastructure projects, such as new transportation links or the development of commercial or retail centers, can be a sign that an area is on the verge of growth. These projects can increase the attractiveness of an area and drive up demand for housing.
  5. Monitor market trends and changes. Finally, it’s a good idea to stay up to date on market trends and changes. This can help you identify areas that are showing signs of growth and potential for strong returns. At Microburbs, we provide data on on property prices, rental rates, and sales volumes, for suburbs across Australia.


By keeping an eye on these factors, you can identify emerging real estate markets in Australia and make informed decisions about your investments. While there are no guarantees in the real estate market, taking the time to research and evaluate potential markets can help you maximize your returns and build a successful portfolio. And with the wealth of data and insights available at Microburbs, you have the tools you need to make informed decisions and navigate the Australian real estate market with confidence.
