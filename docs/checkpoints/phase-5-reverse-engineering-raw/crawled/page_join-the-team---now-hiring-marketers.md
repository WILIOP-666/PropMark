# Join the Team - Now Hiring Marketers
![Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/0e4214a9d2f6-65aef1729bdc56b006a5d1b3_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.August 12, 2024
![Join the Team - Now Hiring Marketers](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/04a34f5bcd67-66b998e2c8367d3f598cff6c_hiring2.jpg)
**Join the Microburbs Team of Exceptional Developers!** At Microburbs, we’re experiencing rapid growth of 30% per month and are on the hunt for top-tier junior marketers to join our innovative team. Microburbs ([www.microburbs.com.au](https://www.microburbs.com.au)) is Australia’s most comprehensive resource for real estate and liveability. Our goal is to understand what makes every single suburb in Australia unique and what patterns drive them together. The purpose is to give people real information instead of stereotypes to help them find their dream suburbs.Ultimately, we believe in hiring people, not the role. If you are someone who is curious, dedicated and looking for responsibility, apply. We are all passionate high achievers with a result-oriented and cooperative culture.Why Work With Us?
  * Share Options: Provided even to part-timers, so everyone benefits from our success.
  * Impactful Work: Help thousands of Australians daily find their perfect property.
  * Minimal Bureaucracy: Dive right in with essential, streamlined processes.
  * High Performance Culture: Supportive, meritocratic, egoless culture of continuous momentum.
  * AI Integration: Work closely with AI to quickly advance your career.
  * Flexible Work Environment: Most of our team is fully remote, with the option to work from our Sydney office if you prefer.


**About you:**
  * Curious, analytical mindset
  * Excellent written English
  * Diligent and rigorous
  * Comfortable with working in a fast-paced and dynamic environment, with the ability to adapt to changing priorities and deadlines.
  * Prepared to work extensively with GPT-4 to augment your capabilities.


**Renumeration:**
$30 - $35 / hour
**Depending on your skills and interests, role may include things like:**
  * Customer interviews and customer development
  * Evolving a marketing strategy and associated tactics in a fast-moving environment
  * Creating content for social media
  * Press releases and interaction with online news sites
  * Reviewing analytics and optimising campaigns
  * Creation and optimisation of funnels
  * Acquiring traffic through Google and Meta
  * Creating and executing email marketing campaigns
  * Syndicating and promoting our podcast.
  * Managing partnerships and collaborations with other businesses or organisations


**Not essential but familiarity with any of these will help:**
  * Geographic based studies (For example: real estate, land economics etc…)
  * Australian public data (data.gov.au)
  * Paid advertising
  * The Australian urban landscape
  * Search engine optimisation (SEO)
  * Social media marketing
  * Real estate
  * Content management
  * Blogging
  * Internet Marketing


Hours are highly flexible but minimum 16 hours per week. This is a remote position where you can work anywhere, though you're welcome to join us in our Sydney office if you're local.Support for any kind of work visa, with assistance in your residency/citizenship journey for retained staff.Join us at Microburbs and make a real difference in the real estate industry!
