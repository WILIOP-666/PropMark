## How to use this calculator
#### When to use this
You're buying a property with less than a 20% deposit and want to know how much Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) will cost you.
#### Steps
  1. Enter the **property value** — the purchase price or valuation.
  2. Enter your **deposit amount** — the calculator works out your Loan-to-Value Ratio (LVR) automatically.
  3. Choose whether to **capitalise the LMI** into your loan (add it to the loan balance) or pay it upfront at settlement.


#### Understanding your results
  * **LMI** — a one-off insurance premium that protects the _bank_ (not you) if you default on the loan. It's required when your deposit is less than 20% of the property value.
  * **LVR (Loan-to-Value Ratio)** — how much you're borrowing as a percentage of the property value. The higher the LVR, the more LMI costs.
  * **Capitalised LMI** — if you add LMI to your loan, you don't pay it upfront, but you'll pay interest on it for the life of the loan, which increases the total cost.


#### Tips
  * Even an extra $10,000 in your deposit can drop you into a lower LMI bracket and save you thousands — try increasing your deposit to see the difference.
  * At 80% LVR or below (i.e. a 20% deposit or more), you pay **zero LMI**. That's the magic number to aim for if you can.
