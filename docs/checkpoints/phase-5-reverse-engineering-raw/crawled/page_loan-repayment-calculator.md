## How to use this calculator
#### When to use this
You want to know what your mortgage repayments will be — whether you're about to buy, or just exploring what different loan sizes would cost you each month.
#### Steps
  1. Enter your **loan amount** — this is the property price minus your deposit (e.g. $750k property with $150k deposit = $600k loan).
  2. Enter the **interest rate** — check your bank's current rate, or use the default to explore.
  3. Choose your **loan term** — 30 years is the most common in Australia.
  4. Select **Principal & Interest** or **Interest Only** to compare both side by side.


#### Understanding your results
  * **Principal & Interest (P&I)** — each payment chips away at the loan balance plus interest. The loan gets smaller over time until it's fully paid off.
  * **Interest Only (IO)** — you only pay the interest each month, so the loan balance stays the same. Monthly payments are lower, but you're not paying off the debt.
  * **Total interest** — this is the extra money you pay the bank over the life of the loan, on top of the amount you borrowed.


#### Tips
  * Switching to **fortnightly payments** saves thousands over the loan life — you end up making the equivalent of 13 monthly payments per year instead of 12.
  * Interest-only loans are common for property investors in the first few years, but the loan doesn't shrink — you'll need to switch to P&I eventually.
