## Abstract
Buyers who search only the big portals miss some homes that are genuinely for sale.
Some agents sell homes quietly on their own websites and nowhere else. We call these homes for sale off market.
These homes are real. Some sell without ever reaching a big portal.
In NSW, homes first seen on agency sites between April and July 2026 sold by late September 2026 with no ad on the big portals in our records.
In 41 suburbs of Melbourne's inner south, September 2026, we flagged about 5 extra per 100 on the big portals. A flag is not a home confirmed for sale.
A random check of NSW agency pages in September 2026 found most flagged pages were old, for homes already sold or leased.
No price effect is measured for the homes in this paper.
## Key findings
  * **Quiet sales are real.** NSW homes first seen on an agency site between April and July 2026 sold by late September 2026 with no ad on the big portals in our records. One was [6 Bond Place](https://www.microburbs.com.au/property-reports/GANSW716870121), [Oxley Park](https://www.microburbs.com.au/suburb-reports/Oxley%20Park) (Sydney), sold 18 May 2026 for $750,000. For a buyer, each was a home the portals never showed.
  * **About 5 extra homes for every 100 on the big portals were flagged on agency sites.** 41 suburbs of Melbourne's inner south, September 2026. Each page is checked before a home is shown as for sale.
  * **Agencies leave old pages up.** So every page is opened before a home is shown as for sale. For a buyer, that means a list of homes genuinely for sale.
  * **Real listings sell.** Flagged homes that later appeared on a big portal sold 4 in 10 within five months, April to September 2026. Some sold without ever appearing on a portal. For a buyer, those are homes the portals never showed.
  * **At LJ Hooker, its own site is a head start, not hidden stock.** 8 in 10 homes on its own site, April to July 2026, were later seen on the big portals. For a buyer, that means seeing some homes early.
  * **The rule for marking a home.** A home is marked only when its own agency page shows it for sale.
  * **Our first rule marked too many homes.** In Brighton, Melbourne, only 8 of 26 held up on 22 September 2026. We replaced it.
  * **Fifteen hand-picked examples were live.** Each was for sale on its agency's own site on 22 September 2026. They were picked as examples, not drawn at random. They show what the finder will mark, not how often.
  * **Buyers are looking for these homes.** Australians search "off market properties" about 1,900 times a month (Google, 2026). Marking only live homes means those searchers see real options.
  * **No measured effect on price, rent or growth.** For the homes in this paper, the benefit is more choice. We did not measure a price effect.


Buyers and buyer's agents on our calls often doubt that off market homes are real.
What they said, strongest first (6 quotes, 2026)
  1. "You and I know that if it's on a website, it's not a legitimate off market." A buyer's agent, customer call, April 2026.
  2. "Off market, forget it. Lists everything as off market. So don't worry, I don't check that." An investor, on another tool, customer call, August 2026.
  3. "I just wonder if that would mean that they're genuinely off market or not. We can't tell." A buyer's agent, customer call, September 2026.
  4. "I don't think you can digitally procure them. That's kind of the whole point of the thing, off market." A buyer's agent, customer call, July 2026.
  5. "Some of it's just going to be pre market stuff. But it's still helpful because then you can show your client you're not missing opportunities." A buyer's agent, customer call, April 2026.
  6. "Clients don't care if it's not on [the big portals] and you can show them a link." A buyer's agent, customer call, April 2026.


## How we checked it
Every figure here is proven, checked or only counted. Trust them in that order.
Proven: a recorded sale, with no portal ad in our records.
Checked: a page opened and read on a stated day, at random or by hand.
Counted: a flag not yet opened. Read it as a ceiling, not a count of live homes.
How we find these homes is our own.

### Real listings sell

### Melbourne's inner south: about 5 extra homes per 100, flagged
In 41 suburbs of Melbourne's inner south, September 2026, we flagged about 5 extra homes on agency sites for every 100 on the big portals.
That is a count of flags. Each one was not opened and confirmed for sale.
Any that are live are extra to the portals, not instead of them. Keep using the portals as well.
Our NSW random check suggests many will prove to be old pages.

## Answering the critics
### Aren't most off market pages just old listings?
Some are. Agencies often leave Sold and Leased pages up.
That is why a home is marked only when its own agency page shows it for sale.
### If it is a genuine listing, why would it not sell?
Real ones do. Flagged homes that later reached a big portal sold 4 in 10 within five months.
The quiet sales sold too: in NSW, first seen April to July 2026, with no portal ad.
### Is 5 extra per 100 enough to matter?
For a buyer with a tight brief, one extra match can be the home.
But that count is flags, not confirmed homes. Judge it by the homes you can check.
### Won't these homes reach the portals next week anyway?
Many will. You may see them before buyers who watch only the portals.
We have not measured whether seeing a home early changes the price paid.
### One region is not Australia
Agreed. The 5 extra per 100 covers Melbourne's inner south only, September 2026.
In NSW, our random check found most flagged pages were old. So this paper leads with sales we can prove.
## Limitations
  * The 5 extra per 100 covers one region, September 2026. It is not a national figure.
  * An earlier, broader check across 100 suburbs in August 2026 found about 2 to 3 extra per 100 homes for sale on the big portals.
  * We do not yet publish a national count of homes confirmed for sale.
  * Quiet sales are confirmed for NSW only, sold April to September 2026.
  * "No portal ad" means none in our records. Our view of the big portals is not complete, so some may have been advertised.
  * Recorded sales arrive weeks after the contract, so the newest quiet sales are undercounted.
  * Homes sell fast. Each example shows its status on 22 September 2026 only.
  * "Not on the big portals" means when we checked. Some homes reach them later.
  * No measured effect on price, rent or growth for the homes in this paper.
