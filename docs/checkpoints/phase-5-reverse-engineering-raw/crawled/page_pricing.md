## Pricing FAQ
Common questions about our plans and billing
Can I change plans at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, you'll receive credit towards your next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). All payments are securely processed through Stripe.
Is there a long-term commitment?
No, all our plans are commitment-free. You can cancel your subscription at any time and continue to use the service until the end of your billing period.
What happens when I reach my report limit?
You'll receive a notification when you're approaching your limit. You can either wait for your limit to reset next month, upgrade to a higher plan, or purchase additional reports as a one-time add-on.
Do unused reports roll over?
No, unused reports do not roll over to the next month. We recommend choosing a plan that matches your typical usage patterns.
What's included in the free plan?
The free plan includes limited access to Suburb Finder and Property Finder, and email support. It's a great way to explore what Microburbs has to offer.
Do you offer refunds?
No. Subscription fees and one-off purchases are non-refundable. If the plan isn't right for you, cancel anytime from your account — you'll keep access until the end of the current billing period and won't be charged again.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes! When you choose quarterly billing, you save approximately 20% compared to monthly billing. This is automatically applied when you select the quarterly option.
