## How to use this calculator
#### When to use this
You're deciding whether to buy a property now or keep renting and invest your deposit elsewhere. This calculator compares the financial outcome of both options over time.
#### Steps
  1. Fill in the **Buying** side — property price, your deposit, interest rate, loan term, annual property costs (rates, insurance, maintenance), and expected capital growth.
  2. Fill in the **Renting** side — your current weekly rent, expected annual rent increases, and what return you'd get investing the deposit instead of buying.
  3. Choose a **time horizon** — how many years you want to compare over.


#### Understanding your results
  * **The verdict** — tells you which option leaves you financially better off after the chosen time period, and by how much.
  * **Total spent** — the total cash out of your pocket over the period (mortgage + deposit + costs for buying, or rent for renting).
  * **Total wealth** — what you own at the end (property equity for buying, or investment portfolio for renting).
  * **Net position** — wealth minus spending. This is the bottom line — a positive number means you came out ahead overall.


#### Tips
  * Try different time horizons — buying usually wins over longer periods (15+ years), while renting can win in the short term.
  * The **investment return rate** matters a lot for the renting side. A higher return makes renting more competitive, so be realistic about what you'd actually earn.
