## How to use this calculator
### When to use this
You're evaluating an investment property and want to know the return you'll get from rent. It helps you compare properties side by side.
### Steps
  1. Enter the property value (or purchase price).
  2. Enter the weekly rent.
  3. Optionally, enter annual expenses (management fees, insurance, maintenance, council rates) to see your net yield.


### Understanding your results
  * **Gross yield** is your annual rent divided by the property value, before any expenses. It's a quick way to compare properties.
  * **Net yield** is the same calculation but after subtracting your annual expenses — a more realistic picture of your return.
  * **Yield gauge** shows yield quality at a glance: under 3% is poor, 3–4% is fair, 4–5% is good, and 5%+ is excellent.
  * **Income breakdown** shows what you actually receive weekly, monthly, and annually.


### Tips
  * High yield doesn't always mean a good investment — check capital growth too. The best investments balance both.
  * Aim for at least 4% gross yield for cash-flow positive properties.
