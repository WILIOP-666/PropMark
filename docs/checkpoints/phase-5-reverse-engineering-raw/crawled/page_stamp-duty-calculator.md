## How to use this calculator
#### When to use this
You're buying a property and want to know how much stamp duty you'll pay. Stamp duty is a government tax charged on property purchases, and it varies by state.
#### Steps
  1. Choose your **state or territory** from the dropdown — rates differ significantly between states.
  2. Enter the **property purchase price** (the price you're paying, not the valuation).
  3. Select whether you're an **owner occupier or investor**.
  4. Check the **first home buyer** box if this is your first property purchase to live in.
  5. Check **foreign purchaser** if you're not an Australian citizen or permanent resident.


#### Understanding your results
  * **Total Stamp Duty** — this is the amount you pay to the state government on top of the property price, due at settlement.
  * **Effective rate** — the stamp duty as a percentage of the purchase price, so you can quickly compare across different prices.
  * **Rate table** — stamp duty works in progressive brackets, just like income tax. You don't pay the top rate on the full amount — only on the portion above each threshold.


#### Tips
  * First home buyers can save thousands in stamp duty — always check the FHB box to see if you qualify for a concession or full exemption.
  * Stamp duty is a one-off cost paid at settlement, so make sure you budget for it on top of your deposit.
