# Suburb Comparison Tool
Compare up to 3 suburbs side-by-side on yield, growth, cash flow, and 5-year projections. Market data auto-fills so you can make faster, data-driven investment decisions.
Sign up freeLog in
Free plan. No credit card required.
## Live market data, side by side
Type a suburb name and median price, rent, vacancy, and growth auto-fill. Compare houses or units across 2 or 3 suburbs.
![Suburb comparison input cards showing Parramatta and Penrith with live market data, property type toggles, and editable fields](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/tools/suburb-comparison-inputs.png)
## See how suburbs stack up
Yield, cash flow, 5-year growth projections, and capital gains — all calculated automatically.
![Comparison summary table showing Parramatta vs Penrith with metrics like median price, yield, cash flow, and 5-year projections](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/tools/suburb-comparison-table.png)
## Instant verdict
See which suburb wins on yield, growth, cash flow, and overall return at a glance.
![Verdict section showing Penrith wins on yield while Parramatta wins on growth, cash flow, and overall return](https://www.microburbs.com.au/images/tools/suburb-comparison-verdict.png)
## How it works
1
### Type a suburb name
Start typing and select from the dropdown. Median price, rent, vacancy rate, and growth forecast auto-fill from live data.
2
### Adjust your assumptions
Tweak council rates, insurance, and strata to match the area. Override any auto-filled value if you have better local knowledge.
3
### Compare and decide
Review the comparison table and verdict. See which suburb wins on yield, growth, cash flow, and 5-year total return.
