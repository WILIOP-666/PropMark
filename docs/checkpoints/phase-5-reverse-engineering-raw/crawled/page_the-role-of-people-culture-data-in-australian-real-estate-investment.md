# The Role of People & Culture Data in Australian Real Estate Investment
![Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/8afa250d100e-66a04ac3bc627c5d1992a6be_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.January 1, 2023
![The Role of People & Culture Data in Australian Real Estate Investment](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/65fbdf6e8cef-670cb41de9e241b80c651a9f_pexels-photo-1438072.jpeg)
When it comes to investing in real estate, it’s important to thoroughly research and understand the local market. One key aspect to consider is people and culture, which can give you a sense of the diverse and unique characteristics of a community.
At [Microburbs](https://www.microburbs.com.au/), we offer a wealth of data on people and culture to help property investors make informed decisions. In this blog post, we’ll explore some of the ways that this data can be useful for understanding the characteristics of a suburb and how they may impact the value of your investment.
**Understanding Satisfaction and Happiness**
One key aspect of our data on people and culture is satisfaction and happiness. This can be useful for understanding the overall well-being and contentment of a community, which can be important to potential buyers or renters. For example, our data includes information on the percentage of residents who are satisfied with their suburb, as well as the percentage who report being happy most of the time.
**Childcare**
Another important aspect of our data on people and culture is the childcare situation in a particular suburb. This can be useful for families who are considering moving to a new area and want to understand the availability and quality of childcare options. Our data includes information on the percentage of children who are in childcare, the quality and availability of childcare.
**Assessing Work and Leisure**
In addition to satisfaction and happiness, our data on people and culture includes information on work and leisure. This can be useful for understanding the lifestyle and priorities of a community. For example, our data includes information on the percentage of residents who are employed, as well as the types of jobs that they hold. This can help you understand the local economy and the opportunities available for employment. Our data also includes information on the percentage of residents who engage in leisure activities, such as exercise and cultural events, which can give you a sense of the community’s interests and priorities.
**Understanding Family Structure and Demographics**
Our data on people and culture also includes information on family structure and demographics. This can be useful for understanding the makeup of a community and how it may be changing over time. For example, our data includes information on the percentage of [families with children](https://www.microburbs.com.au/People-Lifestyle/Top-suburbs-for-Family-households/Sydney), as well as the percentage of [single-parent households](https://www.microburbs.com.au/People-Lifestyle/Top-suburbs-for-Single-parents/Sydney). This can give you a sense of the types of families that make up a community and how this may impact the demand for different types of housing.
**Understanding Migration and Ethnicity**
Another aspect of our data on people and culture is migration and ethnicity. This can provide insight into the diversity of a suburb and how it may be changing over time. For example, our data includes information on net internal migration, the percentage of residents who have arrived in the past 5-25 years, and the percentage of Australian citizens. This can give you a sense of the demographic makeup of a suburb and how it may be evolving.
**Assessing Religious Affiliations and Education**
Our data on people and culture also includes information on religious affiliations and [education](https://www.microburbs.com.au/People-Lifestyle/Bottom-suburbs-for-Education-score/Sydney). This can be useful for understanding the values and priorities of a community, as well as the resources and opportunities available for families. For example, our data includes information on the percentage of residents who are affiliated with different religions, as well as the percentage of students who are meeting minimum literacy and numeracy standards.
**Conclusion**
Overall, our data on people and culture provides a wealth of information on the characteristics of a suburb and how they may impact the value of your real estate investment. By using this data, you can gain a deeper understanding of a community and make more informed decisions about where to invest your money. To learn more about the data we offer at Microburbs, visit our website and explore our suburb profiles.
