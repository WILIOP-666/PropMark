## Buying a Property
Essential calculators for anyone purchasing a home or investment property
### Stamp Duty Calculator
Calculate stamp duty for any state. First home buyer concessions and foreign surcharges included.
[Calculate Stamp Duty](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/stamp-duty-calculator)
### Loan Repayment Calculator
Monthly, fortnightly, and weekly repayments. Compare P&I vs interest-only side by side.
[Calculate Repayments](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/loan-repayment-calculator)
### Borrowing Power Calculator
How much the bank will lend you based on income, expenses, and debts. Uses the +3% serviceability buffer.
[Check Borrowing Power](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/affordability-calculator)
### LMI Calculator
Estimate Lenders Mortgage Insurance if your deposit is under 20%. See how much extra deposit could save you.
[Estimate LMI](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/lmi-calculator)
### Serviceability Calculator
How banks really assess your borrowing capacity. Models HECS debt, credit cards, existing loans, and rental income haircuts.
[Check Serviceability](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/serviceability-calculator)
## Making Decisions
Compare options and model scenarios to make better property decisions
### Rent vs Buy Calculator
Compare renting vs buying over time. See total costs, wealth at the end, and which option leaves you better off.
[Compare Rent vs Buy](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/rent-vs-buy-calculator)
### CAGR Calculator
Calculate the compound annual growth rate of any property. Compare against the national average and project future values.
[Calculate CAGR](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/cagr-calculator)
### Rental Yield Calculator
Gross and net rental yield on any property. Weekly, monthly, and annual income breakdown with a yield quality gauge.
[Calculate Yield](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/rental-yield-calculator)
### Renovation ROI Calculator
Estimate the return on your renovation spend. Compare renovating vs investing the cash elsewhere.
[Calculate Reno ROI](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/renovation-roi-calculator)
## Already Own Property
Tools for existing property owners looking to grow or optimise their portfolio
### Equity Calculator
How much usable equity you have and what you could afford for your next property purchase.
[Calculate Equity](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/equity-calculator)
### Depreciation Estimator
Estimate tax depreciation deductions on your investment property. Division 43 & 40 breakdown with tax savings.
[Estimate Depreciation](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/depreciation-estimator)
### Cash Flow Calculator
Model annual cash flow on an investment property. Income vs all expenses — rent, mortgage, management, insurance, rates.
[Calculate Cash Flow](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/cash-flow-calculator)
### Negative Gearing Calculator
See the after-tax cost of a negatively geared property. Includes depreciation, tax refund, and true annual cost.
[Calculate Neg. Gearing](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/negative-gearing-calculator)
### Capital Gains Tax Calculator
Estimate CGT when selling an investment property. 50% discount, cost base additions, entity type options.
[Calculate CGT](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/cgt-calculator)
### Mortgage Offset Calculator
See how your offset account balance reduces total interest and shortens your loan life.
[Calculate Offset Savings](https://www.microburbs.com.au/tool/mortgage-offset-calculator)
