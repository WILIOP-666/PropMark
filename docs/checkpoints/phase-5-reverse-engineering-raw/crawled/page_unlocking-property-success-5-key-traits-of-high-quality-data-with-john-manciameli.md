### Luke Metcalfe
## About This Episode
Join Luke Metcalfe, chief data scientist and founder of Microburbs, in an enlightening conversation with John Manciameli, one of Australia's top real estate course runners and a seasoned expert in mortgage broking and finance. In this video, we dive deep into the world of property investment, exploring the critical differences between good and bad data and how this knowledge can significantly impact your investment decisions. John also shares a compelling success story where, thanks to precise data from Microburbs, his client secured a property seems to have appreciated significantly in value from day one!
