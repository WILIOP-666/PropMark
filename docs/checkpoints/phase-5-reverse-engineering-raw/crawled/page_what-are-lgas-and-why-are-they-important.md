# What are LGAs and Why are They Important?
![Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/8afa250d100e-66a04ac3bc627c5d1992a6be_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.January 15, 2023
![What are LGAs and Why are They Important?](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/aa4397d5a62e-670cb178bc6a2c73a56fb7ef_pexels-photo-4864108.jpeg)
Local government areas (LGAs) are administrative divisions of a state or territory in Australia that are responsible for providing local government services to their communities. These services can include things like roads, parks and gardens, public facilities, waste management, and community services.
LGAs are typically governed by a council, which is made up of elected representatives who are responsible for setting policies and making decisions on behalf of the community. These councils are accountable to the community and are required to conduct their affairs in an open and transparent manner.
There are over 700 LGAs in Australia, each with its own unique character and amenities. Some LGAs may be predominantly urban, with a high density of housing and businesses, while others may be more rural, with a lower population density.
Local government areas (LGAs) are an important part of the Australian political system, as they are responsible for providing a range of essential services to their communities. These services can have a significant impact on the quality of life of residents, and it is important for LGAs to be well-run and accountable to their communities.
Some of the services that LGAs provide include:
  * Roads: LGAs are responsible for maintaining and repairing local roads, as well as planning and building new roads as needed. This can include everything from pothole repairs to major construction projects.
  * Parks and gardens: LGAs are responsible for maintaining public parks and gardens, including maintaining grass, plants, and trees, as well as providing amenities like playgrounds and picnic areas.
  * Public facilities: LGAs are responsible for maintaining and managing public facilities like libraries, community centers, and sports facilities. These facilities provide important amenities for the community and help to bring people together.
  * Waste management: LGAs are responsible for collecting and disposing of waste in a safe and environmentally responsible manner. This includes everything from garbage collection to recycling programs.
  * Community services: LGAs are responsible for providing a range of community services, including things like social support, youth programs, and seniors services. These services help to ensure that all members of the community have access to the support they need.


Overall, LGAs are an important part of the Australian political system and play a crucial role in providing essential services to their communities. They are responsible for maintaining and improving the quality of life for their residents, and it is important for them to be well-run and accountable to their communities.
