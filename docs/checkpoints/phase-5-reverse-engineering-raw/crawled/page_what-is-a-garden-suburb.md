# What is a Garden Suburb?
![Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/8afa250d100e-66a04ac3bc627c5d1992a6be_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.January 16, 2023
![What is a Garden Suburb?](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/630a72729bce-9de308ef09_free-photo-of-excited-crowd-cheers-on-runners-in-sydney-marathon.jpeg)
Garden suburbs, also known as garden cities, are a type of residential community that combines the best of urban and rural living. They are characterized by their green spaces, parks, and gardens, which provide residents with plenty of opportunities to enjoy the outdoors and connect with nature.
Garden suburbs were first developed in the late 19th and early 20th centuries as a response to the negative impacts of industrialization and urbanization. These communities were designed to provide a healthier, more natural living environment for families, with the goal of improving public health and overall well-being.
One of the key features of garden suburbs is their emphasis on green space. These communities are designed to incorporate a high ratio of green space, with gardens, parks, and other open areas interspersed throughout the community. This not only provides residents with plenty of opportunities to enjoy the outdoors, but it also helps to reduce air pollution and improve overall environmental quality.
In addition to their green spaces, garden suburbs are also known for their layout and design. These communities are typically designed with a focus on pedestrian and bicycle traffic, with wide sidewalks, bike lanes, and other amenities that encourage active transportation. This helps to reduce traffic congestion and improve overall mobility within the community.
Garden suburbs are highly desirable communities, with property values often reflecting their desirable location and amenities. These communities are often sought after by families, professionals, and retirees alike, with their green spaces and active, healthy lifestyle attracting a wide range of residents.
Overall, garden suburbs are a unique type of residential community that combines the best of urban and rural living. With their green spaces, parks, and gardens, these communities offer a healthy, natural living environment that is highly sought after by potential home buyers. If you’re considering a move to a garden suburb, you can expect to enjoy a variety of benefits, including:
  * Improved air quality: The green spaces and parks that are a hallmark of garden suburbs help to improve air quality, providing residents with a healthier living environment.
  * Improved public health: The active, healthy lifestyle that is promoted in garden suburbs can lead to improved public health outcomes, with residents enjoying better physical and mental health as a result.
  * Strong sense of community: The layout and design of garden suburbs often encourage social interaction and a strong sense of community. This can be especially beneficial for families with children, as it provides a safe and supportive environment in which kids can grow and thrive.
  * Higher property values: Due to their desirable location and amenities, garden suburbs often have higher property values compared to other types of residential communities. This can be a great investment for homeowners, with the potential for strong returns over time.


Overall, garden suburbs are a unique and highly desirable type of residential community that offer a range of benefits to residents. If you’re considering a move to a garden suburb, you can expect to enjoy a healthy, natural living environment with a strong sense of community and higher property values.
