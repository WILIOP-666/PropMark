# What Smart Property Investors Expect of Real Estate Data Part 1: Transparency

### Part 1: Transparency in Real Estate Data: Why Clarity and Accuracy Matter
**Why Clarity and Accuracy Matter**
In today’s data-driven world, having access to clear and accurate real estate data can make or break an investment decision. At Microburbs, our customers consistently emphasise the need for transparency in the data we provide. Here are the main reasons why this is crucial:
  * **Informed Decisions:** Accurate data allows for informed decision-making. Whether buying, selling, or investing, precise information is crucial for evaluating risks and opportunities.
  * **Trust and Confidence:** Clear and accurate data build trust and confidence. Customers need to trust the information they are using to make significant financial decisions.
  * **Mitigating Risks:** Transparent data can help mitigate risks by highlighting potential issues such as zoning laws, market volatility, and neighbourhood trends.
  * **Efficiency in Analysis:** Clear data enables efficient analysis. With comprehensive and transparent data, clients can quickly understand market trends and make faster decisions, giving them a competitive edge.
  * **Market Comparisons:** Clarity in data helps in making market comparisons. Customers can benchmark properties against others in the same area, ensuring they are getting the best value.

![](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/d187e8a6d207-670768c11453535c25a8e90c_6707685e64d23e1f9c09ecd9_pexels-kampus-8815909.jpeg)
**Maintaining Data Integrity at Microburbs**
At Microburbs, we go above and beyond to ensure our data is not only accurate but also clearly sourced. Microburbs ensures data integrity by using robust algorithms and verified sources. Our commitment to transparency makes us a reliable partner in their real estate journey. These are some of the ways we maintain data integrity at Microburbs:
  * **AI Algorithms and Verified Sources:** We collect our data independently from various verified sources, namely 90 million+ real estate listings, census data, public financial data, and demographic data, and combine it with AI-powered algorithms to create a robust dataset that reflects real-time market conditions.
  * **Data-First Approach:** As an intensively data-oriented business, we prioritise data accuracy and clarity above all else. Our founder is a data scientist, which underscores our focus on data integrity and precision.
  * **High Commitment to Transparency:** Our dedication to transparency is evident in every aspect of our operations. Our data is clearly sourced and you can talk directly with our founder for further clarification.
