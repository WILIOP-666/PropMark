## The Problem
You know the suburbs. You know the streets. But when a client asks “why should I buy here?”, you need data to back your recommendation. Building that report from scratch takes hours. And when it arrives with someone else's logo, it builds their brand, not yours.
1
### Hours spent building reports from scratch
You pull data from CoreLogic, copy suburb stats into PowerPoint, format it, add your logo, and send it to clients. Every single time. For every suburb.
2
### Generic reports that look like everyone else
If you send a client a report with another company's branding, you are marketing that company. Not yours. Your competitors send the same reports from the same platforms.
3
### No way to capture leads from your own data
You share suburb insights on your website, but there is no branded search tool that captures enquiries while delivering value.
## The Solution: White Labelled Reports in Seconds
With Microburbs white labelling, your logo goes on the report. Your brand is what clients see. The data, design, and 120+ suburb metrics are handled for you.

## Seven Reasons Buyers Agents White Label
1
Professional credibility with clients
A branded report tells clients you have real data behind your recommendation. It carries more weight than a free suburb profile from realestate.com.au.
2
Competitive differentiation
Your reports look different from every other agent using the same generic platforms. Your brand. Your data presentation. Not a commodity.
3
Time savings over building reports manually
Click, download, send. No PowerPoint. No copying suburb stats into a template. A branded report in under 10 seconds.
4
Report design that impresses
Professional layout, clean charts, consistent branding. The design quality of the report reflects directly on your business.
5
Lead capture from your own website
Embed a branded suburb search on your site. Every visitor who searches a suburb becomes a lead in your pipeline.
6
Distribution through your network
Franchise groups and multi-agent teams can push branded suburb data to tens of thousands of clients from a single subscription.
7
A stepping stone to deeper integration
