# Why People Diversify Their Real Estate Portfolio with Different Property Types
![Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/8afa250d100e-66a04ac3bc627c5d1992a6be_Annotation-202020-04-08-20145202.jpg)
Luke Metcalfe, Data Scientist and Founder, Microburbs.December 31, 2022
![Why People Diversify Their Real Estate Portfolio with Different Property Types](https://www.microburbs.com.au/cdn/blog-media/a93a06dc9e02-670cae27065093d7fd80341b_pexels-photo-2999012.jpeg)
When it comes to investing in real estate, one key decision that many investors face is choosing the type of property to invest in. While some investors may choose to focus on a specific type of property, others may opt to diversify their portfolio by investing in a variety of different types of properties.
In this article, we’ll explore some of the reasons why someone might want to invest in different types of properties.
**Diversification**
One key reason to invest in different types of properties is to diversify your portfolio. By investing in a range of different property types, you can spread your risk and potentially reduce the impact of any negative market conditions on your investment.
For example, investing in both residential and commercial properties can provide a diverse range of income streams, as well as potentially offering different levels of risk and return. Similarly, investing in both established and new properties can offer diversification in terms of age and condition.
**Flexibility**
Another reason to invest in different types of properties is to have more flexibility in terms of your investment strategy. For example, investing in both long-term rental properties and short-term holiday rentals can allow you to take advantage of different market conditions and demand.
Similarly, investing in both urban and rural properties can provide the opportunity to capitalize on different types of demand and rental markets.
**Location**
Investing in different types of properties can also allow you to take advantage of different location-specific opportunities. For example, investing in properties in both major cities and regional areas can allow you to tap into different types of demand and rental markets.
Similarly, investing in different types of properties within a specific location can allow you to take advantage of different types of demand and opportunities within that location. For example, investing in both houses and apartments in the same suburb could allow you to tap into different types of demand and rental markets.
**Demographic Considerations**
Investing in different types of properties can also allow you to cater to different types of tenants or buyers. For example, investing in both family-friendly properties and properties that cater to singles or young professionals can allow you to tap into different types of demand.
Similarly, investing in both low-maintenance properties and properties that offer more features and amenities can allow you to cater to different types of tenants or buyers.
In conclusion, investing in different types of properties can offer a range of benefits, including diversification, flexibility, and the opportunity to take advantage of location-specific and demographic-specific opportunities. By considering these factors and doing thorough research, investors can make informed decisions about the types of properties that are the best fit for their investment goals.
