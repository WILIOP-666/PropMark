## The microburb: what makes this pocket different
Port Macquarie is a suburb that combines natural beauty with a strong community feel. While it's renowned for its high accessibility to beaches and parks, offering a serene lifestyle with little crime, its distance from major cities presents connectivity challenges. Notably, the area has a diverse age demographic and faces some economic constraints.
  * High access to parks and green spaces, enhancing quality of life
  * Proximity to Oxley Beach - ideal for those who enjoy coastal living
  * A variety of nearby schools, though performance varies
  * Predominantly reliant on car commuting; limited public transport options
  * Extremely low median weekly household income, paired with a high mortgage-to-income ratio
  * Lies within a flood-prone area, potentially posing risks
