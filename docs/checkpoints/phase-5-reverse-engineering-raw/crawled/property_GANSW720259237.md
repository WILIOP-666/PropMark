## The research behind this page
Every claim here traces to a published Microburbs study. The headline findings:
  * **[The street is the submarket](https://www.microburbs.com.au/research/street-forecasting-blog).** Two streets in the same postcode can diverge 15 to 20 percentage points a year. Our top 10% of street picks beat the market by about 3% a year.
  * **[A 12 year forecasting track record](https://www.microburbs.com.au/research).** Top suburb picks beat their own capital city by about 7.4% a year, with an 82% hit rate.
  * **[Owner-occupier suburbs win](https://www.microburbs.com.au/research/investor-concentration-blog).** Owner-occupier suburbs grew about 221 percentage points more than renter suburbs over the 31 years to 2026.
  * **[The family home beats the investment property](https://www.microburbs.com.au/research).** Across 63,408 paired resales the family home out-returned the levered investor by about 4.9 percentage points a year under the new tax rules.
  * **[Views are real money, when they are real](https://www.microburbs.com.au/research).** A wide water view adds up to 13% against similar no-view homes. A glimpse adds almost nothing.
  * **[Density drags growth](https://www.microburbs.com.au/research).** Hyper-dense pockets underperformed by about 2.6 percentage points a year for eight straight years after 2017.
