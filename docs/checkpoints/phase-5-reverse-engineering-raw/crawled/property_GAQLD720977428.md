## What is behind this price
Read against Microburbs research into how real features move price.
Location and land do the heavy lifting in our valuation model. Bedrooms, bathrooms and garaging together explain only about a tenth of price, and bedroom count barely moves it. Against that, here is what this house actually carries.
  * **The outlook.** Our national view study found a real water view can sell a home for up to 13% more than a similar home without one, a moderate outlook about 7%, and a mere glimpse close to nothing. Tagged here: ocean views, elevated position.
  * **The land.** In our valuation model the size and shape of the block is the single largest driver of price, ahead of anything inside the home. Tagged here: large block.
  * **The position.** Where a home sits does more for its price than how it is built, and these are the parts of the address a renovation cannot change. Tagged here: waterfront.
  * **The pool.** A genuine drawcard in the warmer states, though it reads as a lifestyle feature more than a reliable money-maker. Tagged here: swimming pool.
  * **The aspect.** A northerly orientation, and the natural light it brings, carries a modest and repeatable premium. Tagged here: north facing.
