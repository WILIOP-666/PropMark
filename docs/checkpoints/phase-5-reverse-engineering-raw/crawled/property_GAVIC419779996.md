  * **The land.** In our valuation model the size and shape of the block is the single largest driver of price, ahead of anything inside the home. Tagged here: large backyard, large block.
  * **The position.** Where a home sits does more for its price than how it is built, and these are the parts of the address a renovation cannot change. Tagged here: close to public transport, walking distance to schools, nearby shopping centers, close to park.
  * **The presentation.** Updates and finish lift price inside the roughly one tenth that the building itself contributes. Tagged here: natural light.
