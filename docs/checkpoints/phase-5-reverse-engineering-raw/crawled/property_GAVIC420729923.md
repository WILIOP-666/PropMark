## What is behind this price
Read against Microburbs research into how real features move price.
Location and land do the heavy lifting in our valuation model. Bedrooms, bathrooms and garaging together explain only about a tenth of price, and bedroom count barely moves it. Against that, here is what this house actually carries.
  * **The position.** Where a home sits does more for its price than how it is built, and these are the parts of the address a renovation cannot change. Tagged here: walking distance to schools, nearby shopping centers, close to park.
  * **The presentation.** Updates and finish lift price inside the roughly one tenth that the building itself contributes. Tagged here: stone bench tops, timber flooring, high ceiling, modern appliances.
