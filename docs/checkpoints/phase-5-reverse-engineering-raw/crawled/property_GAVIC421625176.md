## What is behind this price
Read against Microburbs research into how real features move price.
Location and land do the heavy lifting in our valuation model. Bedrooms, bathrooms and garaging together explain only about a tenth of price, and bedroom count barely moves it. Against that, here is what this house actually carries.
  * **The outlook.** Our national view study found a real water view can sell a home for up to 13% more than a similar home without one, a moderate outlook about 7%, and a mere glimpse close to nothing. Tagged here: elevated position, city views.
  * **The land.** In our valuation model the size and shape of the block is the single largest driver of price, ahead of anything inside the home. Tagged here: large backyard.
  * **The position.** Where a home sits does more for its price than how it is built, and these are the parts of the address a renovation cannot change. Tagged here: close to public transport, walking distance to schools, nearby shopping centers.
  * **The aspect.** A northerly orientation, and the natural light it brings, carries a modest and repeatable premium. Tagged here: north facing.
  * **The presentation.** Updates and finish lift price inside the roughly one tenth that the building itself contributes. Tagged here: timber flooring.
