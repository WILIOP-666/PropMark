The median house price in Adelaide, SA is currently $1,010,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Adelaide has a 12-month growth rate of 2.5% for houses and 17.5% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $645,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $900,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,340,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,610,000 in Adelaide.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Adelaide is $650 per week. The median house rent has increased 4.0% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Adelaide is $605,000, which has increased 17.5% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $460,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $670,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $1,020,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $1,520,000 in Adelaide. The median rent for units and apartments in Adelaide is $625 per week and has increased 4.2% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Adelaide
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Adelaide SA 5000.
Is Adelaide family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
5.2% of Adelaide's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
3,328 family households lived in Adelaide; 17.3% were couple families and 11.8% were one-parent families.
18.8% of local families included children under 15. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Adelaide?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Adelaide had 18,202 residents at the 2021 Census.
40.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
20.2% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Adelaide?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 4.3% of occupied private dwellings in Adelaide.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 26.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 66.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Adelaide?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
18.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
15.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
66.3% rented their home. That is about twice the Greater Adelaide rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Adelaide?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Adelaide had 1.8 people.
One-person households made up 45.3% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
Two-person households represented 39.6%, while 4.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Adelaide?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
12.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
26.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
11.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 31.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Adelaide?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
37.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Adelaide rate.
One-car households formed 48.5% of the total.
13.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Adelaide, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
24.2% were at the same address five years earlier, while 75.8% reported a different address.
51.8% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Greater Adelaide rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Adelaide?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
49.1% of residents used only English at home.
42.4% used a language other than English at home.
17.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 4.2% spoke Cantonese and 1.5% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Adelaide residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
51.8% of Adelaide's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 48.2% was born in Australia.
15.7% of residents were born in China.
3.7% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 52.7%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Adelaide?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 23.8% of ancestry responses in Adelaide.
Australian ancestry made up 14.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 25.5% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Adelaide?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
36.6% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
Managers accounted for 13.0% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.7%.
Health care and social assistance employed 14.0% of workers, and education and training employed 8.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Adelaide?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 59.1%, compared with 61.7% across Greater Adelaide.
30.1% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 20.2% worked part time.
Unemployment was 10.5%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Adelaide.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Adelaide show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
66.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
5.5% reported asthma, 4.3% arthritis and 2.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Adelaide?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
39.2% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 20.0% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 11.7%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Adelaide?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,365 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,733.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $400 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Adelaide compare with Greater Adelaide?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 12.7% in Adelaide, compared with 9.8% across Greater Adelaide.
Outright ownership was 18.2% locally and 32.1% across Greater Adelaide. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Renting accounted for 66.3% in Adelaide, against 29.5% across Greater Adelaide.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Adelaide
### Is Adelaide expensive?
The median house price is $1,010,000, while units and apartments sit at $605,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Adelaide good for families?
The Census counted 3,328 local families, and 5.2% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Adelaide?
4-bedroom houses have a median around $1,610,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
Medians describe recorded market activity across the suburb; they are historical context, not a valuation or forecast for an individual home.
