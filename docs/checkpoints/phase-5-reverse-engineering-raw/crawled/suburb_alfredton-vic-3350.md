Is Alfredton family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
23.0% of Alfredton's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of Vic. average.
3,211 family households lived in Alfredton; 47.0% were couple families and 18.5% were one-parent families.
45.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Alfredton?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Alfredton had 11,822 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.1% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of Vic. average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Alfredton?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 90.4% of occupied private dwellings in Alfredton.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 3.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 5.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Alfredton?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

38.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
30.3% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Rest of Vic. average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Alfredton?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Alfredton?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Alfredton?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

64.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
