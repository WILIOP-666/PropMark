The median house price in Armadale (WA), WA is currently $710,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Armadale (WA) has a 12-month growth rate of 19.3% for houses and 23.3% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $375,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $578,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $700,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $780,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $821,000 in Armadale (WA).
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Armadale (WA) is $620 per week. The median house rent has increased 7.8% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Armadale (WA) is $635,000, which has increased 23.3% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $532,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $650,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $683,000 in Armadale (WA). The median rent for units and apartments in Armadale (WA) is $575 per week and has increased 4.5% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Armadale

Is Armadale family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.4% of Armadale's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
3,275 family households lived in Armadale; 30.3% were couple families and 30.7% were one-parent families.
38.1% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Armadale?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Armadale had 13,415 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.7% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Armadale?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 67.5% of occupied private dwellings in Armadale.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 30.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Armadale?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
25.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
32.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
42.7% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Armadale?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Armadale had 2.2 people.
One-person households made up 37.5% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Two-person households represented 32.8%, while 16.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Armadale?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
3.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
66.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
4.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Armadale?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
11.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Perth rate.
One-car households formed 46.1% of the total.
42.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Armadale, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
57.9% were at the same address five years earlier, while 42.1% reported a different address.
27.8% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Armadale?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
78.1% of residents used only English at home.
12.4% used a language other than English at home.
0.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Armadale residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
27.8% of Armadale's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 72.2% was born in Australia.
0.3% of residents were born in China.
1.7% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 81.5%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Armadale?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

How active is the workforce in Armadale?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Armadale show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
47.1% reported no selected long-term health condition.
14.3% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
11.2% reported asthma, 11.8% arthritis and 8.0% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Armadale?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
11.3% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 2.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 46.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Armadale?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
