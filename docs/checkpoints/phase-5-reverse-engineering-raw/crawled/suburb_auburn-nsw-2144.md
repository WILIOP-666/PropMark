Is Auburn family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.5% of Auburn's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
8,583 family households lived in Auburn; 50.1% were couple families and 14.2% were one-parent families.
39.8% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Auburn?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Auburn had 39,333 residents at the 2021 Census.
36.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
19.4% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Auburn?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 42.0% of occupied private dwellings in Auburn.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 47.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Auburn?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
22.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
24.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
52.6% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Auburn?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Auburn had 3.3 people.
One-person households made up 16.6% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 22.3%, while 41.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Auburn?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
17.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
34.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
14.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Auburn?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
18.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 42.6% of the total.
39.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Auburn, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
50.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 49.2% reported a different address.
64.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Auburn?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
12.2% of residents used only English at home.
78.7% used a language other than English at home.
14.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 6.1% spoke Cantonese and 1.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Auburn residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
64.3% of Auburn's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 35.7% was born in Australia.
14.4% of residents were born in China.
5.4% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 56.1%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Auburn?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
16.4% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 6.5% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 9.7%.
Health care and social assistance employed 16.5% of workers, and education and training employed 4.7%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Auburn?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

17.6% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 16.0% worked part time.
Unemployment was 11.4%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Auburn show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
72.9% reported no selected long-term health condition.
3.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
3.3% reported asthma, 3.9% arthritis and 4.7% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Auburn?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
29.0% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 14.5% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 17.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 14.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Auburn?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,000.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Auburn (NSW)
### Is Auburn (NSW) expensive?
The median house price is $1,580,000, while units and apartments sit at $590,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Auburn (NSW) good for families?
The Census counted 8,583 local families, and 17.5% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Auburn (NSW)?
