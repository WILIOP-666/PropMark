Is Aveley family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Aveley had 13,998 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Aveley?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 0.3% of occupied private dwellings.

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
