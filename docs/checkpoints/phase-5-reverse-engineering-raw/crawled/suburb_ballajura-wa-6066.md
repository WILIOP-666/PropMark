Is Ballajura family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

5,109 family households lived in Ballajura; 48.4% were couple families and 18.8% were one-parent families.
37.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Ballajura?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Ballajura had 18,459 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Ballajura?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 94.3% of occupied private dwellings in Ballajura.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 5.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Ballajura?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Ballajura?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Ballajura?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

3.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Ballajura?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

69.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Ballajura, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
