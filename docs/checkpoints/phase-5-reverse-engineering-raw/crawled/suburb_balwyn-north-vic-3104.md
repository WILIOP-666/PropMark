Is Balwyn North family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.8% of Balwyn North's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
5,931 family households lived in Balwyn North; 57.3% were couple families and 14.0% were one-parent families.
38.0% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Balwyn North?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Balwyn North had 21,302 residents at the 2021 Census.
19.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.2% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Balwyn North?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 83.0% of occupied private dwellings in Balwyn North.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 13.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 3.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Balwyn North?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

33.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Balwyn North?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

3.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Balwyn North?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

65.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
