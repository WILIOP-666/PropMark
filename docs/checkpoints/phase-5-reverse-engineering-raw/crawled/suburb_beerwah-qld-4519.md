## Questions people ask about Beerwah
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Beerwah QLD 4519.
Is Beerwah family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
19.5% of Beerwah's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
2,160 family households lived in Beerwah; 40.6% were couple families and 18.0% were one-parent families.
38.1% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Beerwah?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Beerwah had 7,734 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.3% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Beerwah?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 85.8% of occupied private dwellings in Beerwah.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 8.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Beerwah?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
40.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
27.2% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Beerwah?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Beerwah had 2.7 people.
One-person households made up 20.0% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 36.9%, while 26.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Beerwah?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
10.3% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
65.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
1.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Beerwah?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
One-car households formed 30.3% of the total.
67.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Beerwah, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

15.2% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

4.4% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Beerwah residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Beerwah?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
