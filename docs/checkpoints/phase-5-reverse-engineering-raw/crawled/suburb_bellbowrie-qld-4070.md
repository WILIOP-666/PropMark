Is Bellbowrie family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

39.2% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.

What is the age profile of Bellbowrie?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Bellbowrie had 5,495 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Bellbowrie?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 97.1% of occupied private dwellings in Bellbowrie.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 2.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Bellbowrie?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
34.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
52.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
13.5% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Bellbowrie?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 33.2%, while 33.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Bellbowrie?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

49.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
4.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Bellbowrie?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
1.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 28.5% of the total.
69.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Bellbowrie, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

33.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Bellbowrie?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

1.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.

Where were Bellbowrie residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

8.6% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.9% reported asthma, 7.5% arthritis and 4.1% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Bellbowrie?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 17.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 23.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Bellbowrie?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
