The median house price in Benalla, VIC is currently $515,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Benalla has a 12-month growth rate of 8.4% for houses and 4.4% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $670,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $412,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $502,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $610,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $650,000 in Benalla.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Benalla is $500 per week. The median house rent has increased 5.3% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Benalla is $355,000, which has increased 4.4% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $345,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $460,000 in Benalla. The median rent for units and apartments in Benalla is $350 per week and has decreased 6.7% over the last 12 months.
