The median house price in Bendigo, VIC is currently $660,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Bendigo has a 12-month growth rate of 5.6% for houses and 22.9% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $588,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $645,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,010,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,150,000 in Bendigo.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Bendigo is $525 per week. The median house rent has increased 10.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Bendigo is $580,000, which has increased 22.9% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $342,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $495,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $625,000 in Bendigo. ## Questions people ask about Bendigo
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Bendigo VIC 3550.
Is Bendigo family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
12.8% of Bendigo's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of Vic. average.
1,429 family households lived in Bendigo; 30.9% were couple families and 20.6% were one-parent families.
30.2% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Rest of Vic. average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Bendigo?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Bendigo had 5,652 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.5% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of Vic. average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Bendigo?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 81.7% of occupied private dwellings in Bendigo.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 14.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Bendigo?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
31.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Vic. average.
22.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
45.5% rented their home. That is about twice the Rest of Vic. rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Bendigo?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Bendigo had 2.1 people.
One-person households made up 38.5% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
Two-person households represented 35.3%, while 12.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Bendigo?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
15.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of Vic. average.
52.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Rest of Vic. average.
1.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 12.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Bendigo?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
9.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Rest of Vic. rate.
One-car households formed 45.1% of the total.
45.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Bendigo, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
51.5% were at the same address five years earlier, while 48.5% reported a different address.
12.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Vic..
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Bendigo?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
85.6% of residents used only English at home.
8.4% used a language other than English at home.
0.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Bendigo residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
12.6% of Bendigo's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 87.4% was born in Australia.
0.5% of residents were born in China.
2.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 87.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Bendigo?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 42.3% of ancestry responses in Bendigo.
Australian ancestry made up 35.5% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 2.1% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Bendigo?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
30.7% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
Managers accounted for 12.9% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.0%.
Health care and social assistance employed 22.1% of workers, and education and training employed 10.2%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Bendigo?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 60.8%, compared with 57.4% across Rest of Vic..
33.5% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 20.8% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.4%, compared with 4.1% across Rest of Vic..
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Bendigo show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
54.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
13.2% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.5% reported asthma, 10.7% arthritis and 4.3% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Bendigo?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
28.6% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 11.4% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.7%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 26.4%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Bendigo?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,482.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $290 per week.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Bendigo
### Is Bendigo expensive?
The median house price is $660,000, while units and apartments sit at $580,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Bendigo good for families?
The Census counted 1,429 local families, and 12.8% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Bendigo?
Larger houses have a median around $1,150,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
