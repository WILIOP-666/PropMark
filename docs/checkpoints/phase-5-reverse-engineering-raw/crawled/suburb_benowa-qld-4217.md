## Questions people ask about Benowa
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Benowa QLD 4217.
Is Benowa family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.5% of Benowa's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
2,776 family households lived in Benowa; 46.9% were couple families and 14.7% were one-parent families.
38.0% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Benowa?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Benowa had 9,889 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.1% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Benowa?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 60.8% of occupied private dwellings in Benowa.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 28.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 10.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Benowa?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
41.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
37.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
21.9% rented their home. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Benowa?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Benowa had 2.7 people.
One-person households made up 18.6% of occupied homes. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 34.4%, while 29.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Benowa?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
17.3% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
61.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
0.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Benowa?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
One-car households formed 32.6% of the total.
65.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Benowa, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
49.0% were at the same address five years earlier, while 51.0% reported a different address.
36.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Rest of Qld rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Benowa?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
69.8% of residents used only English at home.
24.6% used a language other than English at home.
6.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Benowa residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
36.6% of Benowa's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 63.4% was born in Australia.
4.2% of residents were born in China.
1.4% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 80.2%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

Australian ancestry made up 24.7% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 9.6% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Benowa?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
24.7% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Managers accounted for 16.4% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.9%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.9% of workers, and education and training employed 7.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Benowa?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

29.1% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 21.6% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.8%, compared with 5.2% across Rest of Qld.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Benowa show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
64.7% reported no selected long-term health condition.
5.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.1% reported asthma, 7.6% arthritis and 3.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Benowa?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
29.9% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 10.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 17.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 24.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Benowa?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
