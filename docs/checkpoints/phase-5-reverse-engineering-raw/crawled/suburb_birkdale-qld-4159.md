The median house price in Birkdale, QLD is currently $1,280,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Birkdale has a 12-month growth rate of 7.8% for houses and 9.9% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $785,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,060,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,390,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,710,000 in Birkdale.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Birkdale is $830,000, which has increased 9.9% since the same time last year.
