The median house price in Blacktown, NSW is currently $1,160,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Blacktown has a 12-month growth rate of 5.4% for houses and -2.9% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $895,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,070,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,240,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,360,000 in Blacktown.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Blacktown is $512,000, which has decreased 2.9% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $413,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $465,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $800,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $960,000 in Blacktown. Is Blacktown family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Blacktown?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
