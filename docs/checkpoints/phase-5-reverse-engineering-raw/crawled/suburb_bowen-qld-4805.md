The median house price in Bowen, QLD is currently $560,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Bowen has a 12-month growth rate of 1.8% for houses and 28.3% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $480,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $465,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $540,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $685,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $736,000 in Bowen.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Bowen is $600 per week. The median house rent has increased 4.3% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Bowen is $417,000, which has increased 28.3% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $210,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $375,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $445,000 in Bowen. Is Bowen family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.2% of Bowen's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
2,760 family households lived in Bowen; 36.2% were couple families and 14.9% were one-parent families.
35.8% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Bowen?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Bowen had 11,205 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
35.1% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Bowen?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 83.2% of occupied private dwellings in Bowen.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 13.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Bowen?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
34.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
26.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
39.3% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Bowen?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Bowen had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 29.2% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 37.0%, while 21.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Bowen?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
4.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
66.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
2.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Bowen?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.0% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 35.2% of the total.
58.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Bowen, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

14.5% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Bowen?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
81.8% of residents used only English at home.
8.0% used a language other than English at home.
1.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Bowen residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
14.5% of Bowen's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 85.5% was born in Australia.
0.3% of residents were born in China.
0.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 82.3%.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

8.1% reported asthma, 10.3% arthritis and 5.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Bowen?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
