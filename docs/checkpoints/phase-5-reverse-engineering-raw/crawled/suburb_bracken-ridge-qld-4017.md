The median house price in Bracken Ridge, QLD is currently $1,140,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Bracken Ridge has a 12-month growth rate of 17.6% for houses and 20.7% for units.
Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,030,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,170,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,340,000 in Bracken Ridge.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Bracken Ridge is $738 per week. The median house rent has increased 9.3% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Bracken Ridge is $860,000, which has increased 20.7% since the same time last year.
