Is Brassall family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

3,194 family households lived in Brassall; 37.6% were couple families and 26.7% were one-parent families.
42.8% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Brassall?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Brassall had 12,115 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Brassall?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 0.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Brassall?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

28.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
47.7% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Brassall?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.

How educated is Brassall?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
