Is Broadford family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

37.0% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Vic. average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Broadford?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Broadford had 4,592 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.6% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Rest of Vic. average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Broadford?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Broadford?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

47.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
19.2% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Rest of Vic. average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Broadford?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Broadford?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Broadford?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

65.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Broadford
### Is Broadford expensive?
The median house price is $622,000, while units and apartments sit at $492,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Broadford good for families?
The Census counted 1,273 local families, and 19.2% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Broadford?
Larger houses have a median around $945,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
