## Questions people ask about Brunswick
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Brunswick VIC 3056.
Is Brunswick family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
10.3% of Brunswick's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
5,779 family households lived in Brunswick; 32.2% were couple families and 11.3% were one-parent families.
27.7% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Brunswick?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Brunswick had 24,896 residents at the 2021 Census.
47.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
19.6% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Brunswick?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 35.3% of occupied private dwellings in Brunswick.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 29.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 34.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Brunswick?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
24.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
26.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
49.1% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Brunswick?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Brunswick had 2.1 people.
One-person households made up 34.2% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 36.4%, while 13.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Brunswick?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
44.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
21.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
7.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 9.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Brunswick?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
21.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 52.2% of the total.
26.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Brunswick, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
43.4% were at the same address five years earlier, while 56.6% reported a different address.
30.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Brunswick?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
72.9% of residents used only English at home.
23.8% used a language other than English at home.
1.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.7% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Brunswick residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
30.6% of Brunswick's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 69.4% was born in Australia.
1.2% of residents were born in China.
1.0% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 83.3%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

English ancestry accounted for 31.1% of ancestry responses in Brunswick.
Australian ancestry made up 25.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 4.8% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Brunswick?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
44.1% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Managers accounted for 14.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.0%.
Health care and social assistance employed 14.3% of workers, and education and training employed 12.5%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Brunswick?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 74.5%, compared with 64.1% across Greater Melbourne.
43.6% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 23.1% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.7%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Melbourne.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Brunswick show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
59.3% reported no selected long-term health condition.
15.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.8% reported asthma, 5.6% arthritis and 3.3% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Brunswick?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
43.1% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 23.0% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 9.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 10.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Brunswick?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,162.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $441 per week.

How does Brunswick compare with Greater Melbourne?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
