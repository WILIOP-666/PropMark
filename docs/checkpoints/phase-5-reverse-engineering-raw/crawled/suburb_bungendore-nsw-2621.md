## Questions people ask about Bungendore
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Bungendore NSW 2621.
Is Bungendore family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
23.0% of Bungendore's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
1,380 family households lived in Bungendore; 51.7% were couple families and 9.6% were one-parent families.
42.0% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Bungendore?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Bungendore had 4,745 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.8% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Bungendore?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 94.1% of occupied private dwellings in Bungendore.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 3.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Bungendore?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
26.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
59.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
13.8% rented their home. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Bungendore?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Bungendore had 2.9 people.
One-person households made up 13.9% of occupied homes. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
Two-person households represented 34.4%, while 33.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Bungendore?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
13.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
66.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
0.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Bungendore?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
1.0% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
One-car households formed 19.8% of the total.
79.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Bungendore, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
55.2% were at the same address five years earlier, while 44.8% reported a different address.
12.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Bungendore?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
92.7% of residents used only English at home.
3.3% used a language other than English at home.
0.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Bungendore residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
12.7% of Bungendore's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 87.3% was born in Australia.
0.1% of residents were born in China.
0.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 93.3%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Bungendore?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 43.6% of ancestry responses in Bungendore.
Australian ancestry made up 44.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 0.7% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Bungendore?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
23.6% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Managers accounted for 19.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 16.1%.
Health care and social assistance employed 7.7% of workers, and education and training employed 7.8%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Bungendore?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 75.3%, compared with 56.4% across Rest of NSW.
49.7% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 19.7% worked part time.
Unemployment was 2.3%, compared with 4.6% across Rest of NSW.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Bungendore show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
60.7% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.5% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.0% reported asthma, 7.9% arthritis and 3.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Bungendore?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
23.2% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 13.1% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 19.1%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 31.0%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Bungendore?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,922 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,383.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $510 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Bungendore compare with Rest of NSW?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
