Is Burns Beach family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

What is the age profile of Burns Beach?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+

Do people own or rent in Burns Beach?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Burns Beach?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
