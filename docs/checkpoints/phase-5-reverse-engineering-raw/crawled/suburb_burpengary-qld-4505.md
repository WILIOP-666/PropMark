The median house price in Burpengary, QLD is currently $970,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Burpengary has a 12-month growth rate of 12.8% for houses and 21.4% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $700,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $890,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $980,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,300,000 in Burpengary.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Burpengary is $650 per week. The median house rent has increased 8.3% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Burpengary is $695,000, which has increased 21.4% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $624,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $715,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $820,000 in Burpengary. The median rent for units and apartments in Burpengary is $525 per week and has increased 10.5% over the last 12 months.

Is Burpengary family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
19.8% of Burpengary's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
4,601 family households lived in Burpengary; 42.3% were couple families and 19.0% were one-parent families.
39.1% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Burpengary?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Burpengary had 16,488 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.3% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Burpengary?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 83.5% of occupied private dwellings in Burpengary.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 15.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Burpengary?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
26.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
41.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
31.2% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Burpengary?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 34.4%, while 28.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Burpengary?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
11.2% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
63.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
3.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Burpengary?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
One-car households formed 30.3% of the total.
66.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Burpengary, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

16.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Burpengary?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

5.8% used a language other than English at home.
0.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Burpengary residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

What does the health profile of Burpengary show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+

10.8% reported asthma, 10.1% arthritis and 5.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Burpengary?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
