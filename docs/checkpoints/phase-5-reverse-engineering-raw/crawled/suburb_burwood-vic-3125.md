## Questions people ask about Burwood
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Burwood VIC 3125.
Is Burwood family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
13.4% of Burwood's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
3,519 family households lived in Burwood; 43.5% were couple families and 16.3% were one-parent families.
34.7% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Burwood?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Burwood had 15,147 residents at the 2021 Census.
30.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
25.4% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Burwood?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 57.9% of occupied private dwellings in Burwood.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 26.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 15.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Burwood?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
28.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
39.4% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Burwood?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Burwood had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 27.7% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 30.6%, while 23.3% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Burwood?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
31.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
41.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
4.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Burwood?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
10.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
One-car households formed 39.8% of the total.
49.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Burwood, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
48.1% were at the same address five years earlier, while 51.9% reported a different address.
46.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Burwood?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
50.5% of residents used only English at home.
44.5% used a language other than English at home.
16.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 4.3% spoke Cantonese and 1.9% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Burwood residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
46.4% of Burwood's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 53.6% was born in Australia.
13.4% of residents were born in China.
6.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 69.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Burwood?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 20.9% of ancestry responses in Burwood.
Australian ancestry made up 18.5% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 25.0% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Burwood?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
31.6% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Managers accounted for 13.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.6%.
Health care and social assistance employed 14.2% of workers, and education and training employed 10.7%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Burwood?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 60.9%, compared with 64.1% across Greater Melbourne.
30.4% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 22.3% worked part time.
Unemployment was 7.2%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Melbourne.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Burwood show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
65.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
7.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.5% reported asthma, 6.1% arthritis and 3.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Burwood?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
36.7% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 20.2% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 12.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Burwood?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,684 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,500.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $411 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Burwood compare with Greater Melbourne?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
