## Questions people ask about Caloundra
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Caloundra QLD 4551.
Is Caloundra family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
8.6% of Caloundra's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
990 family households lived in Caloundra; 22.1% were couple families and 19.1% were one-parent families.
21.1% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Caloundra?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Caloundra had 3,932 residents at the 2021 Census.
17.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
54.3% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Caloundra?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 23.6% of occupied private dwellings in Caloundra.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 26.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 48.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Caloundra?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
40.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
15.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
44.2% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Caloundra?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Caloundra had 1.9 people.
One-person households made up 42.2% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 38.6%, while 9.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Caloundra?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
12.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
55.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
1.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 9.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Caloundra?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
12.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Rest of Qld rate.
One-car households formed 53.8% of the total.
33.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Caloundra, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
47.9% were at the same address five years earlier, while 52.1% reported a different address.
21.8% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Caloundra?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
87.1% of residents used only English at home.
6.2% used a language other than English at home.
0.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Caloundra residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
21.8% of Caloundra's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 78.2% was born in Australia.
0.1% of residents were born in China.
0.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 84.0%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.

Health care and social assistance employed 19.2% of workers, and education and training employed 8.0%.

Unemployment was 6.4%, compared with 5.2% across Rest of Qld.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

11.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.8% reported asthma, 17.0% arthritis and 5.4% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Caloundra?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 15.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 35.6%.
