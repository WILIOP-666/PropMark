Is Camp Hill family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

What is the age profile of Camp Hill?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Camp Hill?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Camp Hill?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
26.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
44.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Camp Hill?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

6.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
