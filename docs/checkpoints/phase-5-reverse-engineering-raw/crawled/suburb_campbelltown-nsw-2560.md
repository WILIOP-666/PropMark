Is Campbelltown family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

42.9% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Campbelltown?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Campbelltown had 16,577 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Campbelltown?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 34.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Campbelltown?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

29.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Campbelltown?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

4.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Campbelltown?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
