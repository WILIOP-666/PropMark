Do people own or rent in Camperdown?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

23.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.

How do people get to work in Camperdown?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

4.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 10.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Camperdown?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

17.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Camperdown, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
