Is Campsie family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

6,471 family households lived in Campsie; 40.3% were couple families and 18.3% were one-parent families.
32.4% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Campsie?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Campsie had 26,132 residents at the 2021 Census.
37.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.6% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Campsie?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 67.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Campsie?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

24.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
54.8% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Campsie?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 30.7%, while 24.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Campsie?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

29.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
9.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Campsie?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
19.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 49.4% of the total.
30.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Campsie, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Where were Campsie residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Campsie?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
