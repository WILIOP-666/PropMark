The median house price in Capalaba, QLD is currently $1,090,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Capalaba has a 12-month growth rate of 12.9% for houses and 17.3% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $730,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,000,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,170,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,530,000 in Capalaba.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Capalaba is $750 per week. The median house rent has increased 11.1% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Capalaba is $762,000, which has increased 17.3% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $660,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $725,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $850,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $915,000 in Capalaba. The median rent for units and apartments in Capalaba is $600 per week and has increased 9.1% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Capalaba

Is Capalaba family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.4% of Capalaba's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
5,092 family households lived in Capalaba; 42.1% were couple families and 18.2% were one-parent families.
36.7% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Capalaba?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Capalaba had 18,002 residents at the 2021 Census.
27.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.9% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Capalaba?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 77.7% of occupied private dwellings in Capalaba.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 18.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Capalaba?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
30.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
43.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
26.4% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Capalaba?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Capalaba had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 23.6% of occupied homes. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
Two-person households represented 34.6%, while 24.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Capalaba?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
13.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
64.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
2.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Capalaba?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
One-car households formed 31.7% of the total.
63.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Capalaba, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
62.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 37.4% reported a different address.
22.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Capalaba?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
86.0% of residents used only English at home.
9.9% used a language other than English at home.
0.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Capalaba residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
22.0% of Capalaba's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 78.0% was born in Australia.
0.6% of residents were born in China.
1.1% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 88.3%.

Australian ancestry made up 35.7% of responses.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Capalaba?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 14.9% of workers, and education and training employed 7.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Capalaba?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

11.2% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.0% reported asthma, 9.8% arthritis and 5.4% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Capalaba?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 4.5% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 17.1%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 44.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
