The median house price in Carlingford, NSW is currently $2,110,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Carlingford has a 12-month growth rate of -4.0% for houses and -2.1% for units.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Carlingford is $704,000, which has decreased 2.1% since the same time last year. ## Questions people ask about Carlingford

Is Carlingford family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
20.9% of Carlingford's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
8,017 family households lived in Carlingford; 56.4% were couple families and 13.0% were one-parent families.
42.8% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Carlingford?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Carlingford had 28,044 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.6% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Carlingford?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 58.1% of occupied private dwellings in Carlingford.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 29.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Carlingford?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
31.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
34.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
33.8% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Carlingford?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Carlingford had 3.0 people.
One-person households made up 14.2% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 27.8%, while 36.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Carlingford?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
43.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
33.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
2.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Carlingford?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
4.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 37.7% of the total.
57.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Carlingford, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
57.0% were at the same address five years earlier, while 43.0% reported a different address.
55.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Carlingford?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
32.9% of residents used only English at home.
63.8% used a language other than English at home.
22.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 13.2% spoke Cantonese and 2.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Carlingford residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
55.3% of Carlingford's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 44.7% was born in Australia.
19.4% of residents were born in China.
4.9% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 76.1%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

Australian ancestry made up 11.6% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 40.6% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Carlingford?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
35.5% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 14.1% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 15.0%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.0% of workers, and education and training employed 9.3%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Carlingford?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

32.0% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 16.2% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.2%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Carlingford show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
71.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
4.0% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
5.1% reported asthma, 5.0% arthritis and 4.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Carlingford?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
39.8% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 20.1% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 14.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Carlingford?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,500.
