Is Castlecrag family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Castlecrag?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 1.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Castlecrag?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Castlecrag?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Castlecrag?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Castlecrag?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Castlecrag, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
