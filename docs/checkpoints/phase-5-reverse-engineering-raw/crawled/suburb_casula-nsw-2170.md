Is Casula family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.1% of Casula's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
4,279 family households lived in Casula; 56.3% were couple families and 18.2% were one-parent families.
41.7% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Casula?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Casula had 16,584 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
25.8% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Casula?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 71.0% of occupied private dwellings in Casula.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 24.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Casula?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
27.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
39.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
33.1% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Casula?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Casula?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

43.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
2.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Casula?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 31.0% of the total.
61.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Casula, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Casula?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
