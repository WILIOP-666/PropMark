## Questions people ask about Ceduna

Is Ceduna family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.6% of Ceduna's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of SA average.
507 family households lived in Ceduna; 38.3% were couple families and 19.5% were one-parent families.
41.4% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of SA average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Ceduna?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Ceduna had 1,955 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.0% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of SA average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Ceduna?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 89.2% of occupied private dwellings in Ceduna.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 5.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Ceduna?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
30.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of SA average.
24.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
44.5% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of SA average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Ceduna?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Ceduna had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 30.8% of occupied homes. That is close to the Rest of SA average.
Two-person households represented 33.9%, while 22.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Ceduna?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
4.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of SA rate.
69.0% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of SA average.
0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 9.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Ceduna?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
9.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Rest of SA average.
One-car households formed 33.6% of the total.
57.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Ceduna, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

8.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Rest of SA average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Ceduna?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
82.2% of residents used only English at home.
9.3% used a language other than English at home.
0.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Ceduna residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
8.3% of Ceduna's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 91.7% was born in Australia.
0.0% of residents were born in China.
2.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 88.5%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Ceduna?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
17.4% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat above the Rest of SA average.
Managers accounted for 12.9% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 10.9%.
Health care and social assistance employed 16.7% of workers, and education and training employed 12.8%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Ceduna?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Unemployment was 4.6%, compared with 4.8% across Rest of SA.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Ceduna show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
58.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
6.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.1% reported asthma, 7.8% arthritis and 5.7% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Ceduna?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
15.5% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of SA.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 3.1% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.3%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 39.0%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
