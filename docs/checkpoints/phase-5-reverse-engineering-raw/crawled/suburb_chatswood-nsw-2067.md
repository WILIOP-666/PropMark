## Questions people ask about Chatswood
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Chatswood NSW 2067.
Is Chatswood family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
16.7% of Chatswood's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
6,937 family households lived in Chatswood; 45.7% were couple families and 14.3% were one-parent families.
39.1% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Chatswood?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Chatswood had 25,553 residents at the 2021 Census.
34.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.6% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Chatswood?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 27.9% of occupied private dwellings in Chatswood.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 67.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Chatswood?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
27.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
20.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
52.1% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Chatswood?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Chatswood had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 24.1% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 33.3%, while 24.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Chatswood?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
49.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
18.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
7.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 7.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Chatswood?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
21.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 53.0% of the total.
25.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Chatswood, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
45.3% were at the same address five years earlier, while 54.7% reported a different address.
62.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Chatswood?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
34.8% of residents used only English at home.
61.0% used a language other than English at home.
23.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 12.3% spoke Cantonese and 1.9% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Chatswood residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
62.0% of Chatswood's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 38.0% was born in Australia.
20.1% of residents were born in China.
4.0% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 65.2%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Chatswood?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 13.7% of ancestry responses in Chatswood.
Australian ancestry made up 10.9% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 42.4% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Chatswood?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
39.6% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 17.5% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.3%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.3% of workers, and education and training employed 6.7%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Chatswood?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 60.6%, compared with 60.0% across Greater Sydney.
35.1% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 16.3% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.5%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Chatswood show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
73.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
4.3% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
4.6% reported asthma, 3.9% arthritis and 3.4% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Chatswood?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
42.9% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 23.6% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 8.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Chatswood?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,158 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,858.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $577 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Chatswood compare with Greater Sydney?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 49.9% in Chatswood, compared with 38.9% across Greater Sydney.
Outright ownership was 27.1% locally and 28.6% across Greater Sydney. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Renting accounted for 52.1% in Chatswood, against 37.1% across Greater Sydney.
