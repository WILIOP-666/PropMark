## Questions people ask about Cheltenham

Is Cheltenham family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.0% of Cheltenham's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
6,569 family households lived in Cheltenham; 47.4% were couple families and 15.8% were one-parent families.
39.8% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Cheltenham?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Cheltenham had 23,992 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Cheltenham?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 58.5% of occupied private dwellings in Cheltenham.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 22.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 18.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Cheltenham?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
33.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
38.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
27.9% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Cheltenham?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Cheltenham?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

42.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
2.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Cheltenham?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
One-car households formed 42.1% of the total.
51.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Cheltenham, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

3.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.8% spoke Cantonese and 1.0% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Cheltenham?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
