Is Cherrybrook family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

37.8% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Cherrybrook?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Cherrybrook had 19,082 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.6% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Cherrybrook?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 1.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Cherrybrook?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

44.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

2.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
