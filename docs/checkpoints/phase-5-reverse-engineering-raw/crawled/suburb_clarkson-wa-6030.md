The median house price in Clarkson, WA is currently $868,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Clarkson has a 12-month growth rate of 18.1% for houses and 11.7% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $620,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $729,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $850,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $902,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $900,000 in Clarkson.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Clarkson is $670,000, which has increased 11.7% since the same time last year. Is Clarkson family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

44.0% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Clarkson?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Clarkson had 13,904 residents at the 2021 Census.
33.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
19.7% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Clarkson?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 89.3% of occupied private dwellings in Clarkson.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 9.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Clarkson?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

52.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Clarkson?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Clarkson?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

6.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Clarkson?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 38.1% of the total.
57.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Clarkson, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

20.2% used a language other than English at home.
1.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Clarkson?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
