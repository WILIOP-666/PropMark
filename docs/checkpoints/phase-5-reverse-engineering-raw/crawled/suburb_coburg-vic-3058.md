The median house price in Coburg, VIC is currently $1,280,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Coburg has a 12-month growth rate of 5.7% for houses and -1.1% for units.

Is Coburg family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
15.2% of Coburg's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
6,858 family households lived in Coburg; 44.9% were couple families and 14.1% were one-parent families.
35.8% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Coburg?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Coburg had 26,574 residents at the 2021 Census.
35.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.2% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Coburg?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 65.1% of occupied private dwellings in Coburg.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 24.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 10.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Coburg?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
33.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
32.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
34.0% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Coburg?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 25.3% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 32.8%, while 23.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Coburg?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
38.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
31.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
5.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 5.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Coburg?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
13.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
One-car households formed 46.6% of the total.
40.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Coburg, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Coburg?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

31.6% used a language other than English at home.
1.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.4% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Coburg residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How active is the workforce in Coburg?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Coburg?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
