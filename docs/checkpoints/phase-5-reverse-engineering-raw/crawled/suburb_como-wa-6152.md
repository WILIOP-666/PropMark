The median house price is $1,700,000, while units and apartments sit at $855,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Como (WA) good for families?
The Census counted 3,780 local families, and 13.1% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
