Is Concord family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.3% of Concord's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.

35.7% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Concord?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Concord had 14,551 residents at the 2021 Census.
22.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
33.1% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Concord?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 70.6% of occupied private dwellings in Concord.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 16.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Concord?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
44.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
31.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
23.8% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Concord?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Concord?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

2.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Concord?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

57.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Concord, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Where were Concord residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

What work do people do in Concord?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 14.0% of workers, and education and training employed 10.0%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Concord?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

5.8% reported asthma, 8.0% arthritis and 4.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Concord?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.

### Is Concord expensive?
The median house price is $3,320,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,360,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Concord good for families?
The Census counted 4,032 local families, and 17.3% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Concord?
