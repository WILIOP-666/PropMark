Is Cooktown family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

638 family households lived in Cooktown; 35.6% were couple families and 19.4% were one-parent families.
40.0% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Cooktown?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Cooktown had 2,746 residents at the 2021 Census.
21.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
36.1% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Cooktown?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 8.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Cooktown?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
37.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
23.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
39.2% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Cooktown?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Cooktown?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 8.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Cooktown?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
7.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 41.0% of the total.
51.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Cooktown, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Cooktown?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
