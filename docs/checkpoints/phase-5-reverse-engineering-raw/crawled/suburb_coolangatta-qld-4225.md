Is Coolangatta family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

21.9% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.

Do people own or rent in Coolangatta?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
