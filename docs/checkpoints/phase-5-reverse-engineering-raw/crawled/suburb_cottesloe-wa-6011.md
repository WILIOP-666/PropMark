Is Cottesloe family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Cottesloe had 7,750 residents at the 2021 Census.

What kind of homes are in Cottesloe?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Cottesloe?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Cottesloe?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

7.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Cottesloe?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Cottesloe, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
