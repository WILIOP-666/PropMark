## Questions people ask about Cowra
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Cowra NSW 2794.
Is Cowra family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.6% of Cowra's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.
2,679 family households lived in Cowra; 33.6% were couple families and 18.7% were one-parent families.
33.8% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Cowra?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Cowra had 10,145 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
39.2% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Cowra?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 90.4% of occupied private dwellings in Cowra.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 6.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Cowra?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
43.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
27.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
29.3% rented their home. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Cowra?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Cowra had 2.3 people.
One-person households made up 33.5% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 36.0%, while 17.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Cowra?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
7.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
68.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
0.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Cowra?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
7.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 38.2% of the total.
54.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Cowra, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
62.7% were at the same address five years earlier, while 37.3% reported a different address.
6.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Cowra?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
88.8% of residents used only English at home.
4.1% used a language other than English at home.
0.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Cowra residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
6.9% of Cowra's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 93.1% was born in Australia.
0.2% of residents were born in China.
0.4% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 90.9%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Cowra?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 41.6% of ancestry responses in Cowra.
Australian ancestry made up 43.5% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 0.5% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Cowra?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
13.5% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Managers accounted for 15.7% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.1%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.4% of workers, and education and training employed 8.5%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Cowra?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 52.1%, compared with 56.4% across Rest of NSW.
28.9% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 17.2% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.6%, compared with 4.6% across Rest of NSW.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Cowra show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
51.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
11.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.1% reported asthma, 12.9% arthritis and 6.5% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Cowra?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
14.2% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 2.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 45.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Cowra?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,107 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,235.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $230 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Cowra compare with Rest of NSW?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 7.9% in Cowra, compared with 15.7% across Rest of NSW.
Outright ownership was 43.4% locally and 39.6% across Rest of NSW. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Renting accounted for 29.3% in Cowra, against 27.9% across Rest of NSW.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.
