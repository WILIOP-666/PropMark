The median house price in Cranbourne, VIC is currently $723,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Cranbourne has a 12-month growth rate of 3.3% for houses and 9.3% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $680,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $548,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $695,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $800,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $995,000 in Cranbourne.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Cranbourne is $525 per week. The median house rent has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Cranbourne is $558,000, which has increased 9.3% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $520,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $615,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $703,000 in Cranbourne. The median rent for units and apartments in Cranbourne is $475 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.

Is Cranbourne family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

What is the age profile of Cranbourne?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Cranbourne had 21,281 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Cranbourne?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 3.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Cranbourne?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
23.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
41.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Cranbourne?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Cranbourne?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

2.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Cranbourne?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 35.7% of the total.
58.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Cranbourne, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How active is the workforce in Cranbourne?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

How educated is Cranbourne?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
