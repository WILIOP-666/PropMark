Is Croydon family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.0% of Croydon's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
7,785 family households lived in Croydon; 42.9% were couple families and 17.7% were one-parent families.
38.3% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Croydon?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Croydon had 28,608 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.1% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Croydon?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 83.4% of occupied private dwellings in Croydon.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 11.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 5.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Croydon?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
33.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
39.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
27.4% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Croydon?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Croydon?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
26.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
51.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
2.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Croydon?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
One-car households formed 40.8% of the total.
53.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Croydon, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

25.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

How educated is Croydon?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
