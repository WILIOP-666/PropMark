## Questions people ask about Dalby
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Dalby QLD 4405.
Is Dalby family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
22.6% of Dalby's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
3,299 family households lived in Dalby; 40.8% were couple families and 19.1% were one-parent families.
42.7% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Dalby?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Dalby had 12,758 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
26.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Dalby?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 91.2% of occupied private dwellings in Dalby.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 7.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Dalby?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
29.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
34.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
35.8% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Dalby?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Dalby had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 25.7% of occupied homes. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
Two-person households represented 35.0%, while 24.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Dalby?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
4.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
70.0% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Dalby?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 34.6% of the total.
59.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Dalby, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
55.3% were at the same address five years earlier, while 44.7% reported a different address.
8.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Dalby?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
88.7% of residents used only English at home.
4.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Dalby residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
8.0% of Dalby's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 92.0% was born in Australia.
0.1% of residents were born in China.
0.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 89.6%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Dalby?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 39.0% of ancestry responses in Dalby.
Australian ancestry made up 42.6% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 0.6% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Dalby?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
13.6% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Managers accounted for 11.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.4%.
Health care and social assistance employed 11.6% of workers, and education and training employed 8.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Dalby?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 62.5%, compared with 59.1% across Rest of Qld.
37.3% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 18.7% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.8%, compared with 5.2% across Rest of Qld.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Dalby show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
54.4% reported no selected long-term health condition.
11.0% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.9% reported asthma, 9.2% arthritis and 5.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Dalby?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
16.3% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 2.0% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.7%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 49.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Dalby?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,462 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,300.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $260 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Dalby compare with Rest of Qld?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 4.9% in Dalby, compared with 10.6% across Rest of Qld.
Outright ownership was 29.3% locally and 33.4% across Rest of Qld. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Renting accounted for 35.8% in Dalby, against 32.4% across Rest of Qld.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Dalby
### Is Dalby expensive?
The median house price is $570,000, while units and apartments sit at $460,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Dalby good for families?
The Census counted 3,299 local families, and 22.6% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Dalby?
Larger houses have a median around $770,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
Medians describe recorded market activity across the suburb; they are historical context, not a valuation or forecast for an individual home.
