Is Dapto family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

3,106 family households lived in Dapto; 40.9% were couple families and 20.4% were one-parent families.
36.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Dapto?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Dapto had 10,954 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.2% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Dapto?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 84.7% of occupied private dwellings in Dapto.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Dapto?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
35.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
39.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
25.4% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Dapto?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Dapto?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
15.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.

1.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Dapto?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 35.0% of the total.
58.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Dapto, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

12.3% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.3% reported asthma, 12.6% arthritis and 6.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Dapto?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
