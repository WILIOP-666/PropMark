Is Darlinghurst family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
5.2% of Darlinghurst's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
2,109 family households lived in Darlinghurst; 16.8% were couple families and 8.2% were one-parent families.
16.3% of local families included children under 15. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Darlinghurst?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Darlinghurst had 10,615 residents at the 2021 Census.
50.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
22.6% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Darlinghurst?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 0.9% of occupied private dwellings in Darlinghurst.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 19.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 78.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Darlinghurst?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
17.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
19.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
63.1% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Darlinghurst?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Darlinghurst had 1.7 people.
One-person households made up 51.7% of occupied homes. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
Two-person households represented 35.5%, while 5.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Darlinghurst?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
55.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
10.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
6.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 14.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Darlinghurst?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
45.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 44.1% of the total.
10.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Darlinghurst, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
35.4% were at the same address five years earlier, while 64.6% reported a different address.
40.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Darlinghurst?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
71.8% of residents used only English at home.
20.7% used a language other than English at home.
1.9% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Darlinghurst residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
40.4% of Darlinghurst's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 59.6% was born in Australia.
1.6% of residents were born in China.
0.9% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 72.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Darlinghurst?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+

Australian ancestry made up 19.4% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 6.0% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Darlinghurst?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
45.2% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 21.3% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 9.7%.
Health care and social assistance employed 10.0% of workers, and education and training employed 5.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Darlinghurst?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

48.4% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 15.3% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.5%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Darlinghurst show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
63.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.2% reported asthma, 4.2% arthritis and 1.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Darlinghurst?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
44.6% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 19.8% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 10.7%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 9.1%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Darlinghurst?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
