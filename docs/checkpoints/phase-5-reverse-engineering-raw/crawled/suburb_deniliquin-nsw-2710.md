The median house price in Deniliquin, NSW is currently $415,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Deniliquin has a 12-month growth rate of 14.5% for houses and 15.6% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $300,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $380,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $495,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $625,000 in Deniliquin.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Deniliquin is $425 per week. The median house rent has increased 13.3% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Deniliquin is $330,000, which has increased 15.6% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $332,000 in Deniliquin. The median rent for units and apartments in Deniliquin is $275 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.

Is Deniliquin family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
