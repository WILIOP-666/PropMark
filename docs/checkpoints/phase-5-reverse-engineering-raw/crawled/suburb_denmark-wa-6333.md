What is the age profile of Denmark?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Denmark had 2,691 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Denmark?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.

Do people own or rent in Denmark?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

25.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
30.0% rented their home. That is close to the Rest of WA average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Denmark?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Denmark?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 6.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Denmark?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

48.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Denmark, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Health care and social assistance employed 12.5% of workers, and education and training employed 13.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Denmark?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Denmark?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 6.5% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 31.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
