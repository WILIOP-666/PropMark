The median house price in Devonport, TAS is currently $594,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Devonport has a 12-month growth rate of 15.4% for houses and 16.7% for units.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Devonport is $455,000, which has increased 16.7% since the same time last year.
