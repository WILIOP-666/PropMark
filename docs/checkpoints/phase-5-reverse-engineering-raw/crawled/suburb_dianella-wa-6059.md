Is Dianella family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
15.4% of Dianella's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
6,619 family households lived in Dianella; 45.0% were couple families and 14.3% were one-parent families.
33.3% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Dianella?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Dianella had 24,169 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.2% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Dianella?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 71.4% of occupied private dwellings in Dianella.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 24.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Dianella?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
40.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
34.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
24.9% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Dianella?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Dianella?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

65.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
5.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Dianella?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
One-car households formed 37.6% of the total.
56.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Dianella, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

3.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 2.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Dianella?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
