Is Donvale family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

34.9% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Donvale?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Donvale had 12,644 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 3.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Donvale?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Donvale?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Donvale?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.

How educated is Donvale?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
