Is Doonside family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Doonside?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Doonside?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
