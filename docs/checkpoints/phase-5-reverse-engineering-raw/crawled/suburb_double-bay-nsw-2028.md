Is Double Bay family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
12.4% of Double Bay's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
1,228 family households lived in Double Bay; 33.6% were couple families and 12.9% were one-parent families.
30.3% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Double Bay?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Double Bay had 4,709 residents at the 2021 Census.
35.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.5% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Double Bay?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 13.5% of occupied private dwellings in Double Bay.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 8.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 76.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Double Bay?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
35.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
20.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
44.7% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Double Bay?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Double Bay had 2.1 people.
One-person households made up 34.1% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 39.6%, while 11.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Double Bay?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
60.3% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
16.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
2.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 5.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Double Bay?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
18.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 51.2% of the total.
30.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Double Bay, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

35.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Double Bay?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
79.2% of residents used only English at home.
16.5% used a language other than English at home.
2.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Double Bay residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.6% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 83.7%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Double Bay?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 24.1% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 10.6%.
Health care and social assistance employed 10.2% of workers, and education and training employed 6.0%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Double Bay?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Double Bay?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 10.3%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 7.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.

Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $720 per week.

How does Double Bay compare with Greater Sydney?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+

## A buyer’s quick guide to Double Bay
### Is Double Bay expensive?
The median house price is $7,980,000, while units and apartments sit at $2,020,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Double Bay good for families?
The Census counted 1,228 local families, and 12.4% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Double Bay?
