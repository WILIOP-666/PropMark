## Questions people ask about Doubleview
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Doubleview WA 6018.
Is Doubleview family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.5% of Doubleview's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Perth.
2,455 family households lived in Doubleview; 47.8% were couple families and 15.1% were one-parent families.
42.6% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Doubleview?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Doubleview had 9,205 residents at the 2021 Census.
32.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
23.7% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Doubleview?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 75.0% of occupied private dwellings in Doubleview.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 22.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Doubleview?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
25.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
42.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
32.2% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Doubleview?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Doubleview had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 27.8% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
Two-person households represented 31.0%, while 23.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Doubleview?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
9.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
61.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
3.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Doubleview?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
One-car households formed 34.6% of the total.
59.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Doubleview, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
54.5% were at the same address five years earlier, while 45.5% reported a different address.
29.8% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Doubleview?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
80.7% of residents used only English at home.
15.1% used a language other than English at home.
1.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.4% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Doubleview residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
29.8% of Doubleview's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 70.2% was born in Australia.
1.2% of residents were born in China.
1.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 87.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Doubleview?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 40.2% of ancestry responses in Doubleview.
Australian ancestry made up 31.3% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 3.7% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Doubleview?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
34.4% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Managers accounted for 15.9% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.0%.
Health care and social assistance employed 14.2% of workers, and education and training employed 10.7%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Doubleview?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 70.9%, compared with 65.2% across Greater Perth.
42.2% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 22.5% worked part time.
Unemployment was 3.6%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Perth.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Doubleview show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
65.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
8.9% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.3% reported asthma, 6.5% arthritis and 2.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Doubleview?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
37.6% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 11.6% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 21.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Doubleview?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,305 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,300.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $400 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Doubleview compare with Greater Perth?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+

Outright ownership was 25.8% locally and 29.3% across Greater Perth. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
Renting accounted for 32.2% in Doubleview, against 27.5% across Greater Perth.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Doubleview
### Is Doubleview expensive?
The median house price is $1,530,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,050,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Doubleview good for families?
The Census counted 2,455 local families, and 18.5% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Doubleview?
3-bedroom houses have a median around $1,340,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
Medians describe recorded market activity across the suburb; they are historical context, not a valuation or forecast for an individual home.
