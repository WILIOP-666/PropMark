The median house price is $8,000,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,770,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.

The Census counted 1,113 local families, and 20.4% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
