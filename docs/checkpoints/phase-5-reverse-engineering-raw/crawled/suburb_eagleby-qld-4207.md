Is Eagleby family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

39.4% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Eagleby?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Eagleby?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
