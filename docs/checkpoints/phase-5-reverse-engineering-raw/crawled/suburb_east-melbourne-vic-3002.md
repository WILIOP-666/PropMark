## Questions people ask about East Melbourne
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for East Melbourne VIC 3002.
Is East Melbourne family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
5.8% of East Melbourne's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
1,199 family households lived in East Melbourne; 19.3% were couple families and 7.0% were one-parent families.
16.2% of local families included children under 15. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of East Melbourne?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
East Melbourne had 4,896 residents at the 2021 Census.
39.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
35.9% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in East Melbourne?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 3.2% of occupied private dwellings in East Melbourne.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 22.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 73.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in East Melbourne?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
27.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
18.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
54.1% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in East Melbourne?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in East Melbourne had 1.8 people.
One-person households made up 43.2% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 42.5%, while 6.1% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in East Melbourne?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
48.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
18.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
5.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 13.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in East Melbourne?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
19.0% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 53.2% of the total.
27.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to East Melbourne, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
39.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 60.4% reported a different address.
27.5% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in East Melbourne?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
80.3% of residents used only English at home.
14.1% used a language other than English at home.
2.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were East Melbourne residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
27.5% of East Melbourne's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 72.5% was born in Australia.
2.0% of residents were born in China.
0.7% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 82.6%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape East Melbourne?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 38.1% of ancestry responses in East Melbourne.
Australian ancestry made up 23.3% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 6.7% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in East Melbourne?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
49.2% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is about twice the Greater Melbourne rate.
Managers accounted for 20.0% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.1%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.2% of workers, and education and training employed 9.3%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in East Melbourne?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 69.0%, compared with 64.1% across Greater Melbourne.
45.5% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 18.1% worked part time.
Unemployment was 3.2%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Melbourne.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of East Melbourne show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
59.7% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.7% reported asthma, 7.7% arthritis and 2.3% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is East Melbourne?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
43.1% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 23.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 10.3%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 7.1%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in East Melbourne?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,345 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,383.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $480 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does East Melbourne compare with Greater Melbourne?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+

## A buyer’s quick guide to East Melbourne
### Is East Melbourne expensive?
The median house price is $2,940,000, while units and apartments sit at $710,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is East Melbourne good for families?
The Census counted 1,199 local families, and 5.8% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in East Melbourne?
2-bedroom houses have a median around $1,250,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
