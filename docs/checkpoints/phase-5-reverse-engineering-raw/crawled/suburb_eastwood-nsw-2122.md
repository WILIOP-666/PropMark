Is Eastwood family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

35.0% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Eastwood?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Eastwood had 18,695 residents at the 2021 Census.
31.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.1% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Eastwood?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 17.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 28.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Eastwood?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
31.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
29.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
39.8% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Eastwood?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Eastwood?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

30.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
5.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Eastwood?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
13.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 43.0% of the total.
43.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Eastwood, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

What languages are spoken in Eastwood?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

66.2% used a language other than English at home.
28.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 14.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.

Where were Eastwood residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How active is the workforce in Eastwood?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Eastwood?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.1%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 12.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
