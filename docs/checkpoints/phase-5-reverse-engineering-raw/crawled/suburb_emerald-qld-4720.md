Is Emerald family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Emerald?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Emerald?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Emerald?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
