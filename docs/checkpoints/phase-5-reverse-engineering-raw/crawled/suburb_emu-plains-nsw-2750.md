Is Emu Plains family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.6% of Emu Plains's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
2,263 family households lived in Emu Plains; 45.6% were couple families and 16.7% were one-parent families.
37.2% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Emu Plains?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Emu Plains had 8,126 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.8% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Emu Plains?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 82.2% of occupied private dwellings in Emu Plains.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Emu Plains?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
40.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
37.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
22.9% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Emu Plains?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Emu Plains had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 24.6% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 32.3%, while 26.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Emu Plains?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
31.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
44.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
1.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Emu Plains?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 34.9% of the total.
59.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Emu Plains, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

15.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Emu Plains?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

6.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Emu Plains residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.3% of residents were born in China.
0.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 91.7%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Emu Plains?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 12.8% of workers, and education and training employed 11.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Emu Plains?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

10.0% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.1% reported asthma, 10.9% arthritis and 5.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Emu Plains?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 15.7%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 40.6%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Emu Plains?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
