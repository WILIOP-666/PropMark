The median house price in Fairfield West, NSW is currently $1,280,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Fairfield West has a 12-month growth rate of 6.2% for houses and 9.5% for units.

Do people own or rent in Fairfield West?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Fairfield West?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.6% walked or cycled.

How many cars do households have in Fairfield West?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Fairfield West, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Fairfield West?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
