## Questions people ask about Fitzroy

Is Fitzroy family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
10.2% of Fitzroy's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
2,273 family households lived in Fitzroy; 24.1% were couple families and 16.0% were one-parent families.
25.8% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Fitzroy?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Fitzroy had 10,431 residents at the 2021 Census.
47.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.7% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Fitzroy?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 3.2% of occupied private dwellings in Fitzroy.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 36.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 59.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Fitzroy?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
18.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
20.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
60.7% rented their home. That is about twice the Greater Melbourne rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Fitzroy?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 38.3% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 37.5%, while 11.1% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Fitzroy?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
48.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
16.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
5.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 14.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Fitzroy?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
27.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 52.9% of the total.
19.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Fitzroy, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

31.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Fitzroy?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

23.0% used a language other than English at home.
2.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.8% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

Where were Fitzroy residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 78.7%.

What work do people do in Fitzroy?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

How educated is Fitzroy?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
