What is the age profile of Five Dock?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Five Dock had 9,823 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Flats and apartments accounted for 36.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Five Dock?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

30.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Five Dock?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

2.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Five Dock?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

47.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Five Dock, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

## A buyer’s quick guide to Five Dock
### Is Five Dock expensive?
The median house price is $2,500,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,140,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Five Dock good for families?
The Census counted 2,769 local families, and 17.8% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Five Dock?
2-bedroom houses have a median around $2,340,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
