## Questions people ask about Forrestfield

Is Forrestfield family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.9% of Forrestfield's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Perth.
3,652 family households lived in Forrestfield; 40.7% were couple families and 18.7% were one-parent families.
38.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Forrestfield?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Forrestfield had 13,181 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
28.7% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Forrestfield?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 88.6% of occupied private dwellings in Forrestfield.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 9.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Forrestfield?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
29.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Perth.
47.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
23.3% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Forrestfield?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Forrestfield had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 26.6% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
Two-person households represented 34.6%, while 22.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Forrestfield?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
4.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
70.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
2.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Forrestfield?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
4.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
One-car households formed 33.5% of the total.
62.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Forrestfield, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

14.5% used a language other than English at home.
0.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.9% spoke Hindi.

Where were Forrestfield residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

What work do people do in Forrestfield?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Forrestfield?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

9.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.3% reported asthma, 8.6% arthritis and 5.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Forrestfield?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 15.0%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 47.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
