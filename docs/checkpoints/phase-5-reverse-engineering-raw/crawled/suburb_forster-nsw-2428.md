The median house price in Forster (NSW), NSW is currently $885,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Forster (NSW) has a 12-month growth rate of 1.7% for houses and 4.4% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $588,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $840,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $975,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,380,000 in Forster (NSW).
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Forster (NSW) is $675 per week. The median house rent has increased 8.0% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Forster (NSW) is $655,000, which has increased 4.4% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $420,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $577,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $758,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $782,000 in Forster (NSW). The median rent for units and apartments in Forster (NSW) is $475 per week and has increased 11.8% over the last 12 months.

Is Forster family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
13.0% of Forster's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
3,927 family households lived in Forster; 25.1% were couple families and 17.2% were one-parent families.
25.4% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Forster?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Forster had 14,187 residents at the 2021 Census.
16.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
52.7% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Forster?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 64.6% of occupied private dwellings in Forster.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 15.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 17.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Forster?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
51.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
20.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
27.8% rented their home. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Forster?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 35.1% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 41.5%, while 13.3% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Forster?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
9.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
64.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
0.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Forster?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 47.4% of the total.
45.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Forster, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

11.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Forster?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

3.8% used a language other than English at home.
0.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Forster residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Forster?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 3.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.6%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 42.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Forster?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
