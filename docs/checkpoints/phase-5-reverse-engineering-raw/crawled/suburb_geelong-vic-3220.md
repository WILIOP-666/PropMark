The median house price in Geelong, VIC is currently $900,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Geelong has a 12-month growth rate of 3.5% for houses and 2.1% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $420,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $665,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $900,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,120,000 in Geelong.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Geelong is $575 per week. The median house rent has increased 4.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Geelong is $633,000, which has increased 2.1% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $412,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $600,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $838,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $715,000 in Geelong. The median rent for units and apartments in Geelong is $550 per week and has increased 10.0% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Geelong
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Geelong VIC 3220.
Is Geelong family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
10.2% of Geelong's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well below the Rest of Vic. average.
1,346 family households lived in Geelong; 33.1% were couple families and 11.4% were one-parent families.
26.2% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of Vic. average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Geelong?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Geelong had 5,811 residents at the 2021 Census.
29.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.8% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of Vic. average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Geelong?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 51.8% of occupied private dwellings in Geelong.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 21.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 25.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Geelong?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
34.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Vic. average.
20.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
45.1% rented their home. That is about twice the Rest of Vic. rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Geelong?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Geelong had 2.0 people.
One-person households made up 39.3% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
Two-person households represented 36.0%, while 12.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Geelong?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
21.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
42.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Rest of Vic. average.
2.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 15.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Geelong?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
11.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Rest of Vic. rate.
One-car households formed 47.5% of the total.
40.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Geelong, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
45.3% were at the same address five years earlier, while 54.7% reported a different address.
22.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Rest of Vic. rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Geelong?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
78.2% of residents used only English at home.
16.5% used a language other than English at home.
2.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.6% spoke Cantonese and 0.9% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Geelong residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
22.7% of Geelong's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 77.3% was born in Australia.
1.8% of residents were born in China.
2.8% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 82.1%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Geelong?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 35.3% of ancestry responses in Geelong.
Australian ancestry made up 30.1% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 4.1% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Geelong?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
32.5% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
Managers accounted for 13.2% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 10.8%.
Health care and social assistance employed 22.0% of workers, and education and training employed 9.7%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Geelong?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 63.5%, compared with 57.4% across Rest of Vic..
32.8% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 23.3% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.2%, compared with 4.1% across Rest of Vic..
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Geelong show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
58.9% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.6% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.6% reported asthma, 7.6% arthritis and 3.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Geelong?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
34.0% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Rest of Vic. average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 13.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 21.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Geelong?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,000.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $370 per week.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Geelong
### Is Geelong expensive?
The median house price is $900,000, while units and apartments sit at $633,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Geelong good for families?
The Census counted 1,346 local families, and 10.2% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Geelong?
4-bedroom houses have a median around $1,120,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
Medians describe recorded market activity across the suburb; they are historical context, not a valuation or forecast for an individual home.
