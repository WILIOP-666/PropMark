Is Glenelg family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
6.6% of Glenelg's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
841 family households lived in Glenelg; 22.5% were couple families and 9.9% were one-parent families.
19.1% of local families included children under 15. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Glenelg?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Glenelg had 3,440 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
50.7% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Glenelg?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 15.7% of occupied private dwellings in Glenelg.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 20.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 60.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Glenelg?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
39.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
15.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
44.9% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Glenelg?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Glenelg had 1.8 people.
One-person households made up 46.6% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
Two-person households represented 39.4%, while 6.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Glenelg?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
16.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
51.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
8.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 6.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Glenelg?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
13.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Adelaide rate.
One-car households formed 55.5% of the total.
30.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Glenelg, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
45.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 54.4% reported a different address.
29.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Adelaide average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Glenelg?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

15.2% used a language other than English at home.
1.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.

Where were Glenelg residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.9% of residents were born in China.

What work do people do in Glenelg?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 15.0% of workers, and education and training employed 8.8%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Glenelg?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

10.6% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.

How educated is Glenelg?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
