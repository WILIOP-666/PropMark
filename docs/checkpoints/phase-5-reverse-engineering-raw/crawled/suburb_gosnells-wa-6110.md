The median house price in Gosnells, WA is currently $778,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Gosnells has a 12-month growth rate of 21.6% for houses and 29.0% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $675,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $749,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $828,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $915,000 in Gosnells.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Gosnells is $650 per week. The median house rent has increased 8.3% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Gosnells is $645,000, which has increased 29.0% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $550,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $658,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $684,000 in Gosnells. The median rent for units and apartments in Gosnells is $600 per week and has increased 14.3% over the last 12 months.

Do people own or rent in Gosnells?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
