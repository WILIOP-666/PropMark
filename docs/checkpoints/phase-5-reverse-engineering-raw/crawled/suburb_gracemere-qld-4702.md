## Questions people ask about Gracemere
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Gracemere QLD 4702.
Is Gracemere family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
26.1% of Gracemere's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
3,206 family households lived in Gracemere; 43.0% were couple families and 22.1% were one-parent families.
48.5% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Gracemere?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Gracemere had 12,023 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.7% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Gracemere?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 95.9% of occupied private dwellings in Gracemere.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 1.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Gracemere?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
23.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
36.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
40.1% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Gracemere?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Gracemere had 2.8 people.
One-person households made up 19.2% of occupied homes. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 32.5%, while 30.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Gracemere?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
3.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
69.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
1.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Gracemere?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 32.8% of the total.
64.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Gracemere, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
50.7% were at the same address five years earlier, while 49.3% reported a different address.
7.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Gracemere?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
89.7% of residents used only English at home.
4.0% used a language other than English at home.
0.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Gracemere residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
7.9% of Gracemere's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 92.1% was born in Australia.
0.1% of residents were born in China.
0.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 89.9%.

Health care and social assistance employed 17.4% of workers, and education and training employed 7.4%.

How active is the workforce in Gracemere?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Gracemere show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+

10.7% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
11.1% reported asthma, 8.8% arthritis and 4.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Gracemere?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 1.9% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 52.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
