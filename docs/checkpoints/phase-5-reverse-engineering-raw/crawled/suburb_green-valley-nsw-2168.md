Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Green Valley?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Do people own or rent in Green Valley?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Green Valley?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Green Valley?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

68.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
