The median house price in Greensborough, VIC is currently $1,010,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Greensborough has a 12-month growth rate of -1.8% for houses and 1.4% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $760,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $925,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,160,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,180,000 in Greensborough.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Greensborough is $675 per week. The median house rent has increased 17.4% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Greensborough is $740,000, which has increased 1.4% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $668,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $812,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $860,000 in Greensborough. The median rent for units and apartments in Greensborough is $550 per week and has increased 4.8% over the last 12 months.

Is Greensborough family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.6% of Greensborough's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
6,043 family households lived in Greensborough; 49.3% were couple families and 13.6% were one-parent families.
38.5% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Greensborough?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Greensborough had 21,070 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.8% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Greensborough?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 84.9% of occupied private dwellings in Greensborough.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Greensborough?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
38.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
43.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
17.7% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Greensborough?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Greensborough had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 21.5% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 33.3%, while 28.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Greensborough?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
32.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
46.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
2.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Greensborough?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
4.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 32.7% of the total.
63.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Greensborough, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
69.5% were at the same address five years earlier, while 30.5% reported a different address.
21.5% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Greensborough?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

16.9% used a language other than English at home.
2.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.7% spoke Cantonese and 0.5% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Greensborough residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

2.1% of residents were born in China.
1.4% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 91.6%.

What work do people do in Greensborough?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 15.1% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 14.5%.
Health care and social assistance employed 14.9% of workers, and education and training employed 11.8%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Greensborough?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Greensborough show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+

9.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.4% reported asthma, 9.3% arthritis and 4.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Greensborough?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 11.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.6%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 27.7%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Greensborough?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $404 per week.

How does Greensborough compare with Greater Melbourne?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
