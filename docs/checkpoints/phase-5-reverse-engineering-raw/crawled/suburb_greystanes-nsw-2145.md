Is Greystanes family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

6,406 family households lived in Greystanes; 51.9% were couple families and 16.2% were one-parent families.
38.9% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.

Flats and apartments accounted for 1.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Greystanes?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
