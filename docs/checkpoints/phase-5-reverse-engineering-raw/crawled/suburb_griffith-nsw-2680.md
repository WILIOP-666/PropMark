The median house price in Griffith (NSW), NSW is currently $669,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Griffith (NSW) has a 12-month growth rate of 7.9% for houses and 7.7% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $745,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $600,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $769,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $805,000 in Griffith (NSW).
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Griffith (NSW) is $540 per week. The median house rent has increased 8.0% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Griffith (NSW) is $525,000, which has increased 7.7% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $461,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $582,000 in Griffith (NSW). The median rent for units and apartments in Griffith (NSW) is $425 per week and has increased 6.3% over the last 12 months.

Is Griffith family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
19.4% of Griffith's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
5,000 family households lived in Griffith; 44.0% were couple families and 16.4% were one-parent families.
39.8% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Griffith?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Griffith had 19,505 residents at the 2021 Census.
29.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.5% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Griffith?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 79.8% of occupied private dwellings in Griffith.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 5.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 12.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Griffith?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
28.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
39.1% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Griffith?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Griffith had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 26.5% of occupied homes. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 30.8%, while 27.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Griffith?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
3.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
73.0% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
0.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Griffith?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 35.3% of the total.
58.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Griffith, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
57.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 42.4% reported a different address.
27.2% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Rest of NSW rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Griffith?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
65.1% of residents used only English at home.
28.1% used a language other than English at home.
1.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.5% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Griffith residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
27.2% of Griffith's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 72.8% was born in Australia.
0.4% of residents were born in China.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Griffith?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 12.2% of workers, and education and training employed 6.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Griffith?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

61.1% reported no selected long-term health condition.
7.2% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.9% reported asthma, 8.0% arthritis and 4.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Griffith?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 5.4% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 38.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Griffith?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,483.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $300 per week.
