The median house price in Grovedale, VIC is currently $745,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Grovedale has a 12-month growth rate of 8.8% for houses and 5.4% for units.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Grovedale is $540,000, which has increased 5.4% since the same time last year.
