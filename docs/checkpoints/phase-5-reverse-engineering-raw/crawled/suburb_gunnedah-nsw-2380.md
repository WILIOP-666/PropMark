The median house price in Gunnedah, NSW is currently $580,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Gunnedah has a 12-month growth rate of 7.4% for houses and -5.9% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $650,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $393,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $510,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $700,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $856,000 in Gunnedah.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Gunnedah is $550 per week. The median house rent has increased 4.8% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Gunnedah is $398,000, which has decreased 5.9% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $355,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $488,000 in Gunnedah. The median rent for units and apartments in Gunnedah is $425 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Gunnedah
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Gunnedah NSW 2380.
Is Gunnedah family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
22.7% of Gunnedah's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
2,610 family households lived in Gunnedah; 41.5% were couple families and 18.0% were one-parent families.
42.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Gunnedah?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Gunnedah had 10,359 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.8% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Gunnedah?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 88.0% of occupied private dwellings in Gunnedah.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 9.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Gunnedah?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
33.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
34.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
32.0% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Gunnedah?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Gunnedah had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 29.4% of occupied homes. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 33.5%, while 23.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Gunnedah?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
7.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
65.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
2.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Gunnedah?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.
One-car households formed 35.5% of the total.
58.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Gunnedah, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

5.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Gunnedah?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

3.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.

Where were Gunnedah residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.2% of residents were born in China.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

8.2% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.8% reported asthma, 9.7% arthritis and 5.3% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Gunnedah?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.3%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 46.1%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
