## Questions people ask about Gymea
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Gymea NSW 2227.
Is Gymea family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.8% of Gymea's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
2,329 family households lived in Gymea; 45.9% were couple families and 16.0% were one-parent families.
39.3% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Gymea?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Gymea had 8,219 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.7% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Gymea?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 44.8% of occupied private dwellings in Gymea.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 22.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 31.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Gymea?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
33.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
34.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
32.6% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Gymea?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Gymea had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 26.8% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 33.0%, while 23.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Gymea?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
36.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
38.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
3.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Gymea?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.0% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 43.9% of the total.
48.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Gymea, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
57.4% were at the same address five years earlier, while 42.6% reported a different address.
20.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Gymea?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
82.3% of residents used only English at home.
14.7% used a language other than English at home.
1.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Gymea residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
20.9% of Gymea's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 79.1% was born in Australia.
1.5% of residents were born in China.
0.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 90.2%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

Australian ancestry made up 34.5% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 4.5% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Gymea?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
25.4% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 16.2% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 16.3%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.7% of workers, and education and training employed 8.2%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Gymea?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

37.3% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 18.8% worked part time.
Unemployment was 3.4%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Gymea show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+

7.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.6% reported asthma, 8.7% arthritis and 4.0% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Gymea?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
27.7% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 8.3% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 18.7%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 32.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Gymea?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,600.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $500 per week.

### Is Gymea expensive?
The median house price is $1,860,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,060,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Gymea good for families?
The Census counted 2,329 local families, and 18.8% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Gymea?
