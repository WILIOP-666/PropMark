The median house price in Gympie, QLD is currently $719,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Gympie has a 12-month growth rate of 16.0% for houses and 19.2% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $571,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $695,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $775,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $840,000 in Gympie.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Gympie is $575 per week. The median house rent has increased 9.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Gympie is $590,000, which has increased 19.2% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $424,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $575,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $620,000 in Gympie. The median rent for units and apartments in Gympie is $425 per week and has increased 13.3% over the last 12 months.

Is Gympie family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.4% of Gympie's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
2,871 family households lived in Gympie; 31.6% were couple families and 27.9% were one-parent families.
38.8% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Gympie?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Gympie had 11,355 residents at the 2021 Census.
22.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
36.2% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Gympie?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Gympie?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
33.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.

38.8% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Gympie?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Gympie?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Gympie?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 42.9% of the total.
48.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Gympie, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Gympie?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
