## Questions people ask about Hallam
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Hallam VIC 3803.
Is Hallam family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
19.6% of Hallam's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
2,857 family households lived in Hallam; 50.6% were couple families and 17.0% were one-parent families.
39.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Hallam?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Hallam had 11,355 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
28.3% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Hallam?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 77.5% of occupied private dwellings in Hallam.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 20.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Hallam?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
30.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
42.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
26.9% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Hallam?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Hallam had 3.0 people.
One-person households made up 19.4% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 29.0%, while 33.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Hallam?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
14.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
62.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
2.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Hallam?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
4.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 34.5% of the total.
61.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Hallam, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
63.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 36.2% reported a different address.
53.2% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Hallam?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
37.1% of residents used only English at home.
56.0% used a language other than English at home.
1.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.6% spoke Cantonese and 1.4% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Hallam residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
53.2% of Hallam's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 46.8% was born in Australia.
1.5% of residents were born in China.
4.9% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 76.6%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Hallam?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 14.2% of ancestry responses in Hallam.
Australian ancestry made up 13.7% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 4.2% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Hallam?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
13.9% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
Managers accounted for 9.3% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.9%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.9% of workers, and education and training employed 5.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Hallam?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 54.3%, compared with 64.1% across Greater Melbourne.
29.9% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 16.8% worked part time.
Unemployment was 7.8%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Melbourne.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Hallam show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
62.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
7.2% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.6% reported asthma, 7.4% arthritis and 7.1% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Hallam?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
23.6% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 7.4% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 18.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 31.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Hallam?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,489 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,700.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $361 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Hallam compare with Greater Melbourne?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+

Outright ownership was 30.2% locally and 30.7% across Greater Melbourne. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
Renting accounted for 26.9% in Hallam, against 31.1% across Greater Melbourne.
