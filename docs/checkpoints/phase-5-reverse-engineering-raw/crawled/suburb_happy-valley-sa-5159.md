## Questions people ask about Happy Valley
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Happy Valley SA 5159.
Is Happy Valley family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.0% of Happy Valley's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Adelaide.
3,395 family households lived in Happy Valley; 41.7% were couple families and 14.9% were one-parent families.
34.3% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Adelaide average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Happy Valley?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Happy Valley had 11,420 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
35.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Happy Valley?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 90.0% of occupied private dwellings in Happy Valley.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Happy Valley?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
37.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
51.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
11.6% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Happy Valley?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Happy Valley had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 23.9% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Greater Adelaide average.
Two-person households represented 36.9%, while 22.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Happy Valley?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
8.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Adelaide average.
68.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
2.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Happy Valley?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
One-car households formed 33.2% of the total.
63.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Happy Valley, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
70.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 29.4% reported a different address.
18.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Happy Valley?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
91.0% of residents used only English at home.
6.5% used a language other than English at home.
0.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Happy Valley residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
18.6% of Happy Valley's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 81.4% was born in Australia.
0.4% of residents were born in China.
0.6% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 93.1%.

English ancestry accounted for 49.0% of ancestry responses in Happy Valley.
Australian ancestry made up 39.5% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 1.2% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Happy Valley?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
19.9% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat below the Greater Adelaide average.
Managers accounted for 10.8% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 15.7%.
Health care and social assistance employed 18.3% of workers, and education and training employed 10.0%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Happy Valley?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 62.8%, compared with 61.7% across Greater Adelaide.
34.3% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 22.7% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.1%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Adelaide.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Happy Valley show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
56.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
10.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.1% reported asthma, 10.9% arthritis and 5.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Happy Valley?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
22.1% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 4.8% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 17.6%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 42.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Happy Valley?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $360 per week.
