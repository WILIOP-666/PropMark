## Questions people ask about Harrisdale

Is Harrisdale family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
29.0% of Harrisdale's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
3,254 family households lived in Harrisdale; 64.4% were couple families and 11.1% were one-parent families.
59.9% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Harrisdale?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Harrisdale had 11,667 residents at the 2021 Census.
38.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
11.9% were 55 or older. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Harrisdale?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 98.5% of occupied private dwellings in Harrisdale.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 1.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Harrisdale?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
11.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
68.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
20.3% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Harrisdale?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Harrisdale?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

3.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Harrisdale?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 25.4% of the total.
73.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Harrisdale, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How active is the workforce in Harrisdale?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

5.0% reported asthma, 3.0% arthritis and 3.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Harrisdale?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
