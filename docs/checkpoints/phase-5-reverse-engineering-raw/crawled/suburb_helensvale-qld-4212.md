The median house price in Helensvale, QLD is currently $1,390,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Helensvale has a 12-month growth rate of 6.9% for houses and 9.6% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $775,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,080,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,380,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,950,000 in Helensvale.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Helensvale is $804,000, which has increased 9.6% since the same time last year. Is Helensvale family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

37.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.

Helensvale had 18,949 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Helensvale?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 14.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Helensvale?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
