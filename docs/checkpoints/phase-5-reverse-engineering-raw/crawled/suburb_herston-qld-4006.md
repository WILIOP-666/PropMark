Is Herston family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

38.9% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.

What is the age profile of Herston?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Herston had 2,311 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Herston?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 37.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Herston?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

23.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Herston?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

9.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 11.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Herston?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
