Is Highett family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

40.1% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Highett?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Highett had 12,016 residents at the 2021 Census.
29.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.1% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Highett?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 28.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 17.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Highett?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

41.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
28.0% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Highett?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Highett?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
39.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.

3.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Highett?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 45.1% of the total.
48.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Highett, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

2.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.8% spoke Cantonese and 0.7% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Highett?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
