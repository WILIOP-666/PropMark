The median house price in Highton, VIC is currently $879,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Highton has a 12-month growth rate of 0.4% for houses and 0.8% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $660,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $812,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $981,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,160,000 in Highton.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Highton is $575 per week. The median house rent has increased 4.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Highton is $520,000, which has increased 0.8% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $390,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $515,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $658,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $780,000 in Highton. The median rent for units and apartments in Highton is $450 per week and has increased 2.7% over the last 12 months.
