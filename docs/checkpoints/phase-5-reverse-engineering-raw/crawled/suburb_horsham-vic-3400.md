The median house price in Horsham, VIC is currently $457,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Horsham has a 12-month growth rate of 11.4% for houses and 0.1% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $355,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $349,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $433,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $611,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $712,000 in Horsham.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Horsham is $450 per week. The median house rent has increased 12.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Horsham is $390,000, which has increased 0.1% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $306,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $345,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $499,000 in Horsham. The median rent for units and apartments in Horsham is $350 per week and has increased 7.7% over the last 12 months.
