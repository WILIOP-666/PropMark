Is Horsley Park family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

474 family households lived in Horsley Park; 51.9% were couple families and 12.9% were one-parent families.
29.1% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Horsley Park?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Horsley Park had 1,790 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
35.8% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Horsley Park?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 97.1% of occupied private dwellings in Horsley Park.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 1.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Horsley Park?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
54.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
27.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
18.0% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Horsley Park?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 31.4%, while 38.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Horsley Park?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
32.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
37.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
0.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Horsley Park?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 18.8% of the total.
77.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Horsley Park, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Horsley Park?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

0.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.4% spoke Cantonese and 0.7% spoke Hindi.

Where were Horsley Park residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Horsley Park?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
