What is the age profile of Hughesdale?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Hughesdale had 7,563 residents at the 2021 Census.

26.1% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Flats and apartments accounted for 15.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Hughesdale?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

32.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Hughesdale?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

How do people get to work in Hughesdale?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

5.9% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Hughesdale?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Hughesdale, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

The median house price is $1,510,000, while units and apartments sit at $645,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Hughesdale good for families?
The Census counted 2,072 local families, and 15.6% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Hughesdale?
