Is Illawong family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

2,211 family households lived in Illawong; 51.5% were couple families and 9.2% were one-parent families.
31.3% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Illawong?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Illawong had 7,427 residents at the 2021 Census.
21.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
36.3% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Illawong?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 92.7% of occupied private dwellings in Illawong.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 5.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Illawong?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
50.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
41.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
8.1% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Illawong?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Illawong?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

40.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
1.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Illawong?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 17.5% of the total.
81.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Illawong, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

1.9% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.8% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.

Where were Illawong residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

6.6% reported asthma, 8.3% arthritis and 4.7% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Illawong?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
