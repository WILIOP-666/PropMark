## Questions people ask about Inala
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Inala QLD 4077.
Is Inala family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
23.9% of Inala's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
3,618 family households lived in Inala; 39.6% were couple families and 35.1% were one-parent families.
45.3% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Inala?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Inala had 15,273 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
25.4% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Inala?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 89.0% of occupied private dwellings in Inala.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 6.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 3.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Inala?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
20.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
21.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
57.5% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Inala?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Inala had 2.9 people.
One-person households made up 25.1% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
Two-person households represented 25.0%, while 32.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Inala?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
5.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
64.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
3.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Inala?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
14.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 39.3% of the total.
45.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Inala, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
63.0% were at the same address five years earlier, while 37.0% reported a different address.
44.5% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Inala?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
38.3% of residents used only English at home.
52.6% used a language other than English at home.
0.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Inala residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
44.5% of Inala's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 55.5% was born in Australia.
0.3% of residents were born in China.
0.4% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 73.9%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Inala?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 16.2% of ancestry responses in Inala.
Australian ancestry made up 16.4% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 3.4% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Inala?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
9.0% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
Managers accounted for 4.4% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 8.4%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.4% of workers, and education and training employed 4.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Inala?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 44.0%, compared with 64.5% across Greater Brisbane.
18.9% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 14.4% worked part time.
Unemployment was 13.6%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Brisbane.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Inala show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
59.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
7.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.7% reported asthma, 7.5% arthritis and 6.5% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Inala?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
15.0% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 3.3% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 37.0%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Inala?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $998 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,300.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $250 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Inala compare with Greater Brisbane?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 5.5% in Inala, compared with 17.4% across Greater Brisbane.
Outright ownership was 20.8% locally and 26.7% across Greater Brisbane. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Renting accounted for 57.5% in Inala, against 36.2% across Greater Brisbane.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.
