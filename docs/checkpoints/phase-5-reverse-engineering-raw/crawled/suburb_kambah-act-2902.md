The median house price in Kambah, ACT is currently $925,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Kambah has a 12-month growth rate of 3.9% for houses and -8.7% for units.
Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $850,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,070,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,320,000 in Kambah.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Kambah is $675 per week. The median house rent has increased 3.8% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Kambah is $630,000, which has decreased 8.7% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $553,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $675,000 in Kambah. The median rent for units and apartments in Kambah is $625 per week and has increased 4.2% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Kambah

Is Kambah family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.8% of Kambah's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Australian Capital Territory average.
4,402 family households lived in Kambah; 42.7% were couple families and 17.3% were one-parent families.
37.5% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Australian Capital Territory average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Kambah?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kambah had 15,670 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.2% were 55 or older. That is well above the Australian Capital Territory average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kambah?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 84.7% of occupied private dwellings in Kambah.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 13.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kambah?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
36.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Australian Capital Territory average.
41.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
21.6% rented their home. That is well below the Australian Capital Territory average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kambah?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Kambah had 2.6 people.

Two-person households represented 34.5%, while 25.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kambah?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
10.2% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Australian Capital Territory average.
65.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Australian Capital Territory average.
2.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kambah?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Australian Capital Territory average.
One-car households formed 33.4% of the total.
62.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kambah, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

20.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Australian Capital Territory average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

14.0% used a language other than English at home.
0.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.4% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.

Where were Kambah residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.5% of residents were born in China.

How active is the workforce in Kambah?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

10.9% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.1% reported asthma, 10.4% arthritis and 5.0% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Kambah?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 11.9% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.3%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 26.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
