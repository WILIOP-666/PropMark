Is Keilor East family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

34.8% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.

Keilor East had 15,078 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Keilor East?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Keilor East?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.

How educated is Keilor East?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
