## Questions people ask about Kiama

Is Kiama family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
14.5% of Kiama's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
2,298 family households lived in Kiama; 33.6% were couple families and 11.6% were one-parent families.
28.0% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Kiama?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kiama had 7,904 residents at the 2021 Census.
19.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
47.2% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kiama?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 55.5% of occupied private dwellings in Kiama.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 24.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 19.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kiama?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
48.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
27.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
24.0% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kiama?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Kiama had 2.3 people.
One-person households made up 28.4% of occupied homes. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 41.7%, while 17.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kiama?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
27.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
49.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kiama?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.
One-car households formed 39.8% of the total.
55.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kiama, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
55.5% were at the same address five years earlier, while 44.5% reported a different address.
18.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Kiama?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
89.0% of residents used only English at home.
5.6% used a language other than English at home.
0.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.4% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Kiama residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
18.3% of Kiama's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 81.7% was born in Australia.
0.6% of residents were born in China.
0.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 90.6%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

Australian ancestry made up 36.7% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 1.4% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Kiama?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
30.4% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Managers accounted for 15.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 10.6%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.9% of workers, and education and training employed 12.7%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Kiama?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

25.9% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 17.6% worked part time.
Unemployment was 3.9%, compared with 4.6% across Rest of NSW.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Kiama show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
55.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
7.9% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.9% reported asthma, 13.1% arthritis and 4.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Kiama?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 9.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 17.1%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 29.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Kiama?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,167.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $478 per week.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Kiama
### Is Kiama expensive?
The median house price is $1,540,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,000,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Kiama good for families?
The Census counted 2,298 local families, and 14.5% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Kiama?
