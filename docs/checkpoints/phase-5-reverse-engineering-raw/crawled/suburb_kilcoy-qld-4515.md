## Questions people ask about Kilcoy
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Kilcoy QLD 4515.
Is Kilcoy family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.9% of Kilcoy's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
463 family households lived in Kilcoy; 44.1% were couple families and 16.4% were one-parent families.
42.5% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Kilcoy?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kilcoy had 1,996 residents at the 2021 Census.
31.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.3% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kilcoy?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 95.4% of occupied private dwellings in Kilcoy.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 2.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kilcoy?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
35.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
31.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
33.9% rented their home. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kilcoy?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Kilcoy had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 25.6% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
Two-person households represented 32.3%, while 26.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kilcoy?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
4.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
64.0% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
0.9% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kilcoy?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 37.2% of the total.
58.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kilcoy, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
48.9% were at the same address five years earlier, while 51.1% reported a different address.
28.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Kilcoy?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
65.6% of residents used only English at home.
22.3% used a language other than English at home.
0.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Kilcoy residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
28.3% of Kilcoy's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 71.7% was born in Australia.
0.3% of residents were born in China.
0.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 67.6%.

Australian ancestry made up 30.8% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 1.1% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Kilcoy?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 8.2% of workers, and education and training employed 6.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Kilcoy?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

8.7% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.9% reported asthma, 9.5% arthritis and 5.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Kilcoy?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 2.4% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 9.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 35.1%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Kilcoy?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
