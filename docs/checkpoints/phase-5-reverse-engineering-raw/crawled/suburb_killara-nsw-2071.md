Is Killara family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

3,004 family households lived in Killara; 53.6% were couple families and 13.3% were one-parent families.
39.2% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Killara?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Killara had 10,620 residents at the 2021 Census.
21.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Killara?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 54.6% of occupied private dwellings in Killara.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 2.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 42.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Killara?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
40.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
33.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
26.2% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Killara?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Killara?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
54.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
25.0% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
3.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Killara?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 39.8% of the total.
55.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Killara, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

46.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.

What languages are spoken in Killara?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

17.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 7.8% spoke Cantonese and 1.1% spoke Hindi.

Where were Killara residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Killara?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
