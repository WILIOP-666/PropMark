## Questions people ask about Kilsyth

Is Kilsyth family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.7% of Kilsyth's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
3,266 family households lived in Kilsyth; 42.1% were couple families and 19.0% were one-parent families.
38.7% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Kilsyth?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kilsyth had 11,699 residents at the 2021 Census.
29.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kilsyth?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 84.7% of occupied private dwellings in Kilsyth.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 14.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kilsyth?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
33.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
45.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
20.7% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kilsyth?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Kilsyth had 2.4 people.

Two-person households represented 34.1%, while 22.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kilsyth?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
19.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
59.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
1.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kilsyth?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
4.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 35.7% of the total.
59.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kilsyth, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

0.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Kilsyth?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
