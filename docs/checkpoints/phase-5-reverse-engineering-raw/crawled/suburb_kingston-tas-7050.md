## Questions people ask about Kingston

Is Kingston family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.7% of Kingston's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
3,332 family households lived in Kingston; 40.9% were couple families and 18.0% were one-parent families.
39.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Hobart average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Kingston?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kingston had 12,288 residents at the 2021 Census.
31.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
28.1% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Hobart average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kingston?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 89.2% of occupied private dwellings in Kingston.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kingston?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
34.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
36.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
29.8% rented their home. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Hobart.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kingston?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 27.3% of occupied homes. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Hobart.
Two-person households represented 32.5%, while 23.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kingston?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
8.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Hobart.
62.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
5.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kingston?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
One-car households formed 38.9% of the total.
55.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kingston, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

18.6% used a language other than English at home.
7.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Kingston residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

6.5% of residents were born in China.
1.1% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 81.5%.

Health care and social assistance employed 15.9% of workers, and education and training employed 10.8%.

How active is the workforce in Kingston?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Kingston?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.

The median house price is $790,000, while units and apartments sit at $645,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.

The Census counted 3,332 local families, and 17.7% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
