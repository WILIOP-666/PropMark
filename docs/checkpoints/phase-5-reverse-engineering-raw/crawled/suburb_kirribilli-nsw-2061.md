Is Kirribilli family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

What is the age profile of Kirribilli?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kirribilli had 3,629 residents at the 2021 Census.
38.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

What kind of homes are in Kirribilli?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 87.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kirribilli?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

11.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
65.2% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kirribilli?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kirribilli?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

5.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kirribilli?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 52.4% of the total.
16.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kirribilli, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
