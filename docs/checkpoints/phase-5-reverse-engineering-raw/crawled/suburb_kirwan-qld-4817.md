The median house price in Kirwan, QLD is currently $675,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Kirwan has a 12-month growth rate of 12.5% for houses and 22.7% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $661,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $625,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $710,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $799,000 in Kirwan.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Kirwan is $575 per week. The median house rent has increased 9.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Kirwan is $472,000, which has increased 22.7% since the same time last year. The median rent for units and apartments in Kirwan is $400 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.
