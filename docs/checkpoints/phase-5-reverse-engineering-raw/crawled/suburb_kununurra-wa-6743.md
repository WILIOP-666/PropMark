Is Kununurra family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

1,194 family households lived in Kununurra; 39.7% were couple families and 19.3% were one-parent families.
46.4% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of WA average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Kununurra?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kununurra had 5,494 residents at the 2021 Census.
32.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
19.4% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of WA average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kununurra?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 64.2% of occupied private dwellings in Kununurra.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 14.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 7.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kununurra?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
15.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Rest of WA rate.
19.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
65.3% rented their home. That is about twice the Rest of WA rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kununurra?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 32.6%, while 26.3% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kununurra?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.9% used only a train, tram or bus, while 11.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kununurra?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 34.4% of the total.
55.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kununurra, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

0.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Kununurra?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
