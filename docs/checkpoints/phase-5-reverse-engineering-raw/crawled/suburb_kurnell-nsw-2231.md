Is Kurnell family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

676 family households lived in Kurnell; 53.6% were couple families and 16.4% were one-parent families.
44.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Kurnell?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kurnell had 2,528 residents at the 2021 Census.
23.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
26.0% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kurnell?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 95.3% of occupied private dwellings in Kurnell.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 2.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kurnell?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
34.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
48.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
16.9% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kurnell?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 28.3%, while 42.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kurnell?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

51.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
0.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kurnell?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 23.0% of the total.
74.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kurnell, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

11.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

The median house price is $1,920,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,120,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
