Is Kurrajong family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

925 family households lived in Kurrajong; 46.8% were couple families and 9.6% were one-parent families.
30.5% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.

What is the age profile of Kurrajong?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Kurrajong had 3,113 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
36.2% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Kurrajong?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 1.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Kurrajong?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
44.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
45.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
10.7% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Kurrajong?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 36.4%, while 32.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Kurrajong?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

46.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
0.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Kurrajong?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
0.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 18.9% of the total.
79.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Kurrajong, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

What languages are spoken in Kurrajong?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

0.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.

Where were Kurrajong residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

Health care and social assistance employed 10.8% of workers, and education and training employed 12.1%.

How active is the workforce in Kurrajong?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

7.0% reported asthma, 10.1% arthritis and 4.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Kurrajong?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Kurrajong?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
