## Questions people ask about Labrador
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Labrador QLD 4215.
Is Labrador family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
12.5% of Labrador's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
4,595 family households lived in Labrador; 28.1% were couple families and 24.3% were one-parent families.
31.9% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Labrador?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Labrador had 18,643 residents at the 2021 Census.
27.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
36.8% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Labrador?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 19.0% of occupied private dwellings in Labrador.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 48.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 31.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Labrador?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
25.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
27.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
47.8% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Labrador?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Labrador had 2.0 people.
One-person households made up 38.2% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 37.0%, while 11.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Labrador?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
10.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
62.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
2.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Labrador?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
9.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 51.3% of the total.
39.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Labrador, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
45.9% were at the same address five years earlier, while 54.1% reported a different address.
33.5% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Rest of Qld rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Labrador?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
74.2% of residents used only English at home.
17.5% used a language other than English at home.
1.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.4% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Labrador residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
33.5% of Labrador's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 66.5% was born in Australia.
1.2% of residents were born in China.
1.6% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 77.6%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Labrador?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 17.4% of workers, and education and training employed 7.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Labrador?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

11.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.9% reported asthma, 11.5% arthritis and 4.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Labrador?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 35.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Labrador?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
