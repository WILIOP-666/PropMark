## Questions people ask about Lakemba

Is Lakemba family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
24.8% of Lakemba's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
3,708 family households lived in Lakemba; 60.7% were couple families and 14.3% were one-parent families.
53.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Lakemba?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Lakemba had 17,092 residents at the 2021 Census.
36.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
18.6% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Lakemba?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 25.6% of occupied private dwellings in Lakemba.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 69.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Lakemba?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
20.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
19.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
60.1% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Lakemba?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Lakemba had 3.1 people.
One-person households made up 19.5% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 22.7%, while 39.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Lakemba?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
21.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
40.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
8.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Lakemba?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
15.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 51.6% of the total.
32.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Lakemba, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
53.4% were at the same address five years earlier, while 46.6% reported a different address.
59.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Lakemba?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
16.6% of residents used only English at home.
72.9% used a language other than English at home.
1.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 2.3% spoke Cantonese and 1.7% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Lakemba residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
59.9% of Lakemba's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 40.1% was born in Australia.
1.8% of residents were born in China.
8.8% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 63.3%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

Australian ancestry made up 8.3% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 4.9% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Lakemba?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
19.1% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 6.8% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 10.5%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.8% of workers, and education and training employed 6.0%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Lakemba?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 42.8%, compared with 60.0% across Greater Sydney.
15.9% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 13.7% worked part time.
Unemployment was 13.9%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Lakemba show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
69.8% reported no selected long-term health condition.
4.0% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
4.2% reported asthma, 4.7% arthritis and 5.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Lakemba?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
31.2% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 18.1% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.5%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 13.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Lakemba?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $350 per week.

How does Lakemba compare with Greater Sydney?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
