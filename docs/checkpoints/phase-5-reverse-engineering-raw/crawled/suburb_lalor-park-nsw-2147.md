Do people own or rent in Lalor Park?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Lalor Park?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

2.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Lalor Park?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

47.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Lalor Park, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Lalor Park?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
