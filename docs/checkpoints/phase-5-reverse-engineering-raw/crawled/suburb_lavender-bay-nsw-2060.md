Is Lavender Bay family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

229 family households lived in Lavender Bay; 24.9% were couple families and 8.3% were one-parent families.

What is the age profile of Lavender Bay?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Lavender Bay had 870 residents at the 2021 Census.
35.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
36.2% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Lavender Bay?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 67.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Lavender Bay?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

14.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
58.6% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Lavender Bay?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Lavender Bay?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

5.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 6.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Lavender Bay?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
26.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 50.6% of the total.
23.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Lavender Bay, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Lavender Bay?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
