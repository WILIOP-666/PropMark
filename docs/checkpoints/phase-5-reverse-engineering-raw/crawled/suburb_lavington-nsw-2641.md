The median house price in Lavington, NSW is currently $655,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Lavington has a 12-month growth rate of 12.9% for houses and 27.5% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $538,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $626,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $699,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $776,000 in Lavington.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Lavington is $525 per week. The median house rent has increased 5.0% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Lavington is $440,000, which has increased 27.5% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $270,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $397,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $490,000 in Lavington. The median rent for units and apartments in Lavington is $375 per week and has increased 7.1% over the last 12 months.

Is Lavington family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

37.2% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Lavington?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Lavington had 13,073 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.0% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Lavington?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 22.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Lavington?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

27.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
41.1% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Lavington?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Lavington?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Lavington?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

46.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
