The median house price in Leeming, WA is currently $1,510,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Leeming has a 12-month growth rate of 19.5% for houses and 23.4% for units.
Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,200,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,500,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,640,000 in Leeming.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Leeming is $830,000, which has increased 23.4% since the same time last year. ## Questions people ask about Leeming
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Leeming WA 6149.
Is Leeming family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.0% of Leeming's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
3,273 family households lived in Leeming; 50.2% were couple families and 10.5% were one-parent families.
34.1% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Leeming?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Leeming had 10,883 residents at the 2021 Census.
22.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.7% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Leeming?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 93.4% of occupied private dwellings in Leeming.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 6.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Leeming?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
46.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
41.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
12.1% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Leeming?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Leeming had 2.8 people.
One-person households made up 14.9% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Two-person households represented 35.6%, while 31.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Leeming?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
9.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
60.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
4.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Leeming?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.0% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
One-car households formed 23.5% of the total.
74.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Leeming, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

36.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

20.7% used a language other than English at home.
6.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 2.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.

How active is the workforce in Leeming?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

7.0% reported asthma, 7.3% arthritis and 4.5% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Leeming?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 11.6% of qualification responses.

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Leeming?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
