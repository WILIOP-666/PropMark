Is Leichhardt family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

4,067 family households lived in Leichhardt; 45.4% were couple families and 14.5% were one-parent families.
42.9% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Leichhardt?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Leichhardt had 15,158 residents at the 2021 Census.
34.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.8% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Leichhardt?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 36.6% of occupied private dwellings in Leichhardt.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 33.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 29.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Leichhardt?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
24.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
34.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
41.1% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Leichhardt?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Leichhardt had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 26.3% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 34.6%, while 21.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Leichhardt?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
55.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
20.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
3.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Leichhardt?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
13.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 53.4% of the total.
32.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Leichhardt, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
51.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 48.2% reported a different address.
28.8% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Leichhardt?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
76.3% of residents used only English at home.
19.7% used a language other than English at home.
1.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Leichhardt residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.7% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 86.9%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Leichhardt?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 12.3% of workers, and education and training employed 11.0%.

How active is the workforce in Leichhardt?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

7.8% reported asthma, 5.7% arthritis and 2.7% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Leichhardt?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
