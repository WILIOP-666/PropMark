## Questions people ask about Lobethal

Is Lobethal family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.7% of Lobethal's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Adelaide average.
704 family households lived in Lobethal; 41.9% were couple families and 14.5% were one-parent families.
36.4% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Adelaide.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Lobethal?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Lobethal had 2,534 residents at the 2021 Census.
23.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
35.8% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Lobethal?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 8.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Lobethal?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
34.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Adelaide average.
50.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
15.4% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Lobethal?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 35.8%, while 22.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Lobethal?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Lobethal?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
One-car households formed 31.4% of the total.
65.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Lobethal, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

0.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.

Where were Lobethal residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How active is the workforce in Lobethal?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

10.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.

How educated is Lobethal?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
