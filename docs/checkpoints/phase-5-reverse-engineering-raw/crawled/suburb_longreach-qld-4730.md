What is the age profile of Longreach?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Longreach had 3,124 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Longreach?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Longreach?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

24.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
42.4% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Longreach?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Longreach?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 10.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Longreach?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

54.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Longreach, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
