Is Mannum family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

What is the age profile of Mannum?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Mannum?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 0.4% of occupied private dwellings.

Do people own or rent in Mannum?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Mannum?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.7% walked or cycled.

How many cars do households have in Mannum?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
