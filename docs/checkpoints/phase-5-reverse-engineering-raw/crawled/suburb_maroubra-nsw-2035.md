## Questions people ask about Maroubra
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Maroubra NSW 2035.
Is Maroubra family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
16.0% of Maroubra's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
8,154 family households lived in Maroubra; 44.6% were couple families and 15.3% were one-parent families.
35.6% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Maroubra?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Maroubra had 30,722 residents at the 2021 Census.
31.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Maroubra?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 29.6% of occupied private dwellings in Maroubra.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 20.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 48.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Maroubra?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
30.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
25.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
44.1% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Maroubra?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Maroubra had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 27.9% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 32.9%, while 23.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Maroubra?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
41.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
31.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
4.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Maroubra?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
15.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 45.5% of the total.
39.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Maroubra, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
58.2% were at the same address five years earlier, while 41.8% reported a different address.
42.1% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Maroubra?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
59.9% of residents used only English at home.
34.8% used a language other than English at home.
3.9% spoke Mandarin at home, 3.7% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Maroubra residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
42.1% of Maroubra's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 57.9% was born in Australia.
3.4% of residents were born in China.
0.8% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 79.1%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Maroubra?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 22.6% of ancestry responses in Maroubra.
Australian ancestry made up 19.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 12.6% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Maroubra?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
33.9% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 17.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.6%.
Health care and social assistance employed 13.1% of workers, and education and training employed 10.5%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Maroubra?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 61.2%, compared with 60.0% across Greater Sydney.
34.7% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 17.6% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.6%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Maroubra show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
66.5% reported no selected long-term health condition.
6.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.1% reported asthma, 6.9% arthritis and 3.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Maroubra?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
37.0% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 16.8% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.7%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 17.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Maroubra?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $3,000.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $540 per week.

How does Maroubra compare with Greater Sydney?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
