Is Marrickville family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Marrickville had 26,570 residents at the 2021 Census.

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Marrickville?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How many cars do households have in Marrickville?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
