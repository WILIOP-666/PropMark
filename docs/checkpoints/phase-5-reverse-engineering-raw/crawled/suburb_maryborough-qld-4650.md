Is Maryborough family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
15.4% of Maryborough's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
3,908 family households lived in Maryborough; 27.1% were couple families and 26.6% were one-parent families.
31.8% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Maryborough?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Maryborough had 15,287 residents at the 2021 Census.

42.8% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Maryborough?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 8.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 5.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Maryborough?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

35.4% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Maryborough?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Maryborough?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 6.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Maryborough?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 44.1% of the total.
43.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Maryborough, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
