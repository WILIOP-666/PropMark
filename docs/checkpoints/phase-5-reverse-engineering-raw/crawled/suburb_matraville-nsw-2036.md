The median house price in Matraville, NSW is currently $2,790,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Matraville has a 12-month growth rate of 6.4% for houses and -4.0% for units.

## Questions people ask about Matraville

Is Matraville family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.3% of Matraville's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
2,663 family households lived in Matraville; 50.8% were couple families and 18.7% were one-parent families.
37.4% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Matraville?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Matraville had 9,925 residents at the 2021 Census.
23.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Matraville?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 47.6% of occupied private dwellings in Matraville.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 30.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 21.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Matraville?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
31.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
35.6% rented their home. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Matraville?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Matraville had 2.7 people.
One-person households made up 22.9% of occupied homes. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
Two-person households represented 28.5%, while 30.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Matraville?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
33.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
39.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
3.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Matraville?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
11.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
One-car households formed 37.2% of the total.
51.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Matraville, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
65.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 34.2% reported a different address.
34.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Matraville?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
65.4% of residents used only English at home.
28.9% used a language other than English at home.
2.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 3.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Matraville residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
34.6% of Matraville's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 65.4% was born in Australia.
2.5% of residents were born in China.
0.9% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 85.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

Australian ancestry made up 22.9% of responses.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Matraville?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 13.1% of workers, and education and training employed 11.0%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Matraville?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

6.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.9% reported asthma, 7.8% arthritis and 5.0% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Matraville?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 11.2% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 15.1%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 28.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Matraville?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
