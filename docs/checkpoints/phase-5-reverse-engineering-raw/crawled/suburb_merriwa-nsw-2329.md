Is Merriwa family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

457 family households lived in Merriwa; 37.2% were couple families and 15.3% were one-parent families.
37.6% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Merriwa?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Merriwa had 1,825 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
37.9% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Merriwa?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 3.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Merriwa?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
46.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
27.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
25.8% rented their home. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Merriwa?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 36.9%, while 21.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Merriwa?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
14.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
55.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
0.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 7.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Merriwa?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
4.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 34.5% of the total.
61.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Merriwa, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

6.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

0.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Merriwa?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
