Is Merrylands family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

7,552 family households lived in Merrylands; 53.4% were couple families and 18.5% were one-parent families.
43.3% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Merrylands?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Merrylands had 32,472 residents at the 2021 Census.
33.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Merrylands?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 13.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 36.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Merrylands?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

27.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
49.0% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Merrylands?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Merrylands?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

6.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Merrylands?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

45.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Merrylands, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Merrylands?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
