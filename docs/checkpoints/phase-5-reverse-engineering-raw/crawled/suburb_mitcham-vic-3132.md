Is Mitcham family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

38.6% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.

Mitcham had 16,795 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Mitcham?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 4.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Mitcham?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
