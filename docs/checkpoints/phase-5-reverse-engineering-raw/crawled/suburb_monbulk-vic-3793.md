Do people own or rent in Monbulk?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Monbulk?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Monbulk?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

70.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Monbulk, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
