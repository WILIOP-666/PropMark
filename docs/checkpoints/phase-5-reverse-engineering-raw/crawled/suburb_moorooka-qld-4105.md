Is Moorooka family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.3% of Moorooka's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
2,798 family households lived in Moorooka; 42.2% were couple families and 14.9% were one-parent families.
39.5% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Moorooka?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Moorooka had 10,783 residents at the 2021 Census.
34.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.9% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Moorooka?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 60.5% of occupied private dwellings in Moorooka.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 15.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 23.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Moorooka?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
22.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
35.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
41.6% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Moorooka?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Moorooka?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

50.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
7.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Moorooka?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
One-car households formed 43.3% of the total.
48.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Moorooka, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

18.1% used a language other than English at home.
0.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.6% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Moorooka?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 14.1% of qualification responses.
