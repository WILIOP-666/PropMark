Is Moranbah family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
24.5% of Moranbah's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
2,016 family households lived in Moranbah; 55.8% were couple families and 13.1% were one-parent families.
55.9% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Moranbah?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Moranbah had 9,425 residents at the 2021 Census.
38.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
11.1% were 55 or older. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Moranbah?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 81.7% of occupied private dwellings in Moranbah.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 16.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Moranbah?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
8.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
19.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
71.4% rented their home. That is about twice the Rest of Qld rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Moranbah?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 21.6% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 27.9%, while 33.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Moranbah?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
3.3% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
67.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
2.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Moranbah?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
One-car households formed 26.5% of the total.
71.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Moranbah, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

12.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Moranbah?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

6.4% used a language other than English at home.
0.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Moranbah?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
