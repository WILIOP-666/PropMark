## Questions people ask about Moruya
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Moruya NSW 2537.
Is Moruya family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
14.7% of Moruya's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
1,195 family households lived in Moruya; 29.0% were couple families and 16.6% were one-parent families.
26.7% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Moruya?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Moruya had 4,295 residents at the 2021 Census.
17.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
46.6% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Moruya?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 89.0% of occupied private dwellings in Moruya.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 7.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Moruya?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
49.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
27.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
22.6% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Moruya?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Moruya had 2.2 people.
One-person households made up 30.2% of occupied homes. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 41.5%, while 17.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Moruya?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
9.3% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
66.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
0.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Moruya?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.
One-car households formed 33.7% of the total.
60.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Moruya, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

10.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Moruya?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

3.0% used a language other than English at home.
0.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Moruya residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
10.7% of Moruya's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 89.3% was born in Australia.
0.0% of residents were born in China.
0.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 89.1%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Moruya?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 15.1% of workers, and education and training employed 7.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Moruya?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

23.7% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 18.4% worked part time.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Moruya show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
47.5% reported no selected long-term health condition.
10.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.8% reported asthma, 15.4% arthritis and 5.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Moruya?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
15.1% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 4.6% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 42.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Moruya?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
