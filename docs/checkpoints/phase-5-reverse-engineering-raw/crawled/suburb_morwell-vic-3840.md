The median house price in Morwell, VIC is currently $440,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Morwell has a 12-month growth rate of 20.5% for houses and 21.2% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $350,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $440,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $540,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $665,000 in Morwell.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Morwell is $358,000, which has increased 21.2% since the same time last year. Do people own or rent in Morwell?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
