Is Mount Colah family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Do people own or rent in Mount Colah?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

2.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Mount Colah?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
