## Questions people ask about Mount Cotton
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Mount Cotton QLD 4165.
Is Mount Cotton family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
25.9% of Mount Cotton's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
2,121 family households lived in Mount Cotton; 59.1% were couple families and 9.9% were one-parent families.
49.8% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Mount Cotton?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Mount Cotton had 7,302 residents at the 2021 Census.
31.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
17.2% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Mount Cotton?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 99.6% of occupied private dwellings in Mount Cotton.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 0.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Mount Cotton?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
19.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
66.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
13.8% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Mount Cotton?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Mount Cotton had 3.1 people.
One-person households made up 8.8% of occupied homes. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
Two-person households represented 28.5%, while 42.1% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Mount Cotton?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
17.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
63.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
0.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Mount Cotton?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
0.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 15.7% of the total.
83.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Mount Cotton, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
61.2% were at the same address five years earlier, while 38.8% reported a different address.
19.5% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Mount Cotton?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
92.5% of residents used only English at home.
5.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Mount Cotton residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.2% of residents were born in China.
0.6% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 92.0%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Mount Cotton?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 15.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 14.6%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.4% of workers, and education and training employed 11.0%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Mount Cotton?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

48.1% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 21.5% worked part time.
Unemployment was 2.7%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Brisbane.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Mount Cotton show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
65.7% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.5% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.0% reported asthma, 5.6% arthritis and 2.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Mount Cotton?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
22.6% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 5.8% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 20.5%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 41.7%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Mount Cotton?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
