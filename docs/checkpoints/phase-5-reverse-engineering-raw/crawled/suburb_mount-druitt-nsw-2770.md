All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Mount Druitt?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Mount Druitt?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

5.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Mount Druitt?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Mount Druitt, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
