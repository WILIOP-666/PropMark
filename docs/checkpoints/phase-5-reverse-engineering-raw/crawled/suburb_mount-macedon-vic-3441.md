## Questions people ask about Mount Macedon

Is Mount Macedon family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

423 family households lived in Mount Macedon; 44.4% were couple families and 6.1% were one-parent families.
35.7% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Mount Macedon?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Mount Macedon had 1,450 residents at the 2021 Census.
18.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
40.2% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Mount Macedon?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 98.4% of occupied private dwellings in Mount Macedon.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 0.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Mount Macedon?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
45.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
47.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
7.3% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Mount Macedon?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Mount Macedon had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 17.3% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 42.7%, while 27.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Mount Macedon?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
37.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
41.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Mount Macedon?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
0.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 20.7% of the total.
76.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Mount Macedon, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

17.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

4.1% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.

Where were Mount Macedon residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

6.9% reported asthma, 8.5% arthritis and 3.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Mount Macedon?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 17.7%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
