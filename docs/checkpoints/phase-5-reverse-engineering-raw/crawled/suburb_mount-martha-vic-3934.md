Is Mount Martha family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Mount Martha?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Mount Martha had 19,846 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Mount Martha?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 0.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Mount Martha?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
43.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
43.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Mount Martha?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Mount Martha?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

69.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Mount Martha, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Mount Martha?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
