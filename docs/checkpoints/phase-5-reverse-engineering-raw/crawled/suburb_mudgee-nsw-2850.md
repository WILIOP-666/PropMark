The median house price in Mudgee, NSW is currently $748,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Mudgee has a 12-month growth rate of 3.8% for houses and 5.8% for units.

## Questions people ask about Mudgee
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Mudgee NSW 2850.
Is Mudgee family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.1% of Mudgee's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
2,998 family households lived in Mudgee; 40.5% were couple families and 18.0% were one-parent families.
42.2% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Mudgee?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Mudgee had 11,457 residents at the 2021 Census.
27.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.5% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Mudgee?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 84.6% of occupied private dwellings in Mudgee.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Mudgee?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
30.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
32.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
36.5% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Mudgee?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Mudgee had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 28.8% of occupied homes. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 33.6%, while 22.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Mudgee?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
6.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
67.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
0.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Mudgee?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 35.1% of the total.
58.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Mudgee, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

8.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Mudgee?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

4.6% used a language other than English at home.
0.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.

10.0% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.

These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Mudgee?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
