Is Mulgrave family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Do people own or rent in Mulgrave?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Mulgrave?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
