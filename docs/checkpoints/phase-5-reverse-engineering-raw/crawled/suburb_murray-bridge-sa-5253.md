The median house price in Murray Bridge, SA is currently $620,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Murray Bridge has a 12-month growth rate of 15.9% for houses and 28.0% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $522,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $578,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $705,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $700,000 in Murray Bridge.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Murray Bridge is $500 per week. The median house rent has increased 5.3% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Murray Bridge is $548,000, which has increased 28.0% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $480,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $550,000 in Murray Bridge. The median rent for units and apartments in Murray Bridge is $325 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.

Is Murray Bridge family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

What is the age profile of Murray Bridge?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Murray Bridge had 15,043 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Murray Bridge?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 0.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Murray Bridge?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

28.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
