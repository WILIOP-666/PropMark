Is Nairne family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

42.4% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.

What is the age profile of Nairne?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Do people own or rent in Nairne?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

54.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.

How large are households in Nairne?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

How do people get to work in Nairne?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

2.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Nairne?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Nairne, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Nairne?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
