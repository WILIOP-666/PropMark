## Questions people ask about Nambour
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Nambour QLD 4560.
Is Nambour family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.4% of Nambour's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
3,120 family households lived in Nambour; 36.5% were couple families and 25.3% were one-parent families.
39.6% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Nambour?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Nambour had 12,145 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.7% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Nambour?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 66.3% of occupied private dwellings in Nambour.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 20.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 13.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Nambour?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
23.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
36.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
39.8% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Nambour?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Nambour had 2.3 people.
One-person households made up 32.8% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 32.2%, while 19.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Nambour?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
8.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
67.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
1.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Nambour?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 39.8% of the total.
52.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Nambour, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

17.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Nambour?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

5.9% used a language other than English at home.
0.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

Where were Nambour residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

10.8% reported asthma, 12.3% arthritis and 4.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Nambour?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
