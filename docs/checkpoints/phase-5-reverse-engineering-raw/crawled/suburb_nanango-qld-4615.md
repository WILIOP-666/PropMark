Nanango had 3,679 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Nanango?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Do people own or rent in Nanango?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Nanango?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
