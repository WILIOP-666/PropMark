The median house price in Nerang, QLD is currently $1,120,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Nerang has a 12-month growth rate of 14.4% for houses and 11.9% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $870,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,000,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,240,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,720,000 in Nerang.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Nerang is $888 per week. The median house rent has increased 11.0% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Nerang is $782,000, which has increased 11.9% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $598,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $750,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $818,000 in Nerang. The median rent for units and apartments in Nerang is $725 per week and has increased 9.5% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Nerang
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Nerang QLD 4211.
Is Nerang family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.2% of Nerang's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
4,582 family households lived in Nerang; 38.2% were couple families and 22.3% were one-parent families.
37.2% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Nerang?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Nerang had 17,048 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
31.9% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Nerang?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 61.4% of occupied private dwellings in Nerang.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 23.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 13.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Nerang?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
25.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
40.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
33.2% rented their home. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Nerang?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Nerang had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 28.2% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 32.6%, while 22.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Nerang?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
9.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
68.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
1.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Nerang?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 35.7% of the total.
57.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Nerang, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
58.0% were at the same address five years earlier, while 42.0% reported a different address.
24.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Nerang?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
84.9% of residents used only English at home.
9.6% used a language other than English at home.
0.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Nerang residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
24.4% of Nerang's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 75.6% was born in Australia.
0.4% of residents were born in China.
1.8% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 82.7%.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

11.3% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.3% reported asthma, 10.7% arthritis and 4.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Nerang?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 44.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Nerang?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
