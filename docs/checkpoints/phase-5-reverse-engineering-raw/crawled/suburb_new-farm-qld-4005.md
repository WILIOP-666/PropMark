## Questions people ask about New Farm

Is New Farm family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
9.3% of New Farm's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
2,809 family households lived in New Farm; 28.1% were couple families and 10.1% were one-parent families.
25.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of New Farm?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
New Farm had 12,197 residents at the 2021 Census.
38.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.9% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in New Farm?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 20.5% of occupied private dwellings in New Farm.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 74.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in New Farm?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
24.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
20.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
54.8% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in New Farm?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in New Farm had 1.9 people.
One-person households made up 42.5% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Two-person households represented 37.3%, while 9.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in New Farm?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
26.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
34.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
7.9% used only a train, tram or bus, while 12.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in New Farm?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
15.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 51.7% of the total.
32.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to New Farm, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

What languages are spoken in New Farm?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

15.6% used a language other than English at home.
1.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.

How educated is New Farm?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
