## Questions people ask about New Norfolk

Is New Norfolk family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.6% of New Norfolk's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
1,644 family households lived in New Norfolk; 32.7% were couple families and 25.5% were one-parent families.
36.6% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Hobart.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of New Norfolk?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
New Norfolk had 6,037 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
33.3% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in New Norfolk?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 91.0% of occupied private dwellings in New Norfolk.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in New Norfolk?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
31.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Hobart average.
36.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
31.9% rented their home. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in New Norfolk?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in New Norfolk had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 29.9% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
Two-person households represented 34.8%, while 20.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in New Norfolk?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
4.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Greater Hobart average.
70.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Hobart average.
2.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in New Norfolk?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Hobart average.
One-car households formed 37.0% of the total.
54.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to New Norfolk, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
60.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 39.4% reported a different address.
7.2% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Greater Hobart rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in New Norfolk?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
90.7% of residents used only English at home.
2.5% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were New Norfolk residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
7.2% of New Norfolk's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 92.8% was born in Australia.
0.2% of residents were born in China.
0.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 90.9%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.

Australian ancestry made up 45.1% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 0.5% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in New Norfolk?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
9.8% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is about half the Greater Hobart rate.
Managers accounted for 8.2% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.0%.
Health care and social assistance employed 19.1% of workers, and education and training employed 6.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in New Norfolk?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 51.8%, compared with 61.6% across Greater Hobart.
26.7% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 17.5% worked part time.
Unemployment was 8.4%, compared with 6.2% across Greater Hobart.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of New Norfolk show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
49.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
14.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.2% reported asthma, 13.6% arthritis and 7.0% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is New Norfolk?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
11.5% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is about half the Greater Hobart rate.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 3.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 50.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in New Norfolk?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,112 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,127.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $290 per week.
