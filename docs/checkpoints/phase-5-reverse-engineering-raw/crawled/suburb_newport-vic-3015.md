Is Newport family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.8% of Newport's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
3,774 family households lived in Newport; 52.6% were couple families and 13.3% were one-parent families.
45.8% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Newport?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Newport had 13,658 residents at the 2021 Census.
30.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
23.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Newport?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 66.7% of occupied private dwellings in Newport.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 24.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 7.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Newport?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
28.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
42.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
29.2% rented their home. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Newport?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 21.5% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 31.4%, while 29.3% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Newport?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
42.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
34.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
3.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Newport?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
One-car households formed 41.3% of the total.
53.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Newport, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

23.2% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Newport?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
79.2% of residents used only English at home.
17.6% used a language other than English at home.
0.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.4% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Newport residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

Health care and social assistance employed 11.3% of workers, and education and training employed 10.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Newport?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Newport?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Newport (Vic.)
### Is Newport (Vic.) expensive?
The median house price is $1,220,000, while units and apartments sit at $760,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Newport (Vic.) good for families?
The Census counted 3,774 local families, and 21.8% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Newport (Vic.)?
