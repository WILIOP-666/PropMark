## Questions people ask about Nimbin

Is Nimbin family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
15.1% of Nimbin's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
315 family households lived in Nimbin; 29.2% were couple families and 28.3% were one-parent families.
33.7% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Nimbin?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Nimbin had 1,607 residents at the 2021 Census.
19.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
43.9% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Nimbin?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 92.0% of occupied private dwellings in Nimbin.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 1.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Nimbin?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
55.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
24.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
19.9% rented their home. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Nimbin?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Nimbin had 2.0 people.
One-person households made up 42.6% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 31.4%, while 14.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Nimbin?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
16.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
53.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
0.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 6.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Nimbin?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 47.8% of the total.
45.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Nimbin, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

15.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Nimbin?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

6.5% used a language other than English at home.
0.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.

Where were Nimbin residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

0.0% of residents were born in China.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.

Health care and social assistance employed 22.9% of workers, and education and training employed 13.2%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Nimbin?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Nimbin?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 5.2% of qualification responses.

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Nimbin
### Is Nimbin expensive?
The median house price is $758,000, while units and apartments sit at $545,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Nimbin good for families?
The Census counted 315 local families, and 15.1% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Nimbin?
3-bedroom houses have a median around $855,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
