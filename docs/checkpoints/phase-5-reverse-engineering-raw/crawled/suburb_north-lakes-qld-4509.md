## Questions people ask about North Lakes
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for North Lakes QLD 4509.
Is North Lakes family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
23.8% of North Lakes's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
6,444 family households lived in North Lakes; 52.4% were couple families and 16.3% were one-parent families.
48.2% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of North Lakes?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
North Lakes had 23,030 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
19.6% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in North Lakes?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 84.8% of occupied private dwellings in North Lakes.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 3.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in North Lakes?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
18.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
43.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
38.7% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in North Lakes?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in North Lakes had 2.9 people.
One-person households made up 15.7% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
Two-person households represented 30.2%, while 34.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in North Lakes?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
16.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
60.0% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
2.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in North Lakes?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 33.5% of the total.
63.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to North Lakes, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
45.0% were at the same address five years earlier, while 55.0% reported a different address.
34.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in North Lakes?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
78.7% of residents used only English at home.
17.9% used a language other than English at home.
1.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 1.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were North Lakes residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
34.6% of North Lakes's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 65.4% was born in Australia.
1.0% of residents were born in China.
3.0% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 81.6%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape North Lakes?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 37.9% of ancestry responses in North Lakes.
Australian ancestry made up 31.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 2.9% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in North Lakes?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
21.6% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Managers accounted for 12.7% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 14.5%.
Health care and social assistance employed 16.9% of workers, and education and training employed 8.3%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in North Lakes?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 70.2%, compared with 64.5% across Greater Brisbane.
40.3% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 20.0% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.4%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Brisbane.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of North Lakes show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
63.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.7% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.1% reported asthma, 6.8% arthritis and 4.0% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is North Lakes?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
25.6% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 7.9% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 19.1%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 35.0%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in North Lakes?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,092 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,950.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $420 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does North Lakes compare with Greater Brisbane?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
