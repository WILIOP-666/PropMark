Is North Melbourne family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

32.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of North Melbourne?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
North Melbourne had 14,953 residents at the 2021 Census.
44.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
15.7% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in North Melbourne?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 26.8% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 68.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in North Melbourne?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
13.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
20.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
65.8% rented their home. That is about twice the Greater Melbourne rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in North Melbourne?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in North Melbourne?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

18.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Melbourne rate.
8.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 15.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in North Melbourne?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
32.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Melbourne rate.
One-car households formed 50.8% of the total.
16.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to North Melbourne, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

37.7% used a language other than English at home.
9.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 3.6% spoke Cantonese and 0.8% spoke Hindi.

Where were North Melbourne residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

7.7% of residents were born in China.

What work do people do in North Melbourne?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

How active is the workforce in North Melbourne?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is North Melbourne?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

The median house price is $1,200,000, while units and apartments sit at $580,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is North Melbourne good for families?
