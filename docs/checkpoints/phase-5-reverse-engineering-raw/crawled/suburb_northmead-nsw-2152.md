Is Northmead family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

3,096 family households lived in Northmead; 47.4% were couple families and 15.5% were one-parent families.
40.5% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Northmead?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Northmead had 11,261 residents at the 2021 Census.
29.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.9% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Northmead?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 48.9% of occupied private dwellings in Northmead.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 19.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 31.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Northmead?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
27.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
37.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
34.5% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Northmead?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 31.8%, while 23.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Northmead?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
43.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
30.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
2.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Northmead?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 43.2% of the total.
48.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Northmead, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

34.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Northmead?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

33.1% used a language other than English at home.
3.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 2.3% spoke Cantonese and 2.0% spoke Hindi.

Where were Northmead residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Northmead?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 16.9% of workers, and education and training employed 10.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Northmead?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

7.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.7% reported asthma, 8.4% arthritis and 4.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Northmead?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 14.0% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 15.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 23.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Northmead?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
