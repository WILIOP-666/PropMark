## Questions people ask about Nowra
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Nowra NSW 2541.
Is Nowra family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.4% of Nowra's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
2,373 family households lived in Nowra; 30.5% were couple families and 27.1% were one-parent families.
37.3% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Nowra?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Nowra had 9,956 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
35.0% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Nowra?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 81.1% of occupied private dwellings in Nowra.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 13.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Nowra?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
29.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
22.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
47.8% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Nowra?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Nowra had 2.2 people.
One-person households made up 35.5% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 33.9%, while 17.1% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Nowra?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
7.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
63.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
0.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 5.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Nowra?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
12.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Rest of NSW rate.
One-car households formed 45.1% of the total.
42.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Nowra, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
56.7% were at the same address five years earlier, while 43.3% reported a different address.
13.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Nowra?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
81.8% of residents used only English at home.
7.6% used a language other than English at home.
0.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Nowra residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
13.4% of Nowra's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 86.6% was born in Australia.
0.7% of residents were born in China.
1.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 84.2%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Nowra?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 37.7% of ancestry responses in Nowra.
Australian ancestry made up 38.9% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 1.2% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Nowra?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
13.9% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Managers accounted for 8.5% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 9.3%.
Health care and social assistance employed 21.3% of workers, and education and training employed 5.9%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Nowra?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 43.5%, compared with 56.4% across Rest of NSW.
21.2% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 15.2% worked part time.
Unemployment was 9.2%, compared with 4.6% across Rest of NSW.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Nowra show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
44.5% reported no selected long-term health condition.
16.0% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.8% reported asthma, 13.7% arthritis and 7.5% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Nowra?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
13.5% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 4.2% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 42.1%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Nowra?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,037 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,500.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $300 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Nowra compare with Rest of NSW?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 7.5% in Nowra, compared with 15.7% across Rest of NSW.
Outright ownership was 29.5% locally and 39.6% across Rest of NSW. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
Renting accounted for 47.8% in Nowra, against 27.9% across Rest of NSW.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.
