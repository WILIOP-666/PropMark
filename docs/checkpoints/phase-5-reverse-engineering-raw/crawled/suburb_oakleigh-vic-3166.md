Is Oakleigh family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

2,255 family households lived in Oakleigh; 45.7% were couple families and 13.8% were one-parent families.
33.0% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Oakleigh?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Oakleigh had 8,442 residents at the 2021 Census.
30.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.3% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Oakleigh?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 61.8% of occupied private dwellings in Oakleigh.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 25.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Oakleigh?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
32.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
35.5% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Oakleigh?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 27.0% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 33.0%, while 23.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Oakleigh?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
34.2% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
39.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
4.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Oakleigh?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
11.0% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
One-car households formed 42.2% of the total.
46.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Oakleigh, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
58.4% were at the same address five years earlier, while 41.6% reported a different address.
38.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Oakleigh?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
54.5% of residents used only English at home.
41.9% used a language other than English at home.
6.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.5% spoke Cantonese and 1.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Oakleigh residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

5.6% of residents were born in China.
4.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 79.9%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Oakleigh?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 14.4% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 13.1%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.7% of workers, and education and training employed 11.4%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Oakleigh?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

38.3% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 20.6% worked part time.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

8.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.1% reported asthma, 7.4% arthritis and 4.5% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Oakleigh?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 17.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 15.0%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 17.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Oakleigh?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
