The median house price in Ormeau, QLD is currently $1,130,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Ormeau has a 12-month growth rate of 20.2% for houses and 12.3% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $550,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $920,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,100,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,470,000 in Ormeau.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Ormeau is $800 per week. The median house rent has increased 6.7% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Ormeau is $848,000, which has increased 12.3% since the same time last year. Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $845,000 in Ormeau. The median rent for units and apartments in Ormeau is $625 per week and has increased 2.1% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Ormeau
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Ormeau QLD 4208.
Is Ormeau family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
25.5% of Ormeau's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
4,372 family households lived in Ormeau; 52.2% were couple families and 16.8% were one-parent families.
49.5% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Ormeau?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Ormeau had 15,938 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
19.2% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Ormeau?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 91.0% of occupied private dwellings in Ormeau.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 7.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Ormeau?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
20.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
48.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
30.4% rented their home. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Ormeau?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Ormeau had 3.1 people.
One-person households made up 12.7% of occupied homes. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
Two-person households represented 29.2%, while 39.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Ormeau?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
12.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
66.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
0.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Ormeau?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
1.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
One-car households formed 24.7% of the total.
73.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Ormeau, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
49.4% were at the same address five years earlier, while 50.6% reported a different address.
22.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Ormeau?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
89.2% of residents used only English at home.
6.5% used a language other than English at home.
0.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Ormeau residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
22.9% of Ormeau's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 77.1% was born in Australia.
0.3% of residents were born in China.
0.4% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 83.8%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Ormeau?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 42.6% of ancestry responses in Ormeau.
Australian ancestry made up 38.9% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 1.3% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Ormeau?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
15.6% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Managers accounted for 12.0% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 14.3%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.4% of workers, and education and training employed 8.0%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Ormeau?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 70.2%, compared with 59.1% across Rest of Qld.
41.3% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 19.9% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.5%, compared with 5.2% across Rest of Qld.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Ormeau show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
60.9% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.5% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.3% reported asthma, 7.5% arthritis and 3.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Ormeau?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
17.3% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 3.9% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 19.0%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 46.4%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Ormeau?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,067 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,950.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $445 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Ormeau compare with Rest of Qld?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 12.6% in Ormeau, compared with 10.6% across Rest of Qld.
