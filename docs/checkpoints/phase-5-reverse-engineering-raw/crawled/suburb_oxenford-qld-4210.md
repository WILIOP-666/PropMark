The median house price in Oxenford, QLD is currently $1,180,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Oxenford has a 12-month growth rate of 7.8% for houses and 19.4% for units.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Oxenford is $820,000, which has increased 19.4% since the same time last year. Is Oxenford family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
20.9% of Oxenford's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
3,500 family households lived in Oxenford; 46.3% were couple families and 18.7% were one-parent families.
42.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Oxenford?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Oxenford had 12,273 residents at the 2021 Census.
27.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
25.0% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Oxenford?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 73.4% of occupied private dwellings in Oxenford.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 25.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Oxenford?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
22.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
52.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
24.9% rented their home. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Oxenford?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 32.6%, while 30.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Oxenford?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
11.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
67.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
1.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Oxenford?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
One-car households formed 27.6% of the total.
70.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Oxenford, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

10.1% used a language other than English at home.
1.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Oxenford?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
