Is Paddington family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

34.0% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Paddington?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Paddington had 12,701 residents at the 2021 Census.
37.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
25.7% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Paddington?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 60.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 31.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Paddington?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

26.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
45.9% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Paddington?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Paddington?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

4.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 8.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Paddington?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
21.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 53.6% of the total.
25.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Paddington, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

30.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

12.1% used a language other than English at home.
0.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.7% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

What work do people do in Paddington?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.

How educated is Paddington?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
