The median house price in Padstow, NSW is currently $1,720,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Padstow has a 12-month growth rate of 4.9% for houses and 13.5% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,280,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,480,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,620,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,910,000 in Padstow.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Padstow is $1,040,000, which has increased 13.5% since the same time last year. Is Padstow family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

3,816 family households lived in Padstow; 49.4% were couple families and 17.0% were one-parent families.
34.6% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Padstow?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Padstow had 14,017 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.9% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Padstow?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 6.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Padstow?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Padstow?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
