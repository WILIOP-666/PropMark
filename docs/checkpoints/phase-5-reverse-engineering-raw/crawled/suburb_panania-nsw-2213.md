Is Panania family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

3,647 family households lived in Panania; 53.1% were couple families and 16.0% were one-parent families.
39.5% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Panania?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Panania had 13,507 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.6% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Panania?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 70.7% of occupied private dwellings in Panania.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 24.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Panania?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
37.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
29.9% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Panania?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

How do people get to work in Panania?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

33.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
2.9% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Panania?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
7.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.

55.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Panania, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

What languages are spoken in Panania?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

3.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 3.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.7% spoke Hindi.

Where were Panania residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Panania?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
