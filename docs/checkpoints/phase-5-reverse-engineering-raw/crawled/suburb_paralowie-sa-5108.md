## Questions people ask about Paralowie

Is Paralowie family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.2% of Paralowie's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
4,679 family households lived in Paralowie; 46.9% were couple families and 22.1% were one-parent families.
43.0% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Paralowie?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Paralowie had 17,504 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
22.6% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Paralowie?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 94.2% of occupied private dwellings in Paralowie.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Paralowie?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
24.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
50.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
24.8% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Adelaide average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Paralowie?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 29.1%, while 31.9% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Paralowie?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
3.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
75.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
1.9% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Paralowie?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
4.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat below the Greater Adelaide average.
One-car households formed 32.1% of the total.
63.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Paralowie, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

31.2% used a language other than English at home.
0.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.5% spoke Hindi.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

9.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.2% reported asthma, 8.1% arthritis and 6.3% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Paralowie?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 51.0%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
