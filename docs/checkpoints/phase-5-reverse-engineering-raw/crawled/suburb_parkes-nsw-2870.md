## Questions people ask about Parkes

Is Parkes family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
20.7% of Parkes's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
2,924 family households lived in Parkes; 38.3% were couple families and 19.0% were one-parent families.
39.8% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Parkes?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Parkes had 11,324 residents at the 2021 Census.
23.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.6% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Parkes?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 88.4% of occupied private dwellings in Parkes.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 3.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 6.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Parkes?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
35.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
34.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
30.4% rented their home. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Parkes?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Parkes had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 30.5% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 33.7%, while 22.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Parkes?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
8.2% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
68.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
0.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Parkes?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 36.5% of the total.
56.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Parkes, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

6.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Rest of NSW rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Parkes?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
89.7% of residents used only English at home.
3.9% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Parkes residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
6.0% of Parkes's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 94.0% was born in Australia.
0.3% of residents were born in China.
0.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 90.9%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.

How active is the workforce in Parkes?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Parkes?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 3.2% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.5%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 48.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Parkes?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
