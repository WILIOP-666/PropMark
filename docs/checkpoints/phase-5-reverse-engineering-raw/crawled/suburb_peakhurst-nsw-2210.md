Is Peakhurst family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

38.6% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Peakhurst?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Peakhurst had 12,079 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Peakhurst?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
