## Questions people ask about Port Adelaide
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Port Adelaide SA 5015.
Is Port Adelaide family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
9.5% of Port Adelaide's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
330 family households lived in Port Adelaide; 22.7% were couple families and 21.2% were one-parent families.
23.0% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Port Adelaide?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Port Adelaide had 1,338 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
35.9% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Port Adelaide?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 27.8% of occupied private dwellings in Port Adelaide.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 57.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 9.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Port Adelaide?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
20.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
23.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
55.4% rented their home. That is about twice the Greater Adelaide rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Port Adelaide?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Port Adelaide had 1.9 people.
One-person households made up 43.5% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
Two-person households represented 37.0%, while 9.1% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Port Adelaide?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
10.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Adelaide average.
61.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Adelaide average.
4.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 7.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Port Adelaide?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
17.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Adelaide rate.
One-car households formed 45.3% of the total.
37.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Port Adelaide, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
52.1% were at the same address five years earlier, while 47.9% reported a different address.
23.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Adelaide average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Port Adelaide?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
80.4% of residents used only English at home.
13.4% used a language other than English at home.
1.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.0% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Port Adelaide residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
23.7% of Port Adelaide's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 76.3% was born in Australia.
1.1% of residents were born in China.
0.7% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 86.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Port Adelaide?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 38.4% of ancestry responses in Port Adelaide.
Australian ancestry made up 29.4% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 3.4% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Port Adelaide?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
23.5% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Adelaide.
Managers accounted for 13.5% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.9%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.9% of workers, and education and training employed 6.7%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Port Adelaide?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 60.0%, compared with 61.7% across Greater Adelaide.
35.1% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 16.6% worked part time.
Unemployment was 9.5%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Adelaide.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Port Adelaide show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
52.8% reported no selected long-term health condition.
12.5% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
9.5% reported asthma, 11.0% arthritis and 6.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Port Adelaide?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
23.9% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Greater Adelaide average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 7.0% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.5%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 34.6%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Port Adelaide?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,230 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,672.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $293 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Port Adelaide compare with Greater Adelaide?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 10.5% in Port Adelaide, compared with 9.8% across Greater Adelaide.
Outright ownership was 20.7% locally and 32.1% across Greater Adelaide. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Renting accounted for 55.4% in Port Adelaide, against 29.5% across Greater Adelaide.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Port Adelaide
### Is Port Adelaide expensive?
The median house price is $950,000, while units and apartments sit at $800,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Port Adelaide good for families?
The Census counted 330 local families, and 9.5% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Port Adelaide?
4-bedroom houses have a median around $1,070,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
Medians describe recorded market activity across the suburb; they are historical context, not a valuation or forecast for an individual home.
