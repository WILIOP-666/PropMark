The median house price in Port Augusta, SA is currently $343,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Port Augusta has a 12-month growth rate of 19.3% for houses.

Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $460,000 in Port Augusta. The median rent for units and apartments in Port Augusta is $275 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Port Augusta
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Port Augusta SA 5700.
Is Port Augusta family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.3% of Port Augusta's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of SA average.
1,545 family households lived in Port Augusta; 30.9% were couple families and 25.4% were one-parent families.
37.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Rest of SA average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Port Augusta?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Port Augusta had 6,437 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.3% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of SA average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Port Augusta?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 74.4% of occupied private dwellings in Port Augusta.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 14.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 10.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Port Augusta?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
31.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of SA average.
24.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
44.7% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of SA average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Port Augusta?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Port Augusta had 2.2 people.
One-person households made up 38.0% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of SA average.
Two-person households represented 32.8%, while 16.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Port Augusta?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
1.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of SA rate.
67.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of SA average.
2.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Port Augusta?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
14.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Rest of SA rate.
One-car households formed 41.7% of the total.
43.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Port Augusta, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
63.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 36.2% reported a different address.
10.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Rest of SA average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Port Augusta?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
82.2% of residents used only English at home.
8.8% used a language other than English at home.
0.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Port Augusta residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
10.3% of Port Augusta's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 89.7% was born in Australia.
0.3% of residents were born in China.
1.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 85.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Port Augusta?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 32.0% of ancestry responses in Port Augusta.
Australian ancestry made up 38.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 0.8% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Port Augusta?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
14.8% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is close to the Rest of SA average.
Managers accounted for 7.3% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 9.9%.
Health care and social assistance employed 17.4% of workers, and education and training employed 8.6%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Port Augusta?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 52.5%, compared with 54.1% across Rest of SA.
28.4% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 16.1% worked part time.
Unemployment was 8.1%, compared with 4.8% across Rest of SA.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Port Augusta show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
51.2% reported no selected long-term health condition.
10.2% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
11.7% reported asthma, 11.0% arthritis and 8.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Port Augusta?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
12.6% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Rest of SA average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 3.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 45.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Port Augusta?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,092 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $979.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $194 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Port Augusta compare with Rest of SA?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
