## Questions people ask about Port Douglas
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Port Douglas QLD 4877.
Is Port Douglas family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
8.5% of Port Douglas's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
797 family households lived in Port Douglas; 25.0% were couple families and 11.7% were one-parent families.
25.7% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Port Douglas?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Port Douglas had 3,650 residents at the 2021 Census.
33.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
37.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Port Douglas?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 35.7% of occupied private dwellings in Port Douglas.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 32.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 26.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Port Douglas?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
29.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Rest of Qld average.
27.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
43.8% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Port Douglas?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Port Douglas had 2.1 people.
One-person households made up 32.5% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 45.2%, while 11.1% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Port Douglas?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
10.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
47.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
1.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 16.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Port Douglas?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 50.4% of the total.
40.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Port Douglas, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
38.5% were at the same address five years earlier, while 61.5% reported a different address.
31.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Port Douglas?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
69.9% of residents used only English at home.
14.5% used a language other than English at home.
0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Port Douglas residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
31.9% of Port Douglas's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 68.1% was born in Australia.
0.2% of residents were born in China.
0.7% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 67.6%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Port Douglas?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 36.7% of ancestry responses in Port Douglas.
Australian ancestry made up 24.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 0.9% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Port Douglas?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 13.5% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 9.0%.
Health care and social assistance employed 6.8% of workers, and education and training employed 3.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Port Douglas?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Port Douglas show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
59.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
6.5% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
4.5% reported asthma, 6.8% arthritis and 2.4% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Port Douglas?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 4.9% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 27.6%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Port Douglas?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
