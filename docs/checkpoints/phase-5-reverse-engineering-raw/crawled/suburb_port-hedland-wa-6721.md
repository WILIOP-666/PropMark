Is Port Hedland family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
24.1% of Port Hedland's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of WA average.
934 family households lived in Port Hedland; 53.3% were couple families and 7.3% were one-parent families.
51.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of WA average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Port Hedland?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Port Hedland had 4,081 residents at the 2021 Census.
41.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
13.3% were 55 or older. That is about half the Rest of WA rate.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Port Hedland?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 67.8% of occupied private dwellings in Port Hedland.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 18.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Port Hedland?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
8.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Rest of WA rate.
14.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
77.0% rented their home. That is about twice the Rest of WA rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Port Hedland?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Port Hedland had 2.5 people.
One-person households made up 27.8% of occupied homes. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of WA.
Two-person households represented 31.2%, while 26.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Port Hedland?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
2.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of WA rate.
73.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Rest of WA average.
0.9% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Port Hedland?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Rest of WA rate.
One-car households formed 30.2% of the total.
67.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Port Hedland, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
26.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 73.4% reported a different address.
20.8% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Rest of WA average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Port Hedland?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
76.4% of residents used only English at home.
11.0% used a language other than English at home.
0.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Port Hedland residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
20.8% of Port Hedland's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 79.2% was born in Australia.
0.6% of residents were born in China.
1.1% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 77.3%.

What work do people do in Port Hedland?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 10.2% of workers, and education and training employed 6.2%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Port Hedland?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

5.2% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
5.6% reported asthma, 2.7% arthritis and 1.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Port Hedland?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 38.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
