## Questions people ask about Port Kembla

Is Port Kembla family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.2% of Port Kembla's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
1,356 family households lived in Port Kembla; 37.9% were couple families and 24.7% were one-parent families.
34.7% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Port Kembla?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Port Kembla had 5,088 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.7% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Port Kembla?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 76.8% of occupied private dwellings in Port Kembla.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 6.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 15.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Port Kembla?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
38.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
26.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
34.6% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Port Kembla?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Port Kembla had 2.4 people.
One-person households made up 31.2% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 32.8%, while 20.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Port Kembla?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
20.2% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
54.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
1.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Port Kembla?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
13.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Rest of NSW rate.
One-car households formed 37.7% of the total.
48.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Port Kembla, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
66.2% were at the same address five years earlier, while 33.8% reported a different address.
23.6% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Rest of NSW rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Port Kembla?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
71.1% of residents used only English at home.
23.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Port Kembla residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
23.6% of Port Kembla's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 76.4% was born in Australia.
0.2% of residents were born in China.
0.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 89.9%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Port Kembla?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

How active is the workforce in Port Kembla?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

11.5% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
8.3% reported asthma, 11.2% arthritis and 6.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Port Kembla?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
