Is Port Kennedy family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
22.1% of Port Kennedy's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
3,735 family households lived in Port Kennedy; 46.3% were couple families and 18.7% were one-parent families.
42.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Port Kennedy?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Port Kennedy had 13,477 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.6% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Port Kennedy?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 93.6% of occupied private dwellings in Port Kennedy.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 6.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Port Kennedy?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
22.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
54.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
23.4% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Port Kennedy?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 16.8% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Two-person households represented 32.7%, while 31.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Port Kennedy?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

62.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Perth.
3.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Port Kennedy?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
One-car households formed 30.6% of the total.
66.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Port Kennedy, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

29.1% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Port Kennedy?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

0.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

Where were Port Kennedy residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Port Kennedy?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.5%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 51.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
