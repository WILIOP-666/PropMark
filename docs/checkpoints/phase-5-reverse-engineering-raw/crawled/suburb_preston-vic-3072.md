The median house price in Preston (Vic.), VIC is currently $1,200,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Preston (Vic.) has a 12-month growth rate of 2.5% for houses and 1.8% for units.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Preston (Vic.) is $580,000, which has increased 1.8% since the same time last year. Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Do people own or rent in Preston?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
