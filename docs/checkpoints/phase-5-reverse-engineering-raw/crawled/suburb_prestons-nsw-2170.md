The median house price in Prestons, NSW is currently $1,210,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Prestons has a 12-month growth rate of 1.2% for houses and 18.1% for units.
Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,080,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,220,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,430,000 in Prestons.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Prestons is $1,080,000, which has increased 18.1% since the same time last year. ## Questions people ask about Prestons
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Prestons NSW 2170.
Is Prestons family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
22.5% of Prestons's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
4,000 family households lived in Prestons; 63.7% were couple families and 15.5% were one-parent families.
45.9% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Prestons?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Prestons had 15,694 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Prestons?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 92.5% of occupied private dwellings in Prestons.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 6.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Prestons?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
24.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
57.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
18.7% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Prestons?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Prestons had 3.6 people.
One-person households made up 8.8% of occupied homes. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Two-person households represented 18.7%, while 54.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Prestons?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
29.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
45.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
1.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Prestons?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 21.3% of the total.
75.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Prestons, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
74.5% were at the same address five years earlier, while 25.5% reported a different address.
43.1% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Prestons?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
39.3% of residents used only English at home.
55.4% used a language other than English at home.
0.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.3% spoke Cantonese and 6.7% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Prestons residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
43.1% of Prestons's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 56.9% was born in Australia.
0.9% of residents were born in China.
3.2% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 88.2%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Prestons?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
21.7% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 10.0% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 16.8%.
Health care and social assistance employed 13.5% of workers, and education and training employed 9.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Prestons?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

31.8% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 15.0% worked part time.
Unemployment was 6.2%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Prestons show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
68.8% reported no selected long-term health condition.
4.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.0% reported asthma, 5.5% arthritis and 6.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Prestons?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
28.4% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 8.5% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 18.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 29.7%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Prestons?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,200.

The median house price is $1,210,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,080,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
