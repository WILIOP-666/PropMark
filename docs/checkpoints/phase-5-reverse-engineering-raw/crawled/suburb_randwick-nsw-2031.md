## Questions people ask about Randwick
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Randwick NSW 2031.
Is Randwick family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
14.7% of Randwick's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
7,224 family households lived in Randwick; 40.9% were couple families and 12.9% were one-parent families.
36.3% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Randwick?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Randwick had 28,943 residents at the 2021 Census.
38.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
23.7% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Randwick?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 13.8% of occupied private dwellings in Randwick.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 16.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 68.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Randwick?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
23.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
24.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
51.9% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Randwick?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Randwick had 2.2 people.
One-person households made up 30.7% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 36.9%, while 16.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Randwick?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
49.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
20.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
4.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 8.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Randwick?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
17.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 53.7% of the total.
28.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Randwick, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
46.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 53.2% reported a different address.
40.2% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Randwick?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
73.5% of residents used only English at home.
20.9% used a language other than English at home.
2.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Randwick residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
40.2% of Randwick's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 59.8% was born in Australia.
2.0% of residents were born in China.
1.0% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 77.5%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Randwick?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 31.1% of ancestry responses in Randwick.
Australian ancestry made up 22.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 6.4% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Randwick?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
42.1% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 18.0% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.1%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.4% of workers, and education and training employed 11.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Randwick?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 68.9%, compared with 60.0% across Greater Sydney.
43.9% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 16.6% worked part time.
Unemployment was 3.6%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Randwick show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
67.7% reported no selected long-term health condition.
6.5% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.5% reported asthma, 5.1% arthritis and 2.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Randwick?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
41.8% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 19.6% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 10.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 12.6%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Randwick?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,422 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $3,000.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $572 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Randwick compare with Greater Sydney?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 49.7% in Randwick, compared with 38.9% across Greater Sydney.
Outright ownership was 23.9% locally and 28.6% across Greater Sydney. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Renting accounted for 51.9% in Randwick, against 37.1% across Greater Sydney.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Randwick
### Is Randwick expensive?
The median house price is $3,860,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,250,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Randwick good for families?
The Census counted 7,224 local families, and 14.7% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Randwick?
