Is Redfern family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
8.2% of Redfern's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
2,665 family households lived in Redfern; 25.4% were couple families and 10.1% were one-parent families.
24.2% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Redfern?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Redfern had 13,072 residents at the 2021 Census.
44.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.1% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Redfern?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 1.4% of occupied private dwellings in Redfern.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 28.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 68.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Redfern?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
15.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
21.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
63.8% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Redfern?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Redfern had 1.9 people.
One-person households made up 42.4% of occupied homes. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
Two-person households represented 35.7%, while 9.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Redfern?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
54.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
12.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
7.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 9.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Redfern?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
39.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 45.9% of the total.
14.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Redfern, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
40.7% were at the same address five years earlier, while 59.3% reported a different address.
40.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Redfern?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
65.0% of residents used only English at home.
26.7% used a language other than English at home.
3.6% spoke Mandarin at home, 2.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Redfern residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
40.3% of Redfern's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 59.7% was born in Australia.
4.3% of residents were born in China.
1.4% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 73.5%.

What work do people do in Redfern?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 17.3% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 10.6%.
Health care and social assistance employed 11.1% of workers, and education and training employed 8.6%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Redfern?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

11.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.8% reported asthma, 6.1% arthritis and 3.5% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Redfern?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.0%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 11.1%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
