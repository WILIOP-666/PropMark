Is Revesby family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

4,020 family households lived in Revesby; 50.4% were couple families and 19.7% were one-parent families.
38.7% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Revesby?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Revesby had 15,268 residents at the 2021 Census.
27.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Revesby?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 29.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 6.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Revesby?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

33.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
36.9% rented their home. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Revesby?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Revesby?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

4.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Revesby?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

52.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Revesby, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Where were Revesby residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Revesby?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.

The median house price is $1,620,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,140,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
