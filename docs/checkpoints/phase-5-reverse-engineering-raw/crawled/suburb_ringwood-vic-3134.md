Is Ringwood family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

38.7% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.

Ringwood had 19,144 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Flats and apartments accounted for 13.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Ringwood?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Ringwood?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

3.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
