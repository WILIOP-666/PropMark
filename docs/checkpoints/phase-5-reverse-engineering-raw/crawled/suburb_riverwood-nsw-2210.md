Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.

## A buyer’s quick guide to Riverwood
### Is Riverwood expensive?
The median house price is $1,470,000, while units and apartments sit at $680,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Riverwood good for families?
The Census counted 3,305 local families, and 15.4% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Riverwood?
