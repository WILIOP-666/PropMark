Is Robina family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
16.4% of Robina's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
7,163 family households lived in Robina; 40.9% were couple families and 17.7% were one-parent families.
35.4% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Robina?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Robina had 25,659 residents at the 2021 Census.
26.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
30.7% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Robina?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 54.5% of occupied private dwellings in Robina.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 31.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 13.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Robina?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.6% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
33.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
33.9% rented their home. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Robina?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 36.4%, while 24.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Robina?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
15.2% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
62.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
1.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Robina?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 36.6% of the total.
59.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Robina, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

17.3% used a language other than English at home.
3.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Robina?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 18.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 30.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
