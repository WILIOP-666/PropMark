Is Rockdale family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
13.7% of Rockdale's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
3,902 family households lived in Rockdale; 41.7% were couple families and 13.6% were one-parent families.
34.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Rockdale?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Rockdale had 15,475 residents at the 2021 Census.
41.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Rockdale?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 24.3% of occupied private dwellings in Rockdale.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 69.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Rockdale?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
23.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
28.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
48.6% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Rockdale?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Rockdale had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 23.7% of occupied homes. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
Two-person households represented 32.1%, while 24.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Rockdale?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
26.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
29.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
15.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 3.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Rockdale?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
19.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 52.2% of the total.
28.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Rockdale, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
47.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 52.2% reported a different address.
61.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Rockdale?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
28.0% of residents used only English at home.
65.3% used a language other than English at home.
5.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 4.1% spoke Cantonese and 1.5% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Rockdale residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

6.5% of residents were born in China.
3.0% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 62.6%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Rockdale?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 9.6% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.9%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.7% of workers, and education and training employed 5.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Rockdale?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Unemployment was 6.8%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

4.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.

How educated is Rockdale?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

## A buyer’s quick guide to Rockdale
### Is Rockdale expensive?
The median house price is $1,780,000, while units and apartments sit at $732,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Rockdale good for families?
The Census counted 3,902 local families, and 13.7% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Rockdale?
