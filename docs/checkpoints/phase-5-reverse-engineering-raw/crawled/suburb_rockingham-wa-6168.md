The median house price in Rockingham (WA), WA is currently $852,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Rockingham (WA) has a 12-month growth rate of 16.7% for houses and 22.4% for units.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Rockingham (WA) is $600,000, which has increased 22.4% since the same time last year.
