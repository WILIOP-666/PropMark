All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Rocklea?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How large are households in Rocklea?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

How do people get to work in Rocklea?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

4.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Rocklea?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

47.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Rocklea, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Rocklea?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
