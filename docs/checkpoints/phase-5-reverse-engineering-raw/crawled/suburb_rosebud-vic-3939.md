The median house price in Rosebud, VIC is currently $780,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Rosebud has a 12-month growth rate of 0.1% for houses and 5.1% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $650,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $775,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $995,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,200,000 in Rosebud.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Rosebud is $675,000, which has increased 5.1% since the same time last year. Is Rosebud family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
15.0% of Rosebud's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
3,861 family households lived in Rosebud; 32.7% were couple families and 20.0% were one-parent families.
30.5% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Rosebud?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Rosebud had 14,381 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
43.0% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Rosebud?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 88.7% of occupied private dwellings in Rosebud.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 8.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Rosebud?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
42.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
30.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
27.2% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Rosebud?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Rosebud?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Rosebud?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

53.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
