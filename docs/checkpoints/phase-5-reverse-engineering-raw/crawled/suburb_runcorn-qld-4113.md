Is Runcorn family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.6% of Runcorn's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
3,821 family households lived in Runcorn; 48.0% were couple families and 15.8% were one-parent families.
40.4% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Runcorn?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Runcorn had 14,199 residents at the 2021 Census.
35.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.9% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Runcorn?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 69.4% of occupied private dwellings in Runcorn.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 30.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Runcorn?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
27.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
33.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
38.8% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Runcorn?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 29.9%, while 31.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Runcorn?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

58.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
8.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Runcorn?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 38.4% of the total.
57.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Runcorn, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Runcorn?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
