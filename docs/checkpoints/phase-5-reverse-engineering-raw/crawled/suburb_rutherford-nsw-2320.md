Is Rutherford family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.9% of Rutherford's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
3,552 family households lived in Rutherford; 39.9% were couple families and 22.0% were one-parent families.
42.9% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Rutherford?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Rutherford had 13,091 residents at the 2021 Census.
28.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
27.4% were 55 or older. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Rutherford?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 86.9% of occupied private dwellings in Rutherford.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Rutherford?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
25.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
38.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
36.0% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Rutherford?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Rutherford?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

63.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
0.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Rutherford?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

56.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
