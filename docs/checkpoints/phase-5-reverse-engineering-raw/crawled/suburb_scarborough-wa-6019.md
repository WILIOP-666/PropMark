What is the age profile of Scarborough?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Scarborough?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 16.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Scarborough?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

How do people get to work in Scarborough?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Scarborough?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Scarborough, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Scarborough?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

The median house price is $1,520,000, while units and apartments sit at $870,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Scarborough (WA) good for families?
The Census counted 4,463 local families, and 14.5% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
