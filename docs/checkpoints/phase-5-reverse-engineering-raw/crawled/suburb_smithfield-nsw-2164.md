Is Smithfield family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
19.6% of Smithfield's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
3,248 family households lived in Smithfield; 51.2% were couple families and 22.4% were one-parent families.
39.1% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Smithfield?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Smithfield had 13,160 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Smithfield?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 77.3% of occupied private dwellings in Smithfield.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 10.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 10.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Smithfield?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
28.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
27.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
44.5% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Smithfield?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 23.0% of occupied homes. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
Two-person households represented 23.4%, while 38.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Smithfield?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
23.2% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
47.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
1.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Smithfield?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
11.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
One-car households formed 35.5% of the total.
52.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Smithfield, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

52.1% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Smithfield?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

62.0% used a language other than English at home.
1.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Smithfield residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

1.0% of residents were born in China.
0.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 76.0%.

What work do people do in Smithfield?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

How active is the workforce in Smithfield?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

5.9% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.

These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Smithfield?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 4.2% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 33.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.

The median house price is $1,350,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,070,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
