## A buyer’s quick guide to South Perth
### Is South Perth expensive?
The median house price is $2,350,000, while units and apartments sit at $878,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is South Perth good for families?
The Census counted 3,283 local families, and 12.0% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in South Perth?
3-bedroom houses have a median around $1,760,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
