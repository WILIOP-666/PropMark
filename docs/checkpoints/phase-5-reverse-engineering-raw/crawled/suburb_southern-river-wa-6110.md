## Questions people ask about Southern River
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Southern River WA 6110.
Is Southern River family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
25.2% of Southern River's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
3,484 family households lived in Southern River; 63.6% were couple families and 11.0% were one-parent families.
51.7% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Southern River?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Southern River had 12,852 residents at the 2021 Census.
30.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
15.7% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Southern River?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 97.0% of occupied private dwellings in Southern River.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 2.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Southern River?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
20.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
63.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
16.9% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Southern River?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Southern River had 3.3 people.
One-person households made up 11.4% of occupied homes. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
Two-person households represented 23.3%, while 45.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Southern River?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
6.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
67.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
2.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Southern River?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
1.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
One-car households formed 22.1% of the total.
76.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Southern River, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
60.7% were at the same address five years earlier, while 39.3% reported a different address.
47.8% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Southern River?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
56.3% of residents used only English at home.
40.1% used a language other than English at home.
6.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.7% spoke Cantonese and 1.3% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Southern River residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
47.8% of Southern River's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 52.2% was born in Australia.
3.2% of residents were born in China.
7.6% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 80.9%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Southern River?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 25.2% of ancestry responses in Southern River.
Australian ancestry made up 20.7% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 12.9% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Southern River?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
23.2% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
Managers accounted for 11.5% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 13.3%.
Health care and social assistance employed 14.7% of workers, and education and training employed 8.5%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Southern River?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 72.9%, compared with 65.2% across Greater Perth.
42.8% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 23.1% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.1%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Perth.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Southern River show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
72.5% reported no selected long-term health condition.
5.3% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
5.6% reported asthma, 3.9% arthritis and 3.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Southern River?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
31.3% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 11.2% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 16.1%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 29.3%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Southern River?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,364 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,100.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $420 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Southern River compare with Greater Perth?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
Working from home was 6.9% in Southern River, compared with 7.6% across Greater Perth.
Outright ownership was 20.0% locally and 29.3% across Greater Perth. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Renting accounted for 16.9% in Southern River, against 27.5% across Greater Perth.
Capital-region comparisons use the same Census definitions and year.
