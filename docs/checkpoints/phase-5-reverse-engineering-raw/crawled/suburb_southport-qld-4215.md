Is Southport family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
11.4% of Southport's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
8,575 family households lived in Southport; 26.9% were couple families and 22.0% were one-parent families.
29.0% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Southport?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Southport had 36,786 residents at the 2021 Census.
31.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
29.8% were 55 or older. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Southport?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 23.8% of occupied private dwellings in Southport.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 20.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 54.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Southport?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
21.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
18.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
60.2% rented their home. That is about twice the Rest of Qld rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Southport?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 35.1% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 38.3%, while 11.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Southport?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
9.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
55.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
5.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 6.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Southport?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
12.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Rest of Qld rate.
One-car households formed 51.3% of the total.
36.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Southport, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

38.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Rest of Qld rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Southport?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
67.4% of residents used only English at home.
24.6% used a language other than English at home.
4.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Southport?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
