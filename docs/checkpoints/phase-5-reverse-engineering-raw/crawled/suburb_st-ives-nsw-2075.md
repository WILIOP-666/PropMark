Is St Ives family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Flats and apartments accounted for 26.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in St Ives?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in St Ives?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in St Ives?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

62.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
