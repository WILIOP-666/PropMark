Is St Marys family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

44.5% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of St Marys?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
St Marys had 13,256 residents at the 2021 Census.
31.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
23.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in St Marys?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 51.2% of occupied private dwellings in St Marys.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 30.0% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 18.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in St Marys?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
18.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
27.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
54.2% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in St Marys?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 28.5%, while 23.3% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in St Marys?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
20.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
49.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
3.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in St Marys?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
12.5% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
One-car households formed 46.2% of the total.
41.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to St Marys, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

33.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in St Marys?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

30.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.9% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 1.6% spoke Hindi.

Where were St Marys residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

What work do people do in St Marys?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is St Marys?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 7.2% of qualification responses.

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
