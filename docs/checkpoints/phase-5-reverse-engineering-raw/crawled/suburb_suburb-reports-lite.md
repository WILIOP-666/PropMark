# Free suburb reports for 901 Australian suburbs
A snapshot of what a suburb costs, what it rents for, how safe it is and who lives there — free to read, no account required. Every report links on to the suburbs around it, so you can follow a search across a city.
Need the full picture? [See the complete Microburbs suburb report](https://www.microburbs.com.au/suburb-reports) — every metric we hold, for any of the ~15,300 Australian localities.
## What is in a free report
  * Median house and unit prices, and how they have moved
  * Rental yields and what the suburb rents for
  * Safety, drawn from crime recorded below suburb level
  * Who lives there — age, income, households, ownership
  * The streets it covers, and the suburbs around it


The [full report](https://www.microburbs.com.au/suburb-reports) adds growth forecasts, development and planning, risk overlays, schools and transport, and comparisons against neighbouring suburbs. You can also search every suburb on numbers rather than names in the [Suburb Finder](https://www.microburbs.com.au/suburb-finder).
