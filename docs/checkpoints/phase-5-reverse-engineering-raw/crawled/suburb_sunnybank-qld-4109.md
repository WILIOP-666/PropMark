## Questions people ask about Sunnybank
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Sunnybank QLD 4109.
Is Sunnybank family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
15.0% of Sunnybank's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
2,160 family households lived in Sunnybank; 40.7% were couple families and 17.6% were one-parent families.
34.1% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Sunnybank?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Sunnybank had 8,892 residents at the 2021 Census.
34.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
26.0% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Brisbane.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Sunnybank?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 85.8% of occupied private dwellings in Sunnybank.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 13.1% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Sunnybank?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
36.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
22.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
41.2% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Sunnybank?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Sunnybank had 3.0 people.
One-person households made up 20.0% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Two-person households represented 29.1%, while 33.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Sunnybank?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
12.5% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
53.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
9.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Sunnybank?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
9.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
One-car households formed 35.6% of the total.
55.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Sunnybank, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
48.1% were at the same address five years earlier, while 51.9% reported a different address.
56.3% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about twice the Greater Brisbane rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Sunnybank?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
38.2% of residents used only English at home.
57.1% used a language other than English at home.
26.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 6.8% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Sunnybank residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
56.3% of Sunnybank's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 43.7% was born in Australia.
16.9% of residents were born in China.
3.6% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 64.0%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Sunnybank?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 18.0% of ancestry responses in Sunnybank.
Australian ancestry made up 13.0% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 35.0% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Sunnybank?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
20.7% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Managers accounted for 9.0% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.2%.
Health care and social assistance employed 16.0% of workers, and education and training employed 6.3%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Sunnybank?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 57.3%, compared with 64.5% across Greater Brisbane.
26.6% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 21.0% worked part time.
Unemployment was 7.6%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Brisbane.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Sunnybank show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
69.3% reported no selected long-term health condition.
5.6% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
5.7% reported asthma, 5.7% arthritis and 4.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Sunnybank?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
34.8% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 14.0% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 15.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 20.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Sunnybank?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $1,586 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,000.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $400 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Sunnybank compare with Greater Brisbane?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+

## A buyer’s quick guide to Sunnybank
### Is Sunnybank expensive?
The median house price is $1,550,000, while units and apartments sit at $874,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
### Is Sunnybank good for families?
The Census counted 2,160 local families, and 15.0% of residents were under 15. That describes the community; school quality, travel times and the right fit for a particular family need separate research.
### What can I get for $1.5m in Sunnybank?
3-bedroom houses have a median around $1,500,000, the closest bedroom benchmark we hold to a $1.5m budget. Individual homes can differ substantially with block size, renovation and location.
