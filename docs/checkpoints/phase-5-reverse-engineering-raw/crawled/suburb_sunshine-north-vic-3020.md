The median house price in Sunshine North, VIC is currently $780,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Sunshine North has a 12-month growth rate of 4.0% for houses and 9.0% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $645,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $764,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $850,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $930,000 in Sunshine North.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Sunshine North is $638,000, which has increased 9.0% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $300,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $607,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $722,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $810,000 in Sunshine North. The median rent for units and apartments in Sunshine North is $550 per week and has increased 4.8% over the last 12 months.

Is Sunshine North family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

2,976 family households lived in Sunshine North; 46.4% were couple families and 23.9% were one-parent families.
37.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Sunshine North?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Sunshine North had 12,047 residents at the 2021 Census.
29.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Sunshine North?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Sunshine North?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

29.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
30.8% rented their home. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Sunshine North?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

58.1% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
3.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Sunshine North?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 35.7% of the total.
55.2% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Sunshine North, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
