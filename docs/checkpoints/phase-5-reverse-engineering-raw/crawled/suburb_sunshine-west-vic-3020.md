The median house price in Sunshine West, VIC is currently $780,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Sunshine West has a 12-month growth rate of 6.8% for houses and 23.2% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $610,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $748,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $830,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $995,000 in Sunshine West.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Sunshine West is $525 per week. The median house rent has increased 10.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Sunshine West is $690,000, which has increased 23.2% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $490,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $700,000, Four-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $750,000 in Sunshine West. The median rent for units and apartments in Sunshine West is $495 per week and has decreased 1.0% over the last 12 months.

Is Sunshine West family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

35.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Sunshine West?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 14.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 3.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Sunshine West?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Sunshine West?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

3.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Sunshine West?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

54.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Sunshine West, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Sunshine West?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
