Is Surfers Paradise family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

5,891 family households lived in Surfers Paradise; 21.4% were couple families and 13.7% were one-parent families.
20.8% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Surfers Paradise?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Surfers Paradise had 26,412 residents at the 2021 Census.
34.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.7% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Surfers Paradise?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 81.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Surfers Paradise?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.

Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.

32.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
