The median house price in Swan Hill, VIC is currently $510,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Swan Hill has a 12-month growth rate of 4.1% for houses and 12.0% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $398,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $465,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $640,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $686,000 in Swan Hill.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Swan Hill is $525 per week. The median house rent has increased 5.0% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Swan Hill is $412,000, which has increased 12.0% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $405,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $645,000 in Swan Hill. The median rent for units and apartments in Swan Hill is $375 per week and has increased 15.4% over the last 12 months.
