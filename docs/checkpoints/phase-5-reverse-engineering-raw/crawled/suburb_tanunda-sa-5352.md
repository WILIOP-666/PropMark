Is Tanunda family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

Tanunda had 4,710 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Tanunda?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 8.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Tanunda?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

35.4% were buying their home with a mortgage.
21.2% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Rest of SA average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Tanunda?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

0.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Tanunda?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.

How educated is Tanunda?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

The median house price is $820,000, while units and apartments sit at $555,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
