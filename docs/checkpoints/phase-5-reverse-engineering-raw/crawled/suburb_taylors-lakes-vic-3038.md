The median house price in Taylors Lakes, VIC is currently $942,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Taylors Lakes has a 12-month growth rate of -2.3% for houses and 10.5% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $575,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $850,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,000,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,230,000 in Taylors Lakes.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Taylors Lakes is $612,000, which has increased 10.5% since the same time last year.
