## Questions people ask about Teneriffe
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Teneriffe QLD 4005.
Is Teneriffe family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
8.4% of Teneriffe's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
1,471 family households lived in Teneriffe; 24.5% were couple families and 8.5% were one-parent families.
22.2% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Teneriffe?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Teneriffe had 5,520 residents at the 2021 Census.
48.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
21.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Teneriffe?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 10.0% of occupied private dwellings in Teneriffe.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 1.5% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 88.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Teneriffe?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
18.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
29.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
52.9% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Teneriffe?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Teneriffe had 1.9 people.
One-person households made up 38.2% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Two-person households represented 44.2%, while 6.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Teneriffe?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
31.1% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
33.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
7.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 12.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Teneriffe?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
One-car households formed 60.5% of the total.
31.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Teneriffe, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
28.5% were at the same address five years earlier, while 71.5% reported a different address.
25.7% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Teneriffe?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
83.9% of residents used only English at home.
10.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.8% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Teneriffe residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
25.7% of Teneriffe's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 74.3% was born in Australia.
0.6% of residents were born in China.
0.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 84.0%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Teneriffe?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 40.8% of ancestry responses in Teneriffe.
Australian ancestry made up 26.8% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 2.7% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Teneriffe?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
46.0% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is about twice the Greater Brisbane rate.
Managers accounted for 21.9% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.7%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.4% of workers, and education and training employed 7.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Teneriffe?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 79.1%, compared with 64.5% across Greater Brisbane.
57.1% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 15.3% worked part time.
Unemployment was 2.5%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Brisbane.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Teneriffe show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
64.8% reported no selected long-term health condition.
9.7% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.1% reported asthma, 4.2% arthritis and 1.8% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Teneriffe?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
45.5% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 17.1% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.0%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 11.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Teneriffe?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,645 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,167.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $510 per week.

How does Teneriffe compare with Greater Brisbane?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+

The median house price is $4,200,000, while units and apartments sit at $1,160,000. Bedroom count, land and condition still create a wide range around those suburb medians.
