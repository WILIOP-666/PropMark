## Questions people ask about Tenterfield

Is Tenterfield family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
14.7% of Tenterfield's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
1,104 family households lived in Tenterfield; 25.4% were couple families and 16.5% were one-parent families.
28.3% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Tenterfield?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Tenterfield had 4,067 residents at the 2021 Census.
15.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
50.4% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Tenterfield?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 93.7% of occupied private dwellings in Tenterfield.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 1.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 3.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Tenterfield?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
54.8% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
22.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
23.2% rented their home. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Tenterfield?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Tenterfield?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
12.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
62.4% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
0.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 5.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Tenterfield?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
6.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 39.1% of the total.
53.8% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Tenterfield, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

0.1% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

Where were Tenterfield residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

11.6% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.0% reported asthma, 16.9% arthritis and 7.3% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Tenterfield?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

What do incomes and housing costs look like in Tenterfield?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
