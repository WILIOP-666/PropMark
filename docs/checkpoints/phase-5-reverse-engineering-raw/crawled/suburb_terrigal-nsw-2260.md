## Questions people ask about Terrigal

Is Terrigal family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
17.9% of Terrigal's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
3,601 family households lived in Terrigal; 43.0% were couple families and 14.4% were one-parent families.
35.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Terrigal?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Terrigal had 12,730 residents at the 2021 Census.
22.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.6% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Terrigal?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 71.0% of occupied private dwellings in Terrigal.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 7.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 21.5% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Terrigal?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
39.9% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
35.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
24.5% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Terrigal?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Terrigal had 2.6 people.
One-person households made up 22.3% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 35.7%, while 25.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Terrigal?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
33.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
44.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Terrigal?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
3.6% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 33.0% of the total.
63.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Terrigal, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
54.3% were at the same address five years earlier, while 45.7% reported a different address.
21.2% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Terrigal?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
87.6% of residents used only English at home.
7.7% used a language other than English at home.
0.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Terrigal residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
21.2% of Terrigal's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 78.8% was born in Australia.
0.7% of residents were born in China.
0.5% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 88.8%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Terrigal?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 18.0% of workers, and education and training employed 10.2%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Terrigal?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

7.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.5% reported asthma, 8.7% arthritis and 3.4% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Terrigal?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 8.8% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 17.9%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 28.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Terrigal?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
