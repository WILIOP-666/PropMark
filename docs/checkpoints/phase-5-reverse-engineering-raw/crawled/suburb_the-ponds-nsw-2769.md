Is The Ponds family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

4,373 family households lived in The Ponds; 72.7% were couple families and 8.1% were one-parent families.
62.7% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of The Ponds?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
The Ponds had 16,315 residents at the 2021 Census.
34.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
13.7% were 55 or older. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in The Ponds?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 83.7% of occupied private dwellings in The Ponds.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 15.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in The Ponds?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
12.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
64.7% were buying their home with a mortgage.
22.8% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in The Ponds?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 16.6%, while 55.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in The Ponds?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

28.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
2.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in The Ponds?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
1.7% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 30.4% of the total.
67.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to The Ponds, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is The Ponds?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
