## Questions people ask about Thornleigh
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Thornleigh NSW 2120.
Is Thornleigh family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
20.9% of Thornleigh's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
2,508 family households lived in Thornleigh; 55.7% were couple families and 14.1% were one-parent families.
43.1% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Thornleigh?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Thornleigh had 8,898 residents at the 2021 Census.
24.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
26.4% were 55 or older. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Thornleigh?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 76.5% of occupied private dwellings in Thornleigh.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 15.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 8.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Thornleigh?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
32.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
44.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
23.8% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Thornleigh?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Thornleigh had 2.9 people.
One-person households made up 16.2% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 28.1%, while 35.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Thornleigh?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
48.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
30.0% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
3.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.6% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Thornleigh?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.4% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 37.2% of the total.
57.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Thornleigh, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
63.8% were at the same address five years earlier, while 36.2% reported a different address.
36.5% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Thornleigh?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
66.0% of residents used only English at home.
31.9% used a language other than English at home.
7.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 3.5% spoke Cantonese and 1.6% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Thornleigh residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
36.5% of Thornleigh's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 63.5% was born in Australia.
6.4% of residents were born in China.
3.7% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 87.0%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Thornleigh?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 29.5% of ancestry responses in Thornleigh.
Australian ancestry made up 28.0% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 14.7% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Thornleigh?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
38.1% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Managers accounted for 17.5% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.9%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.0% of workers, and education and training employed 13.2%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Thornleigh?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 67.0%, compared with 60.0% across Greater Sydney.
39.4% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 19.2% worked part time.
Unemployment was 4.2%, compared with 5.1% across Greater Sydney.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Thornleigh show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
64.8% reported no selected long-term health condition.
7.8% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
7.9% reported asthma, 5.8% arthritis and 4.3% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Thornleigh?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
39.1% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 19.7% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.3%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 16.5%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Thornleigh?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
Median household income was $2,681 per week in the 2021 Census.
The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,800.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $530 per week.
These household figures are from 2021; current sale and asking-rent medians appear in the market section.
How does Thornleigh compare with Greater Sydney?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+

### Is Thornleigh good for families?

### What can I get for $1.5m in Thornleigh?
