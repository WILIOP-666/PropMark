The median house price in Thornlie, WA is currently $870,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Thornlie has a 12-month growth rate of 19.2% for houses and 14.6% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $558,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $828,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $910,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,060,000 in Thornlie.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Thornlie is $725 per week. The median house rent has increased 11.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Thornlie is $610,000, which has increased 14.6% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $518,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $688,000 in Thornlie. The median rent for units and apartments in Thornlie is $575 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.
