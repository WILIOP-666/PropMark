The median house price in Toorak, VIC is currently $4,550,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Toorak has a 12-month growth rate of 12.3% for houses and 5.5% for units.
Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $3,650,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $2,700,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $5,250,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $7,300,000 in Toorak.

The median price of apartments and units for sale in Toorak is $868,000, which has increased 5.5% since the same time last year. ## Questions people ask about Toorak
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Toorak VIC 3142.
Is Toorak family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
10.9% of Toorak's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
3,308 family households lived in Toorak; 34.5% were couple families and 11.3% were one-parent families.
24.3% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Toorak?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Toorak had 12,817 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
40.3% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Toorak?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 30.3% of occupied private dwellings in Toorak.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 19.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 49.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Toorak?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
44.2% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
23.5% were buying their home with a mortgage.
32.3% rented their home. That is close to the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Toorak?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Toorak had 2.1 people.
One-person households made up 35.2% of occupied homes. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Two-person households represented 37.5%, while 15.2% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Toorak?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
43.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
33.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
4.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Toorak?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Melbourne.
One-car households formed 43.9% of the total.
47.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Toorak, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
54.1% were at the same address five years earlier, while 45.9% reported a different address.
30.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Melbourne average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Toorak?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
74.0% of residents used only English at home.
20.0% used a language other than English at home.
6.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.6% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Toorak residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
30.9% of Toorak's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 69.1% was born in Australia.
5.0% of residents were born in China.
1.9% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 83.1%.
Birthplace, citizenship and ancestry describe different parts of identity.
What ancestries shape Toorak?Ancestry responses show how residents describe family and cultural background.+
English ancestry accounted for 32.0% of ancestry responses in Toorak.
Australian ancestry made up 22.2% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 11.1% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Toorak?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
40.5% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Managers accounted for 24.1% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 11.4%.
Health care and social assistance employed 15.1% of workers, and education and training employed 7.3%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Toorak?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+
The labour-force participation rate was 59.5%, compared with 64.1% across Greater Melbourne.
35.4% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 18.6% worked part time.
Unemployment was 3.7%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Melbourne.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Toorak show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
64.0% reported no selected long-term health condition.
6.3% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.2% reported asthma, 7.0% arthritis and 2.5% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Toorak?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
44.9% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 18.3% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 11.8%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 8.1%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Toorak?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $3,152.
Occupied renting households reported a median rent of $486 per week.

How does Toorak compare with Greater Melbourne?A few clear differences place the suburb in its wider metropolitan context.+
