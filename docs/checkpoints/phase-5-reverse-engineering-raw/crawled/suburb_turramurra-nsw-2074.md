Is Turramurra family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
19.1% of Turramurra's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
3,682 family households lived in Turramurra; 54.8% were couple families and 11.1% were one-parent families.
39.4% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Turramurra?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Turramurra had 12,850 residents at the 2021 Census.
21.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.1% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Turramurra?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 68.1% of occupied private dwellings in Turramurra.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 27.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Turramurra?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
40.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
39.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
19.8% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Turramurra?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Turramurra had 2.8 people.
One-person households made up 17.9% of occupied homes. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 30.6%, while 32.7% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Turramurra?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
56.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
24.3% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
2.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Turramurra?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
5.3% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Sydney rate.
One-car households formed 37.8% of the total.
56.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Turramurra, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

39.4% of residents were born outside Australia. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Turramurra?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

29.3% used a language other than English at home.
10.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 4.0% spoke Cantonese and 1.1% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Turramurra residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
39.4% of Turramurra's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 60.6% was born in Australia.
8.4% of residents were born in China.

What work do people do in Turramurra?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 14.0% of workers, and education and training employed 10.6%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Turramurra?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Turramurra show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+

5.4% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
6.6% reported asthma, 6.6% arthritis and 3.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Turramurra?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 23.2% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 9.7%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Turramurra?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
