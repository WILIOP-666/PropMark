## Questions people ask about Two Wells

Is Two Wells family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.2% of Two Wells's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
920 family households lived in Two Wells; 47.5% were couple families and 13.2% were one-parent families.
40.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat above the Greater Adelaide average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Two Wells?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Two Wells had 3,233 residents at the 2021 Census.
29.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
24.2% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Two Wells?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 99.2% of occupied private dwellings in Two Wells.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 0.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Two Wells?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
22.7% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
65.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.
11.4% rented their home. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Two Wells?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Two Wells had 2.8 people.
One-person households made up 15.6% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Adelaide average.
Two-person households represented 34.5%, while 30.6% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Two Wells?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
5.7% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is somewhat below the Greater Adelaide average.
74.6% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Adelaide average.
0.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Two Wells?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
1.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
One-car households formed 16.8% of the total.
80.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Two Wells, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
58.6% were at the same address five years earlier, while 41.4% reported a different address.
10.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Two Wells?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

6.2% used a language other than English at home.
0.0% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.0% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Two Wells residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
10.9% of Two Wells's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 89.1% was born in Australia.
0.0% of residents were born in China.
0.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 91.0%.

Australian ancestry made up 45.5% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 0.2% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Two Wells?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+
10.3% of employed residents worked in professional occupations. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
Managers accounted for 11.7% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 14.0%.
Health care and social assistance employed 12.0% of workers, and education and training employed 6.2%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Two Wells?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

40.1% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 18.5% worked part time.
Unemployment was 3.9%, compared with 5.5% across Greater Adelaide.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Two Wells show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
57.7% reported no selected long-term health condition.
10.1% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
12.0% reported asthma, 10.4% arthritis and 5.4% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Two Wells?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
9.9% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is about half the Greater Adelaide rate.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 2.6% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.3%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 56.2%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Two Wells?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $1,700.
