Is Wahroonga family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.7% of Wahroonga's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
4,885 family households lived in Wahroonga; 56.0% were couple families and 10.7% were one-parent families.
40.2% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Wahroonga?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Wahroonga had 17,853 residents at the 2021 Census.
19.3% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
34.0% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Wahroonga?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 74.4% of occupied private dwellings in Wahroonga.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 5.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 19.6% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Wahroonga?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
40.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
41.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Wahroonga?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 29.4%, while 35.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Wahroonga?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

2.4% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Wahroonga?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

61.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Wahroonga, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
