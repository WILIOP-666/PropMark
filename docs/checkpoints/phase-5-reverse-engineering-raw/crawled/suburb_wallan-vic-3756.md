Is Wallan family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

47.4% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Melbourne average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Wallan?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Wallan had 15,004 residents at the 2021 Census.
32.2% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Wallan?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 7.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 0.1% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Wallan?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
19.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
59.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
21.8% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Melbourne average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Wallan?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Wallan?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Wallan?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.

How educated is Wallan?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
