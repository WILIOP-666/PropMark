## Questions people ask about Wallsend
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Wallsend NSW 2287.
Is Wallsend family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
15.6% of Wallsend's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
3,397 family households lived in Wallsend; 37.2% were couple families and 21.0% were one-parent families.
35.0% of local families included children under 15. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of NSW.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Wallsend?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Wallsend had 13,244 residents at the 2021 Census.
27.7% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.5% were 55 or older. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Wallsend?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 77.7% of occupied private dwellings in Wallsend.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 16.9% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.8% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Wallsend?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
30.5% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Rest of NSW average.
31.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
38.2% rented their home. That is well above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Wallsend?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Wallsend had 2.3 people.
One-person households made up 31.1% of occupied homes. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Two-person households represented 33.1%, while 18.4% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Wallsend?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
17.6% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
56.2% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat below the Rest of NSW average.
1.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.9% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Wallsend?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
8.9% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
One-car households formed 39.5% of the total.
51.7% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Wallsend, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

15.0% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Wallsend?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
83.1% of residents used only English at home.
12.0% used a language other than English at home.
1.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.2% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Wallsend residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
15.0% of Wallsend's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 85.0% was born in Australia.
1.1% of residents were born in China.
1.0% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 88.1%.

Australian ancestry made up 39.1% of responses.
Chinese ancestry represented 2.3% of responses.
People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Wallsend?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Managers accounted for 8.9% of employed residents, while clerical and administrative workers made up 12.4%.
Health care and social assistance employed 21.4% of workers, and education and training employed 9.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Wallsend?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Wallsend show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
50.6% reported no selected long-term health condition.
14.7% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
10.7% reported asthma, 12.2% arthritis and 6.7% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Wallsend?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Postgraduate degrees accounted for 7.4% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 13.5%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 39.9%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Wallsend?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+
