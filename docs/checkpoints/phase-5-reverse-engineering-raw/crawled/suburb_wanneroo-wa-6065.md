The median house price in Wanneroo, WA is currently $935,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Wanneroo has a 12-month growth rate of 19.4% for houses and 25.9% for units.
Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $861,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $1,080,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $1,060,000 in Wanneroo.
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Wanneroo is $725 per week. The median house rent has increased 11.5% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Wanneroo is $730,000, which has increased 25.9% since the same time last year. One-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $560,000, Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $670,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $796,000 in Wanneroo. The median rent for units and apartments in Wanneroo is $675 per week and has remained unchanged over the last 12 months.
