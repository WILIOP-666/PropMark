The median house price in Warwick (Qld), QLD is currently $656,000, based on the latest 12 months of sales. Warwick (Qld) has a 12-month growth rate of 22.6% for houses and 29.3% for units.
One-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $502,000, Two-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $530,000, Three-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $620,000, Four-bedroom houses have a median sale price of $765,000, Larger houses have a median sale price of $872,000 in Warwick (Qld).
If you're looking to lease, the median rent for houses in Warwick (Qld) is $550 per week. The median house rent has increased 4.8% over the last 12 months.
The median price of apartments and units for sale in Warwick (Qld) is $485,000, which has increased 29.3% since the same time last year. Two-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $460,000, Three-bedroom apartments and units have a median sale price of $488,000 in Warwick (Qld). The median rent for units and apartments in Warwick (Qld) is $400 per week and has increased 6.7% over the last 12 months.

## Questions people ask about Warwick

Is Warwick family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.3% of Warwick's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is almost exactly typical for Rest of Qld.
3,204 family households lived in Warwick; 30.8% were couple families and 22.3% were one-parent families.
34.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of Qld average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Warwick?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Warwick had 12,294 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.1% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
39.2% were 55 or older. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Warwick?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 84.7% of occupied private dwellings in Warwick.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 12.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 2.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Warwick?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
37.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
26.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
36.8% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Warwick?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Warwick had 2.2 people.
One-person households made up 34.7% of occupied homes. That is well above the Rest of Qld average.
Two-person households represented 36.2%, while 17.1% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Warwick?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
5.0% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
68.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
0.1% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.5% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Warwick?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
7.8% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is somewhat above the Rest of Qld average.
One-car households formed 42.8% of the total.
49.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Warwick, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

8.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is about half the Rest of Qld rate.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

3.9% used a language other than English at home.
0.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.1% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Warwick?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
