## Questions people ask about Wellington Point

Is Wellington Point family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
18.2% of Wellington Point's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
3,735 family households lived in Wellington Point; 47.3% were couple families and 14.3% were one-parent families.
35.9% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Wellington Point?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Wellington Point had 12,661 residents at the 2021 Census.
20.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
32.6% were 55 or older. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Wellington Point?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 89.0% of occupied private dwellings in Wellington Point.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 6.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 4.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Wellington Point?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
36.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
43.8% were buying their home with a mortgage.
20.2% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Wellington Point?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Wellington Point?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

2.8% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Wellington Point?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

69.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Wellington Point, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Wellington Point?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
