## Questions people ask about Werrington

Is Werrington family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
23.0% of Werrington's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Sydney average.
1,373 family households lived in Werrington; 45.1% were couple families and 24.7% were one-parent families.
49.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Werrington?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Werrington had 5,328 residents at the 2021 Census.
34.4% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
17.9% were 55 or older. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Werrington?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 43.1% of occupied private dwellings in Werrington.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 37.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 19.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Werrington?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
16.1% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
37.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
46.8% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Werrington?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

One-person households made up 25.3% of occupied homes. That is close to the Greater Sydney average.
Two-person households represented 29.0%, while 26.0% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Werrington?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
22.9% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is well below the Greater Sydney average.
46.9% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Sydney average.
6.6% used only a train, tram or bus, while 0.4% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Werrington?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
11.1% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Sydney.
One-car households formed 45.4% of the total.
43.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Werrington, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

33.9% of residents were born outside Australia. That is somewhat below the Greater Sydney average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Werrington?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

30.8% used a language other than English at home.
1.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.5% spoke Cantonese and 1.7% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Werrington residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

1.1% of residents were born in China.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Werrington?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 17.2% of workers, and education and training employed 6.5%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Werrington?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.

How educated is Werrington?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
