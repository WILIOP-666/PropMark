## Questions people ask about Willetton
Open any question for a plain-English reading of the public 2021 Census for Willetton WA 6155.
Is Willetton family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
21.9% of Willetton's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Perth average.
5,328 family households lived in Willetton; 59.7% were couple families and 12.4% were one-parent families.
45.7% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Willetton?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Willetton had 19,262 residents at the 2021 Census.
22.6% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
26.2% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Willetton?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 90.0% of occupied private dwellings in Willetton.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 4.3% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 5.7% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Willetton?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
40.0% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
40.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.
20.1% rented their home. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Willetton?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+
The average household in Willetton had 3.1 people.
One-person households made up 14.3% of occupied homes. That is well below the Greater Perth average.
Two-person households represented 25.3%, while 41.8% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Willetton?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
7.8% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is almost exactly typical for Greater Perth.
62.8% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
3.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.7% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Willetton?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
2.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about half the Greater Perth rate.
One-car households formed 27.9% of the total.
70.0% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Willetton, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
62.0% were at the same address five years earlier, while 38.0% reported a different address.
52.1% of residents were born outside Australia. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Willetton?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+
52.8% of residents used only English at home.
44.8% used a language other than English at home.
14.2% spoke Mandarin at home, 4.8% spoke Cantonese and 1.8% spoke Hindi.
Home language is separate from English proficiency.
Where were Willetton residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+
52.1% of Willetton's population was born outside Australia, while roughly 47.9% was born in Australia.
7.1% of residents were born in China.
8.3% were born in India, while Australian citizenship across the whole suburb stood at 79.9%.

People may nominate more than one ancestry, so these are shares of responses.
What work do people do in Willetton?Occupation describes a person’s role; industry describes the sector employing them.+

Health care and social assistance employed 15.9% of workers, and education and training employed 11.1%.
Occupation and industry figures describe employed residents of the suburb.
How active is the workforce in Willetton?Participation, employment pattern and unemployment describe the resident labour force.+

35.2% of residents aged 15 and over worked full time, while 23.3% worked part time.
Unemployment was 5.9%, compared with 5.3% across Greater Perth.
Labour-force figures come from the 2021 Census.
What does the health profile of Willetton show?Selected long-term health conditions help describe community service needs.+
69.8% reported no selected long-term health condition.
5.0% reported a long-term mental health condition, including depression or anxiety.
5.4% reported asthma, 6.0% arthritis and 4.6% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.
These are self-reported suburb-wide Census figures, not address-level health risks.
How educated is Willetton?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
38.5% of people in the qualifications table held a bachelor degree. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Postgraduate degrees accounted for 16.1% of qualification responses.
Diploma-level qualifications represented 14.2%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 19.8%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
What do incomes and housing costs look like in Willetton?Census income and household payments give historical context for local living costs.+

The median monthly mortgage repayment reported by local households was $2,100.
