Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Winston Hills?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Flats and apartments accounted for 7.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Winston Hills?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

45.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Winston Hills?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

1.5% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.0% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Winston Hills?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 31.3% of the total.
63.6% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Winston Hills, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Winston Hills?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
