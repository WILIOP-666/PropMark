Is Woodridge family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+
23.0% of Woodridge's residents were children under 15 in the 2021 Census. That is somewhat above the Greater Brisbane average.
2,798 family households lived in Woodridge; 37.9% were couple families and 33.9% were one-parent families.
47.6% of local families included children under 15. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Woodridge?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Woodridge had 12,982 residents at the 2021 Census.
27.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
23.8% were 55 or older. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Woodridge?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 60.2% of occupied private dwellings in Woodridge.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 38.6% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 1.2% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Woodridge?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
18.4% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
19.3% were buying their home with a mortgage.
62.3% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Woodridge?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Woodridge?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+
5.4% of workers worked from home on Census day. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
66.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well above the Greater Brisbane average.
3.7% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.8% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Woodridge?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
14.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 44.9% of the total.
40.9% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Woodridge, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

33.4% used a language other than English at home.
0.7% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.5% spoke Cantonese and 0.7% spoke Hindi.

Where were Woodridge residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

9.4% reported asthma, 8.1% arthritis and 6.9% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Woodridge?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Diploma-level qualifications represented 12.4%, while certificate-level qualifications represented 38.6%.
Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
