Is Woolgoolga family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

1,615 family households lived in Woolgoolga; 36.2% were couple families and 17.2% were one-parent families.
32.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Rest of NSW average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Woolgoolga?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Woolgoolga had 6,151 residents at the 2021 Census.
25.0% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
40.0% were 55 or older. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Woolgoolga?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 13.2% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 3.4% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Woolgoolga?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

23.6% were buying their home with a mortgage.
30.5% rented their home. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Woolgoolga?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Woolgoolga?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

65.5% drove a car as their only travel method. That is somewhat above the Rest of NSW average.
0.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 4.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Woolgoolga?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 41.1% of the total.
53.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Woolgoolga, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.

22.4% used a language other than English at home.
1.3% spoke Mandarin at home, 0.2% spoke Cantonese and 0.1% spoke Hindi.

How educated is Woolgoolga?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+

Qualification categories are reported separately and are not school ratings.
