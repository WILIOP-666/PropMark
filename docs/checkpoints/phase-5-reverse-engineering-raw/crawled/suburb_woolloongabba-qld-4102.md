Is Woolloongabba family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

1,670 family households lived in Woolloongabba; 27.4% were couple families and 11.9% were one-parent families.
25.3% of local families included children under 15. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Woolloongabba?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Woolloongabba had 8,687 residents at the 2021 Census.
44.9% of residents were aged 25 to 44.
14.0% were 55 or older. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Woolloongabba?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+
Separate houses made up 29.8% of occupied private dwellings in Woolloongabba.
Townhouses and other attached homes represented 0.7% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 69.3% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Woolloongabba?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+
10.3% of households with a stated tenure owned their home outright. That is about half the Greater Brisbane rate.
17.2% were buying their home with a mortgage.
72.5% rented their home. That is about twice the Greater Brisbane rate.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Woolloongabba?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Two-person households represented 41.6%, while 12.5% had four or more usual residents.
Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Woolloongabba?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

36.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is well below the Greater Brisbane average.
13.3% used only a train, tram or bus, while 11.1% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Woolloongabba?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+
16.2% of occupied households had no motor vehicle available. That is about twice the Greater Brisbane rate.
One-car households formed 51.8% of the total.
32.1% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Woolloongabba, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

Mobility figures cover residents who answered the relevant Census question.
What languages are spoken in Woolloongabba?Language at home gives a direct view of the community’s everyday linguistic mix.+

32.3% used a language other than English at home.
8.5% spoke Mandarin at home, 2.1% spoke Cantonese and 1.1% spoke Hindi.

Where were Woolloongabba residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

8.1% reported asthma, 4.3% arthritis and 2.2% diabetes excluding gestational diabetes.

How educated is Woolloongabba?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
