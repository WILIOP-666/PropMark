Is Wynnum family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

37.6% of local families included children under 15. That is close to the Greater Brisbane average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Wynnum?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Wynnum had 14,036 residents at the 2021 Census.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Wynnum?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Wynnum?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

36.0% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Wynnum?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

5.0% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Wynnum?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

54.5% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Wynnum, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

How educated is Wynnum?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
