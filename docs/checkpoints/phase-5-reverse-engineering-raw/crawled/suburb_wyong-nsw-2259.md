What is the age profile of Wyong?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+

22.8% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.

Flats and apartments accounted for 11.0% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Wyong?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

22.9% were buying their home with a mortgage.

Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.

How do people get to work in Wyong?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

3.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 2.2% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Wyong?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

42.4% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Wyong, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+
