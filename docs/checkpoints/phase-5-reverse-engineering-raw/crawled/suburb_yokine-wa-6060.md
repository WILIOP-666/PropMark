Is Yokine family-friendly?Age, family structure and household size describe the community families would be joining.+

3,372 family households lived in Yokine; 38.2% were couple families and 14.7% were one-parent families.
34.4% of local families included children under 15. That is somewhat below the Greater Perth average.
These are suburb-wide household figures, not school or childcare ratings.
What is the age profile of Yokine?The balance between younger adults, families and older residents changes how a suburb feels day to day.+
Yokine had 12,706 residents at the 2021 Census.
35.5% of residents were aged 25 to 44.

Age shares use the suburb’s total Census population.
What kind of homes are in Yokine?The dwelling mix shows whether the suburb is mostly detached homes, townhouses or apartments.+

Townhouses and other attached homes represented 39.4% of occupied private dwellings.
Flats and apartments accounted for 19.9% of occupied private dwellings.
All three shares use occupied private dwellings as the denominator.
Do people own or rent in Yokine?Tenure shows how many households own outright, have a mortgage or rent.+

32.1% were buying their home with a mortgage.
39.8% rented their home. That is well above the Greater Perth average.
Tenure shares include households that stated one of these three arrangements.
How large are households in Yokine?Household-size data shows the mix behind the suburb-wide average.+

Household size counts usual residents in occupied homes.
How do people get to work in Yokine?Census-day travel shows the balance between home, road, public transport and active travel.+

63.7% drove a car as their only travel method. That is close to the Greater Perth average.
8.2% used only a train, tram or bus, while 1.3% walked or cycled.
Travel figures are a 2021 Census-day snapshot.
How many cars do households have in Yokine?Vehicle availability helps describe the routines local households can support.+

One-car households formed 45.8% of the total.
47.3% of households had two or more vehicles.
Vehicle counts refer to occupied households, not individual residents.
Who's moving to Yokine, and who stays?Address history shows how settled or recently mobile residents were at the Census date.+

2.4% spoke Mandarin at home, 1.3% spoke Cantonese and 0.7% spoke Hindi.

Where were Yokine residents born?Birthplace adds another layer to the local story, separate from ancestry and language.+

How educated is Yokine?Qualifications describe the study pathways represented among local residents.+
